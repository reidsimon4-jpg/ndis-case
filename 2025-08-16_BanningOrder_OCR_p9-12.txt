Decision to issue a banning order

The conduct set out above, engaged in by S REID and Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd, an entity for which S REID
has authority or responsibility for (or significant influence over) planning, directing or controlling its
activities, strongly suggests that The Hive Pty Ltd is not suitable to provide supports or services to
people with disability for the following reasons:

Â¢ Section 3 of the NDIS Act contains the objects of the NDIS Act, which includes:

\n\n===== page-11.png =====\n
supporting the independence and social and economic participation of people with
disability;
enabling people with disability to exercise choice and control in the pursuit of their

goals and the planning and delivery of their supports;

promoting the provision of high quality and innovative supports that enable people
with disability to maximise independent lifestyles and full inclusion in the
community; and

protecting and prevent people with disability from experiencing harm arising from
poor quality or unsafe supports or services provided under the NDIS.

In giving effect to the objects of the NDIS Act:

Â°

section 3(3)(b) of the NDIS Act requires regard to be had to the need to ensure the
financial sustainability of the NDIS. Similarly, as set out in section 4(17) of the NDIS
Act, it is the intention of Parliament that the Commissioner, amongst others, have
regard to the need to ensure the financial sustainability of the NDIS in performing
functions and exercising powers under the NDIS Act;

sections 4(4) and 4(6) of the NDIS Act, provide principles guiding actions under the
NDIS Act including:

= People with disability should be supported to exercise choice, including in
relation to taking reasonable risks, in the pursuit of their goals and the
planning and delivery of their supports; and

= People with disability have the same right as other members of Australian
society to respect for their worth and dignity and to live free from abuse,
neglect and exploitation.

It is my view based on the conduct set out above that S REID and Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd, an entity
for which S REID has responsibility and authority, have acted in a manner that is contrary to
these guiding principles, as well as the objects of the NDIS Act, and in circumstances have
deliberately undermined those principles. More specifically:

â€”â€”

Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd engaged in conduct which limited NDIS participants from
exercising their choice and control over their own supports and services, and acted
without their consent or authorisation to make decisions about the engagement of
supports and services under their NDIS plans. This conduct is serious, and has had
significant impacts on the health and wellbeing of participants, particularly where
deliberate conduct has been engaged in to subvert the authority of NDIS participants
and their nominees;

Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd has engaged in aggressive and dishonest conduct, including the
submission of falsified claims for supports and services against NDIS plans, driving a
culture of inflating claims and hours for services delivered, authorising payments
without participant consent and entering into new service agreements without the
knowledge or consent of NDIS participants and their nominees; and

The conduct set out above and the offences for which V REID has been charged and S
REID involved, presents significant risks to the financial sustainability of the NDIS, as

\n\n===== page-12.png =====\n
well as to NDIS participants, as victims of fraud. Such conduct takes funds from NDIS
participant plans, stopping participants from being able to purchase the supports and
service they genuinely need. Further, fraud makes it harder for participants to pursue
their goals and outs additional pressures on carers, family, friends and support
networks.

Therefore, | have formed the view that allowing The Hive Pty Ltd to provide supports or services to
people with disability poses a risk to people with disability. My view is that is that the need to protect
the interests of people with disability, who may be particularly vulnerable, and ensure that they are
kept safe, requires that in matters of this kind, a provider whose key personnel has engaged and been
involved in conduct set out above, must be prevented from delivering supports and services to people
with disability. My view is that The Hive Pty Ltd is not currently suitable to provide supports or services
to people with disability.

Taking into account the matters set out above, | consider that it is proportionate to impose a
banning order on The Hive Pty Ltd for a period of five (5) years with effect from 5:00pm (AWST) on
21 December 2022.
