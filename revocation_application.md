## 1. Executive Summary

The Applicants seek the **immediate revocation** of all banning orders currently in force or recorded against them.

The grounds for this application are unassailable:
1.  **No Evidence of Charges:** The Commission has formally admitted in proceedings before the Administrative Review Tribunal (ART) that *"There is no evidence of any criminal charges at this stage"* (Respondent's Submissions, 17 Sep 2025, Para 47).
2.  **Rationale Extinguished:** The banning orders against Simon Reid and Vida Reid were explicitly "pegged" to the duration of criminal proceedings. Those proceedings have concluded with **all charges dismissed**. The stated rationale for the bans has therefore ceased to exist.
3.  **Procedural Fairness:** The ban against **The Hive Pty Ltd** is void *ab initio* as the show cause notice was delivered *after* the ban took effect.
4.  **Malice & Misfeasance:** There is credible evidence of witness tampering and coercion by Commission investigators (Jan 2025), rendering the enforcement action an abuse of process.

---

## 2. Primary Ground: The "No Evidence" Admission

**2.1 The Commission's Admission**
On 17 September 2025, in *Reid and Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission*, the Respondent stated:
> *"The Respondent's previous position was based on the fact that there were criminal charges laid... **There is no evidence of any criminal charges at this stage.**"* (Para 47)

**2.2 The Effect on the Banning Orders**
The Internal Review decisions for both Simon Reid (Para 47) and Vida Reid (Para 63) tied the **duration** of the bans directly to the criminal proceedings.
*   **Simon Reid Review:** *"The period of the banning order corresponds with the amount of time it is reasonably expected to take for the current regulatory and legal processes... to conclude."*
*   **Vida Reid Review:** *"The period of the banning order corresponds with the amount of time it is reasonably expected to take for the criminal proceedings to be finally determined..."*

**Conclusion:** The proceedings are determined. The charges are dismissed. The Commission admits there is "no evidence" of charges. **The bans must be revoked immediately.** To maintain them now is to punish the Applicants for crimes they were never convicted of, based on evidence the prosecution abandoned.

## 3. Secondary Grounds

### 3.1 The FFG Ban Expiry
The Commission has notified Simon Reid (Letter, 13 Feb 2026) that the FFG banning order expired on 20 December 2025.
*   **Submission:** While the ban has expired, it remains on the register "indefinitely". This causes ongoing reputational harm.
*   **Revocation Required:** The FFG ban should be **revoked** (removed) rather than merely noted as expired, because it was based on the same flawed "criminal charges" logic that has now collapsed.

### 3.2 Witness Tampering & Malice
The Applicants have evidence that on 7 January 2025, NDIS Investigator John Maloney:
*   Raided the home of former employee Virginie Fanny-Dulloo.
*   Threatened her with imprisonment (*"It's your neck or her neck"*) to coerce a statement against Vida Reid.
*   Admitted to unlawful surveillance of the Applicants' calls and movements.
**Submission:** This conduct demonstrates that the Commission's actions are driven by malice and an improper purpose, invalidating the administrative decision-making process.

### 3.3 The Hive Pty Ltd – Void Order
*   **Fact:** The show cause notice for The Hive was delivered by Australia Post on **22 December 2022**.
*   **Fact:** The banning order was made on **21 December 2022**.
*   **Result:** The Hive was banned *before* it received notice. This is a fundamental breach of procedural fairness and s 73ZN(7) of the NDIS Act. The order is void and must be revoked.

## 4. Orders Sought

1.  **Revoke** the banning orders against Simon Reid, Vida Reid, The Hive Pty Ltd, and Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd pursuant to s 73ZO(1) of the *National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013*.
2.  **Remove** the entry for Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd (FFG) from the NDIS Banning Orders Register, acknowledging that the ban was made in error or is no longer appropriate.
3.  **Confirm** in writing that no further enforcement action will be taken based on the withdrawn charges.
