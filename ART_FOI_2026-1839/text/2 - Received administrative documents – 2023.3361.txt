addition to documents that the respondent considers relevant to the first applicant
and one or both of the other applicants. The second and third applicants’ Tribunal
documents contain documents relevant to that applicant alone.

Section 35 application and proposed orders/ Request for direction limiting
public access

Please find enclosed a proposed order that the respondent seeks from the Tribunal
in relation to, separately provided, documents.

has provided the Tribunal documents in a form that, it is submitted, is appropriate for

   making a banning order stating, ‘The evidence supporting the proposed banning of Mrs Reid
   and the regulatory actions against FFG was similar or overlapped considerably.’

   submission by 16 November 2022 on the proposed revocation of registration and a banning
   order.

   relied on for the proposed revocation of registration and banning order.

5. On 15 November 2022, the NDIS Commission declined to provide disclosure on the basis that
   it was satisfied the evidence supporting the various regulatory actions against Ms Reid had
   been adequately disclosed in the notices dated 19 October 2022.

   opportunity to make submissions about a proposed banning order.

   Vida Reid and Mr Simon Reid (collectively known as ‘the Applicants’).
