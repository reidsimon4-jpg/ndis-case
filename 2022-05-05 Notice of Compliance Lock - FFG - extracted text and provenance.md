## Key features (analysis)

1. **No statutory provision is cited anywhere.** The only legal reference in the document is a warning that providing false information to the Commonwealth is an offence, plus a link to a compliance-monitoring webpage. No section of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013, no rule, no instrument.
2. **No named decision-maker.** Signed "NDIA Payment Review Team — Delegate of the Chief Executive Officer." A delegation is held by a person or office-holder, not a team; the identity of the actual delegate (if any) is unknown.
3. **The lock was applied 3 May 2022, notified 5 May 2022** — two dates, plead both, do not conflate.
4. **The documented measure is manual assessment only**: "all future payments will be manually assessed prior to being paid... this does not prevent you from submitting payment requests." It applies to **Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd only** — The Hive is not mentioned.
5. **The escalation gap (the load-bearing point):** the documented measure (manual assessment of FFG's automated payments) is *not* what was later executed. By June–July 2022 the practical position was non-payment (the "URGENT: Non-Payment" chain re participant Gregory Clark); by 4 October 2022 a plan manager recorded an "instruction from NDIA" that "effective immediately, we are not able to pay ALL FFG **and The Hive** invoices" — a total stop, extended to an entity never given any notice, executed through third parties. On 6 October 2022 the agency officer Lundgren wrote that "the Agency has not directed Assist Plan Managers to cease all payments." **Documented measure ≠ executed measure ≠ official denial.** Whatever power supported manual assessment, nothing documented supports the October total stop.
6. **Intelligence was copied from day one.** The covering email cc'd NDIA.INTELLIGENCE@ndis.gov.au — the "financial sustainability" framing of the notice sat alongside an intelligence interest from the outset.
7. The four allegations in the notice are all claiming-accuracy issues (transport claims not aligning with plans; 398 hours cleaning over two weeks; 110 hours from an expired plan; 200 hours incorrect units 1–12 August 2021). These are the original case; the contemporaneous response chain (May–July 2022) records correction, cancellation of the disputed payment and repayment offers.
8. The agency's 19 July 2022 email "Compliance Lock questions" (NDIA.PAYMENT.REVIEW) explains the lock as "a result of a review of the overall claiming of Forward Focused P/L" — and likewise cites **no statutory provision** (text-searched 2 July 2026: no "section", "Act", "pursuant to", "delegate" or "authority" hits).

**Notice of Compliance Lock — Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd**

Dear Vida,

The National Disability Insurance Agency (Agency) conducts reviews of provider claims to ensure the NDIS and participant plans remain financially sustainable.

A review has been conducted on the claims lodged by Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd which identified several inaccurate payment requests:

1. Payment requests not aligning with participant plans (e.g., claims for Specialised Transport to School/Education Facility/Employment/Community)
2. Payment requests exceeding what would be deemed reasonable and necessary (e.g., 398 hours claimed for House Cleaning and Other Household Activities for two weeks).
3. Inappropriate claiming from expired plans (e.g., 110 hours claimed for House And/or Yard Maintenance from expired plan)
4. Incorrect number of units claimed (e.g., 200 hours claimed for House And/or Yard Maintenance between 1 August 2021 and 12 August 202[1]).

As a result of the payment review, the Agency has applied a compliance lock on 3 May 2022 to all future automated payments to Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd.

This means all future payments will be manually assessed prior to being paid. However, please note this does not prevent you from submitting payment requests.

To receive further payments, you will need to provide the following supporting documentation accompanying payment request submissions:

- Copies of invoices
- Service agreements
- Timesheets (where applicable, e.g., for all payment requests for supported independent living)
- Contact details of support workers, including names, date of birth, address, and phone numbers (where appropriate, e.g., for personnel who provide 24/7 supports)

Please continue to lodge payment requests via the Provider Portal, and submit supporting documentation to: NDIA.PAYMENT.REVIEW@ndis.gov.au

For further information relating to accurate claiming please refer to: https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/working-provider/provider-compliance-monitoring

Please note that providing false information [to] the Commonwealth is a serious offence.

Kind Regards,

**NDIA Payment Review Team**
**Delegate of the Chief Executive Officer**
National Disability Insurance Agency
