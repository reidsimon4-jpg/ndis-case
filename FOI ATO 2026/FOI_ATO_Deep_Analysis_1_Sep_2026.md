## 3. The “same week as the banning” question — settled

1. **The stream started 28 July 2022 for both companies** — 3.5 months pre-banning. The 16 Nov 2022 HH audit letter is the standard escalation of the unanswered July letter plus three failed calls; its timing was set by the ATO's internal clock.
2. **The trigger is stated in the ATO's own reasons** (FFG ¶26): super contributions reported by the funds vs STP payroll data. Data matching. No complaint or referral required, and none appears.
3. **Zero NDIS fingerprints** in any internal document (Feb 2023 SEO emails, call notes, Siebel case), consistent with the s 24A(1) “no documents exist” findings for ATO↔Commission/NDIA/Fraud Fusion Taskforce correspondence.
4. FFG's audit is 2025–26, not 2022 — nothing to do with the banning week at all.

**Verdict: convergence, not coordination.** The same underlying stress (unpaid/late super through FY22) produced both correlations; the ATO machinery ran on data and its own timetable.

**The hole in the middle:** the ATO accepted the Nov 2024 late-payment offsets for Mar'22 (100%) and Dec'22, then simply never amended Jun'22 or Sep'22 — the two biggest quarters — despite identical statements claiming 100% offsets, lodged the same day, by the same agent, verified against the same fund data (MATS). If Jun and Sep are amended on the same basis:
- SGC falls from $81,706 to **≈ $32,723** (−$48,983)
- Penalties recalculated on the reduced base fall by **≈ $60–70k** (and see the 712% anomaly below)
- **Total HH swing ≈ $100–117k**, plus consequential GIC remission.

**The 712% anomaly:** when the ATO reduced Mar'22 SGC by 83%, it reduced the penalty by only 4.4% and printed the result as “additional charge rate 712.39%”. Part 7 of the SGAA caps the additional charge at **200% of the SGC**. On the amended SGC of $2,059.28 the cap is $4,118.56; the standing penalty is $14,020.42. The ATO's own Dec'22 recalculation respected ~200% of the amended figure; Mar'22 did not. Internally inconsistent and squarely objectionable/remittable.

**Amendment window:** the NOAs themselves state the ATO can amend within **4 years of the SGC assessment**. Jun'22 was assessed 27 Feb 2023 → amendment window closes **≈ 27 Feb 2027 (six months away)**. Sep'22 (assessed 14 May 2024) → May 2028. An amendment request is not an objection — no 60-day limit applies.
