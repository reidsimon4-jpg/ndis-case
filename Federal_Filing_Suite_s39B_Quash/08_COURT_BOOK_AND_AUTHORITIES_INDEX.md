## F. Authorities bundle

### Statutes and rules

1. *National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013* (Cth), historical compilation applicable on 20–21 December 2022 and 21 April 2023: ss 73ZN, 99 and 100.
2. *Judiciary Act 1903* (Cth), s 39B.
3. *Federal Court of Australia Act 1976* (Cth), ss 21–23.
4. *Federal Court Rules 2011*, rr 8.02, 17.01 and 31.11.
5. Administrative and Constitutional Law and Human Rights National Practice Area Practice Note (ACLHR-1), effective 7 February 2025.
6. *Evidence Act 1995* (Cth), s 128, concerning objection to particular evidence that may tend to incriminate and certificates where the statutory procedure applies.
7. *Criminal Procedure Act 2004* (WA), ss 25 and 79, concerning dismissal for want of prosecution and its consequences.

### Cases — core

1. *Sunflower Care Services Pty Ltd v Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission (No 2)* [2025] FCA 1442 at [127]–[141], [180]–[191], [202]–[209], [222], [225]–[231] and formal orders.
2. *Nathanson v Minister for Home Affairs* [2022] HCA 26 at [1]–[2] — material procedural unfairness and realistic possibility.
3. *Minister for Immigration and Citizenship v Li* [2013] HCA 18 at [76] — evident and intelligible justification.
4. *Prior v Mole* [2017] HCA 10 at [4], and if needed [98]–[100] — objectively reasonable grounds for belief.
5. *Re Refugee Review Tribunal; Ex parte Aala* [2000] HCA 57 at [52], [55]–[56], [82]–[83] — jurisdictional error, discretion, alternative remedy and delay.

### Cases — distinguish, not lead

6. *Abdullah and Commissioner, NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission* [2026] ARTA 743 — independent merits hearing; fact-specific cure and evaluation; no immunity from legality review.
7. *Johnston and Commissioner, NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission* [2024] AATA 3438 at [28] — activity specification not challenged.

### Authorities to add only if the respondent raises the issue

- severability of invalid administrative instruments;
- whether certiorari is available as ancillary relief in the precise s 39B pleading;
- effect of pending merits review on discretionary judicial relief;
- lawful use versus fettering by administrative policy; and
- register correction and prospective versus past declaratory relief.
