# Draft Form 35 — Interlocutory application and proposed short minute

**Federal Court of Australia — Western Australia District Registry**  
**Simon Reid and Vida Reid v Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission**

## Interlocutory application

The Applicants apply under r 17.01 of the *Federal Court Rules 2011* for the following orders.

1. The proceeding be listed for a case-management hearing on the first available date.

2. The proceeding be expedited.

3. By [7 DAYS AFTER FIRST DIRECTIONS], the Respondent provide to the Applicants:

    a. the complete decision record for each original and internal-review decision;

    b. the instrument of delegation for each decision-maker;

    c. the documents and attachments identified in each statement of reasons or executive brief as having been considered; and

    d. a privilege schedule for any identified document withheld from the record.

4. By [DATE], the parties confer and file:

    a. a statement of agreed facts;

    b. an agreed chronology;

    c. an indexed and paginated electronic court book; and

    d. a list of the issues that remain genuinely disputed.

5. The Applicants file and serve any further affidavit evidence and a final outline of submissions by [DATE].

6. The Respondent file and serve any affidavit evidence and outline of submissions by [DATE].

7. The Applicants file and serve any reply evidence and reply submissions by [DATE].

8. The final application be listed for hearing on an estimate of one day on the first available date after [DATE].

9. Pending final determination or further order, the Respondent be restrained, by itself, its delegates, officers, employees or agents, from:

    a. enforcing the banning orders against the Applicants as varied on 21 April 2023; and

    b. publishing or representing those orders as operative on the NDIS Commission banning-order register,

subject to the undertakings in the Schedule.

10. Alternatively to order 9, pending final determination or further order, the Respondent be restrained from enforcing the words:

    a. “any involvement with or in the NDIS sector, in any capacity”;

    b. “directly or indirectly”; and

    c. “support, funding, guidance or assistance of any form”,

in each 21 April 2023 varied order.

10A. Further or alternatively, pending final determination, the Respondent be restrained from enforcing the First Applicant’s order merely because he seeks, arranges, attends meetings concerning, communicates about, advocates about, or provides ordinary unpaid familial assistance concerning disability supports or services for Vida Reid or Alex Reid, provided that he does not receive NDIS funding, submit or authorise provider claims, control provider funds, or supply paid support workers through an NDIS provider.

11. The parties notify the Administrative Review Tribunal of the orders made in this proceeding within two business days and cooperate to avoid duplication between this proceeding and ART matters 2023/3361 and 2023/3363.

12. Costs be reserved.

## Schedule — proposed undertakings

**Do not file or offer these undertakings without express instructions. Modify to preserve any concrete lawful work opportunity.**

Until final judgment or further order, each Applicant undertakes to the Court not to:

1. act as an executive, director, key personnel member or provider manager of a registered or unregistered NDIS provider;
2. access PRODA or any provider payment or claiming system;
3. control NDIS participant funds, provider claims or provider invoicing;
4. solicit NDIS participants; or
5. provide direct supports or services to an NDIS participant,

except with the prior written consent of the Respondent or leave of the Court.

The undertakings do not prevent Simon from ordinary unpaid conduct as Vida’s spouse or Alex’s parent or carer in seeking, arranging, attending meetings concerning, communicating about or advocating about their supports, provided he does not undertake the financial, claiming, provider-management or paid-support activities restrained above.

The undertakings are offered solely to manage interlocutory risk and are not admissions concerning the validity of the orders or the merits of the Commission’s allegations.

## Grounds for urgency

1. The challenged wording is materially broader than wording held invalid in *Sunflower Care Services Pty Ltd v Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission (No 2)* [2025] FCA 1442.
2. The orders have operated for more than three years and expire in December 2027, leaving a diminishing period in which final relief can have practical effect.
3. The orders have continuing coercive and publication effects and the Applicants cannot identify the outer boundary of the conduct prohibited.
4. The ART matters commenced in 2023 and remain without final determination.
5. The principal issues are questions of statutory construction and legality capable of resolution on a confined documentary record.
6. Protective undertakings can manage participant and financial risk pending judgment.

### Settling note — delete before filing

Form 35 is used after the proceeding has commenced. If urgent interim relief must be sought at commencement, Registry practice and the duty-judge procedure should be confirmed. Do not seek a publication correction that would conceal the existence of the Court proceeding; ask for accurate notation or removal of the invalid order according to the Court’s direction.
