  REQUEST FOR NON-DISCLOSURE ORDERS UNDER SECTION 70 OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE
                         REVIEW TRIBUNAL ACT 2024

Orders sought:

Under s 70(2) of the Administrative Review Tribunal Act 2024 (Cth) (ART Act), the
Administrative Review Tribunal directs in the above proceedings:

1.     that the following documents not be subject to disclosure requirements under s 27 or
       any other section of the ART Act, and that the disclosure of these documents to the
       applicants or the public at large is prohibited, on the basis that the documents were
       created for the dominant purpose of providing legal advice to the Respondent on the
       matters in dispute and are therefore subject to claims of legal professional privilege:

      a       Attachment O to Briefing Note EC22-022103 in relation to a notice of intention to
              issue a banning order against Simon Reid, being a copy of legal advice provided
              to the Respondent about that briefing note; and

      b       Attachment G to Briefing Note EC22-002521 in relation to the issuing of a
              banning order against Simon Reid, being a copy of legal advice provided to the
              Respondent about that briefing note.
