Key Points
1. The Commission holds information to suggest that Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd (REG ID 4-
   4GHG-1073) has contravened, is contravening or is proposing to contravene, the NDIS Act.
2. For the reasons set out in documents provided, Compliance (WA) and Compliance (QLD/NT)
   considers there is sufficient evidence available for you to form the reasonable belief that
   Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd and Vida Reid, has contravened, is contravening or is proposing
   to contravene the NDIS Act, by failing to comply with their obligations.
3. Compliance WA and Compliance QLD/NT recommends you, as the delegate, review and consider
   the relevant parts of the information they provided (as referenced) in making your decision.

Background
7. The Delegations instrument, No. 2 of 2022 dated 21 June 2022, and effective 22 August 2022,
   provides the Branch Head, North Central West with delegation against NDIS Act s73N, s73P, and
   s73ZN.
Legislative Obligations
8. Section 73J of the NDIS Act provides that,
         A person contravenes this section if the person:
            (a) is a registered NDIS provider; and
            (b) breaches a condition to which the registration of the person is subject.
        Civil penalty:     250 penalty units.
9. The conditions include those provided at section 73F of the NDIS Act. Section 73F of the NDIS Act
   states that the registration of a person as a registered NDIS provider is subject to a number of
   conditions. These conditions include:
10. Section 73F(2)(b) - a condition that the person comply with all applicable requirements of the
    NDIS Code of Conduct;
11. Section 73F(2)(c) - a condition that the person comply with all applicable standards and other
    requirements of the NDIS Practice Standards;
12. Section 73T(1) of the NDIS Act states that the National Disability Insurance Scheme rules may
    make provision for or in relation to standards concerning the quality of supports or services to be
    provided by registered NDIS providers.
13. Further, the NDIS Act s73V provides that NDIS providers and members of the key personnel of
    NDIS providers must comply with the NDIS Code of Conduct, and

        (3) A person contravenes this section if the person:
                 (a) is subject to a requirement under the NDIS Code of Conduct; and
                (b) fails to comply with the requirement.
        Civil penalty:     250 penalty units.
14. The NDIS Act s73N provides for the Suspension of registration on the Commission’s initiative, for
    a specified period (not longer than 30 days) where
    14.1. S73N (1)(a) the Commissioner reasonably believes that the person has contravened, is
          contravening, or is proposing to contravene, this Act; or…
    14.2. S73N (1)(e) the Commissioner is no longer satisfied that the key personnel of the person (if
          any) are suitable to be involved in the provision of supports or services to people with
          disability, having regard to any matters prescribed by the National Disability Insurance
          Scheme rules for the purposes of this paragraph.
15. The NDIS Act s73P provides that registration can be revoked if the same circumstances as
    articulated at 14.1 are met.
16. Further, the NDIS Act s73ZN provides that Banning orders can be given if the same circumstances
    as articulated at 14.1 arise.
17. Both Revocation of registration and Banning orders require a process to be followed whereby a
    notice is issued to the relevant party affording them an opportunity to make submissions on the
    matter.
Financial Implications

20. Will be primarily determined upon receipt of submissions from the parties we are recommending
    documents be issued to on this occasion.

ASSESSMENT

Compliance WA and Compliance QLD/NT has completed a comprehensive assessment of
the evidence and information obtained in this matter. Given the large volume of evidence
and multiple allegations made against Forward Focused Group and Ms Reid, the
assessment of the evidence has not been replicated within this document, nor has all the
evidence been provided as an attachment. The evidence of direct relevance to the alleged
breaches of the NDIS Act, including the NDIS Code of Conduct and the NDIS Practice
Standards has been summarised and attached.

Unfortunately, due to the recurrent or frequent nature of the some alleged conduct,
Compliance WA and Compliance QLD/NT have not been able to ascertain specific dates of
all of the contraventions identified. However, we can confirm that the conduct occurred after
the commencement of the NDIS Code of Conduct in WA (1 December 2020).

Compliance WA and Compliance QLD/NT has placed importance on the information
provided by Danica Hall and Janine Hall, regarding consideration of actions the Commission
should take. The information provided is consistent and demonstrates a pattern of behaviour
by FFG and Ms Reid in terms of the type of support she provides NDIS participants, and the
control and manipulative behaviour she exerts over NDIS participants.

According to the NDIA Report, Forward Focused Group provided services to 141
participants (Attachment 5).

Compliance (WA) and Compliance (QLD/NT) hold concerns about the risk associated with
Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd continuing to operate as a registered NDIS provider and
supporting vulnerable NDIS participants, given the seriousness of the provider’s
contraventions of the NDIS Act. Particularly those relating to breaches of the NDIS Code of
Conduct.

Compliance (WA) and Compliance (QLD/NT) considers Forward Focused Group’s conduct,
in particular, the failure to act with integrity, honesty and transparency, is serious and
warrants action by the Commission.

Banning Order/s

When determining whether a person is suitable to be providing supports and services to
people with disability, the delegate does not require any relevant criminal matters to have
been finalised before deciding to make a Banning Order against an individual. The service of
a banning order against Ms Reid is an administrative decision. The Banning Order may be
varied or revoked, should new information be provided to the Commission.

Importantly, if a Banning Order is issued to Ms Reid, the Banning Order will not prevent her
or her son’s access to NDIS funded supports and services. However, Ms Reid will be unable
to be the provider of NDIS funded supports and services to her son, which, in any case, is
discouraged by the NDIA.

A Banning Order would restrict Ms Reid’s capacity to earn an income. Compliance (WA) and
Compliance (QLD/NT) acknowledge the imposition of a Banning Order will affect her
employment opportunities. However, it is with in the Commission’s responsibilities to
consider the suitability of Ms Reid, and for the delegate to weigh up the interests of Ms Reid
in continuing to work in the disability sector against the interests of NDIS participants.

The NDIA has measures in place relating to Ms Reid and Forward Focused Group. Whilst
it’s acknowledged that Forward Focused Group was the subject of an NDIA compliance lock
on 3 May 2022, the Banning Order relates to Ms Reid’s suitability to be involved in the
disability sector. The NDIA’s action relate to the protection of government assets, while it is
for the Commission to make a determination in relation to Ms Reid’s suitability to remain in
the disability sector.

In considering the length of any banning order, it is acknowledged that while Ms Reid has
not been convicted of any criminal offence in relation to the matter set out in this brief, we
need to ensure that participants are not put at risk.

The Banning Order for a period of ten years will act as a preventative and protective
measure to ensure Ms Reid does not have the opportunity to engage in any activities of
concern.

The compliance team, if provided with an outcome of criminal proceedings relating to Ms
Reid, will assess the outcome and consider whether to make a recommendation to the
delegate to consider varying the Banning Order.
