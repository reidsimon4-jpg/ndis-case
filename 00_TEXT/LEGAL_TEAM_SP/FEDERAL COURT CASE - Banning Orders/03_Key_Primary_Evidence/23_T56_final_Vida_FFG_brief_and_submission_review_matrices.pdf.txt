Legislative Obligations
26. Section 73J of the NDIS Act provides that,
                                             Official
                                                 1087                                        Page 3 of 5
                                                Official                                                        T56
         A person contravenes this section if the person:
            (a) is a registered NDIS provider; and
            (b) breaches a condition to which the registration of the person is subject.
        Civil penalty:     250 penalty units.
27. The conditions include those provided at section 73F of the NDIS Act. Section 73F of the NDIS Act
    states that the registration of a person as a registered NDIS provider is subject to a number of
    conditions. These conditions include:
28. Section 73F(2)(b) - a condition that the person comply with all applicable requirements of the
    NDIS Code of Conduct;
29. Section 73F(2)(c) - a condition that the person comply with all applicable standards and other
    requirements of the NDIS Practice Standards;
30. Section 73T(1) of the NDIS Act states that the National Disability Insurance Scheme rules may
    make provision for or in relation to standards concerning the quality of supports or services to be
    provided by registered NDIS providers.
31. Further, the NDIS Act s73V provides that NDIS providers and members of the key personnel of
    NDIS providers must comply with the NDIS Code of Conduct, and
        (3) A person contravenes this section if the person:
                 (a) is subject to a requirement under the NDIS Code of Conduct; and
                (b) fails to comply with the requirement.
        Civil penalty:     250 penalty units.
32. The NDIS Act s73N provides for the Suspension of registration on the Commission’s initiative, for
    a specified period (not longer than 30 days) where
    32.1. S73N (1)(a) the Commissioner reasonably believes that the person has contravened, is
          contravening, or is proposing to contravene, this Act; or…
    32.2. S73N (1)(e) the Commissioner is no longer satisfied that the key personnel of the person (if
          any) are suitable to be involved in the provision of supports or services to people with
          disability, having regard to any matters prescribed by the National Disability Insurance
          Scheme rules for the purposes of this paragraph.
33. The NDIS Act s73P provides that registration can be revoked if the same circumstances as
    articulated at 14.1 are met.
34. Further, the NDIS Act s73ZN provides that Banning orders can be given if the same circumstances
    as articulated at 14.1 arise.
35. Both Revocation of registration and Banning orders require a process to be followed whereby a
    notice is issued to the relevant party affording them an opportunity to make submissions on the
    matter.
