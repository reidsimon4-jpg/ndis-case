Request for a stay

We refer to Mrs Reid’s request of 28 January 2025 to stay her application (and that
of Forward Focussed Group [FFG]), seeking a review of the NDIS Commission’s
decision to issue a Banning Order under section 73ZN(2) of the National Disability
Insurance Scheme Act 2013 made on 20 December 2022.

At the outset and to avoid confusion, we confirm that we act for the NDIS
Commission. The Commission is an independent government regulator who
regulates the quality and safety of NDIS supports and services. In these
proceedings, we do NOT act for the National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA),
who are the government agency that implements and manages the NDIS. For
abundant clarity, the NDIA are NOT a party to these proceedings. We read the
references in Mrs Reid’s submissions to the NDIS and the NDIS Investigator as
being references to the NDIA.

Following receipt of Mrs Reid’s submissions, the Commission has made enquiries
with the NDIA and has been informed that there is no current criminal investigation
pertaining to Mrs Reid or to FFG. In light of this information, the Commission cannot
agree to the stay requested by Mrs Reid, having regard to the Tribunal’s objectives
in section 9 of the Administrative Review Tribunal Act 2024.

We observe that Mrs Reid’s recent stay request is made against the following recent
procedural background to these proceedings:

A Directions hearing was held on 3 October 2024 before Deputy President Lazanas
where Mrs Reid was represented by her newly appointed solicitors. The subsequent
directions included for Mrs Reid and FFG to file and serve any written submissions
and evidence in relation to their stay application and request for confidentiality
regarding a medical report before 24 October 2024 and for an interlocutory hearing
to be listed on 25 November 2024.
