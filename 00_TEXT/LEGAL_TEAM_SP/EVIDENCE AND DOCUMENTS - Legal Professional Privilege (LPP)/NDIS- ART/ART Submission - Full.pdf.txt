 14.    Furthermore, in a joint hearing, evidence or arguments presented for

        own evidence. The converse is also true. Severance ensures the Tribunal
        can consider I, VIDA REID’s case on its own merits, focusing on my
        conduct and the specific evidence relating to my, without being swayed
        by evidence that strictly concerns Mr. Reid or the company.

 18.    (iv) Prejudice from Joint Evidence and Joint Decision-Making: The
        current consolidation of matters means that all evidence filed is deemed
        to be in common and that a single hearing (or set of findings) might
        dispose of all three cases . The Applicant submits that this arrangement is
        no longer tenable without causing injustice. For instance, if the matters
        remained joined and proceeded to hearing, the Tribunal might hear a
        large volume of evidence – some of which relates only to FFG or only to
        Mr. Reid – and yet that evidence would technically be “before it” in
        deciding I, VIDA REID’s case as well. Even with the best of intentions,
        there is a risk that evidence not strictly relevant to I, VIDA REID
        could influence the outcome for my. The safer course, to preserve
        impartial adjudication, is to formally segregate the cases and require that
        only evidence properly relating to I, VIDA REID’s matter is led in my
        hearing. The Applicant notes that even the Respondent has treated the
        applicants differently in certain respects (for example, the duration and
        terms of banning orders differ, as do some allegations). A joint hearing
        could thus result in a composite outcome that does not accurately reflect
        the nuances of each party’s situation. This composite approach would be
        prejudicial to I, VIDA REID, who deserves a finding that is based on my
        own conduct and circumstances alone.
 19.    (v) Efficiency and Case Management: It is acknowledged that tribunals
        often join matters for efficiency wmye facts overlap. However, efficiency
        must not trump fairness. In this case, any administrative efficiency gained
        by a joint hearing is outweighed by the practical difficulties and
        fairness issues highlighted above. Moreover, severing I, VIDA REID’s
        matter need not unduly waste resources – quite the contrary. My case, if
        separated, could potentially proceed on a clearer, narrower path,
        focusing on the specific evidence about my role (for example, the period
        up to October 2022) and the legal issues about my banning order. This
        might well shorten my hearing. The remaining matters (Mr. Reid and
        FFG) could be managed together or separately as appropriate, but I, VIDA
        REID would no longer be required to attend to those. If anything,
        severance may streamline the resolution by allowing each matter to
        be negotiated or heard without entanglement. For example, it is
        conceivable that Mr. Reid and the Respondent could reach some consent
        position or narrowed issues once I, VIDA REID’s participation is carved
        out. In sum, separate hearings would localize each dispute, likely
        making each easier to settle or adjudicate.
