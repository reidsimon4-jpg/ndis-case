Hi Leah,

Thankyou for taking the time to talk to me this afternoon. My name is Candice Goldsmith, I am the mother of 3 children and all of them require NDIS support.

Janey does not attend school, they were medically cleared from attending all of last year due to significant mental health issues.
Janey has just started a traineeship (2 days per week) at AYLA working in the op shop. Janey is supported there by staff as AYLA are also and NDIS provider.
Janey attend Headspace Early Psychosis team regularly however they are unable to provide consistent psychological support (there is only one psychologist for the whole
centre) and since May, this psychologist has rescheduled 14 appointments meaning Janey has only had face to face 3 times. Headspace support Janey to attend private
psychology to have DBT which os required to treat BPD symptoms.
Janey is on very high doses of antipsychotic and antidepressant medication.
Janey has a very long (10 year history) of self harm and suicidal ideation/attempts. Janey is an amazing artist and musician.

Tristan is 16 1/2 years old. Tristan has a diagnosis of Autism (moderate severity) as well as a recent diagnosis of Generalised Anxiety Disorder. Tristan has been
homeschooled for the past 6 years due to a significant traumatic event that occurred whilst at mainstream schooling.
Tristan is on antidepressant medication.
Tristan struggles with most aspects of daily life as well as having significant sensory overload. Tristan loves his dog Odie and spending time with Janey looking at op shops.
Tristan also has a strong interest in animation

Bonnie (Lily-li) is 13 years old. She has a diagnosis of SPD, Hypermobility, Autism level 2 as well as severe ADHD.
Bonnie attends year 7 at Kalamunda SHS and is in the specialist program for visual arts. Bonnie struggles with motor planning and attention but does not require nor wish
medication support.
Bonnie loves the outdoors, cooking and reading.

I will send through the plans shortly but hope this gives you some idea of who these three amazing individuals are and some of the barriers they face day to day.
