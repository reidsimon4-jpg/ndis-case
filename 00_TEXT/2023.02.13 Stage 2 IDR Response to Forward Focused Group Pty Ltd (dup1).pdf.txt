under ProRisk Health Care Professionals Civil Liability Policy No.HP-006656-2022 from 31

(Declinature Letter).

Swiss Re International SE (SRI) is the underwriter of the Policy and has conducted a Stage 2
review of the claim in light of your email.

Having done so, SRI confirms the indemnity position as outlined in the Declinature Letter.

1.    BACKGROUND

1.1   The factual background is set out in the Declinature Letter and, as such, we do not repeat
      it here.

2.    INDEMNITY

2.1   The indemnity position outlined in the Declinature Letter is that Exclusion 3.1 of the Policy
      applies to the whole of the claim and there is no cover for any part of the claim by FFG as:

      (a)      one aspect of the NDIS investigation, and its decision to suspend FFG’s registration,
               is due to alleged veiled and direct threats, bullying and harassment by employees
               and officers;

      (b)      threats, bullying and harassment, whether written or spoken word, each satisfy the
               ordinary definition of “verbal abuse”;




a
      (c)      the NDIS investigation is therefore connected with “verbal abuse” committed by
               employees/officers of FFG; and

      (d)      Exclusion 3.1 applies to both actual and alleged abuse. It is not relevant that there
               is no final determination. It is sufficient that there is only an allegation of abuse for
               Exclusion 3.1 to operate.

2.2   In your email, you asked that matter be escalated to Stage 2 as the Stage 1 process had
      not resolved the complaint to your satisfaction.

2.3   FFG’s basis for requesting a Stage 1 review was that the Declinature Letter assumed FFG’s
      knowledge of the alleged abuse, but did not state the basis for the assumption.

      Review

2.4   Having reviewed the NDIS Revocation Notice, Suspension Notice, the Policy, the
      Declinature Letter and your email, we agree that the indemnity position set out in the
      Declinature Letter, and the stated basis for the Declinature Letter, is sound.

2.5   In relation to your point that the Declinature Letter assumed FFG’S knowledge of the
      alleged abuse, it is stated in the Revocation Notice that you, also referred to as the
      “Founding Executive Director of FFG”, were the perpetrator of the alleged abuse.

2.6   The Revocation Notice outlines the following examples of “veiled and direct threats,
      bullying and harassment”:

      (a)      across May and June 2022, the Provider attempted to obtain control of participant
               funds and participants supports for participant Alexis Bott. When the participant
               confirmed, in writing that they didn’t want to take the action to release their funds
               from the existing manager the Founding Executive Director writes “we will be
               terminating your services with us effective immediately”.

      (b)      series of emails 24 June 2022 to 19 June 2022 regarding participant Sarah-Jane
               Clulow advising a four week notice for service termination. The emails show that
               the participant was seeking review of an invoice for services not received.

      (c)      on 1 July 2022, the Provider responds to an account statement from a supplier
               stating that, ““we will no longer pay any invoices for Sarah. She has terminated her
               contract with us.”

      (d)      series of emails 15 June 2022 to 19 June 2022 regarding participant Corina
               Abraham ceasing some services with Hybrid Hive. The participant seeks to meet to
               discuss their concerns. By email dated 19 June, the Provider through Vida Reid
               purports to cease support coordination and terminate the contract for all services
               with immediate effect. The email claims “ongoing abusive manner of our staff by
               yourself NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission 5 and the break in trust” as the
               grounds. The email includes a perceived threat that invoices are to be paid
               immediately or be reported to the NDIS.

      (e)      email 16 June 2022 by the Founding Director to participant, Ms Abraham, provides
               that the support worker has a non-compete and non-disclosure contract which will
               prevent her for working privately for the participant. It also states that if the
               participant had “come to us in good faith…I would have said okay.”

      (f)    email 16 June 2022 by the Founding Director to participant, Ms Abraham, “I would
             like all of your outstanding invoices paid immediately please or I will report this to
             the NDIS”.

2.7   The alleged perpetrator is stated in the above emails to be the “Founding Director” of FFG.
      FFG is the policyholder.

2.8   In circumstances where the “Founding Director” has direct knowledge of the alleged
      abuse, as she is identified as the alleged perpetrator, that knowledge is therefore
      attributed to FFG.

2.9   In any event, the carve out for vicarious liability does not operate because the policyholder
      was aware of the alleged abuse.
