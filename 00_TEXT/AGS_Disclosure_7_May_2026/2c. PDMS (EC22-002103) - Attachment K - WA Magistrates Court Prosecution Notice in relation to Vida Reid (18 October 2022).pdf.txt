That between 1 December 2021 and 17 May 2022 at Perth in the State of Western Australia, Vida

property was proceeds of crime and at the time of dealing the value of the money or property was

Charge 2 of 4

That between 26 December 2021 and 23 June 2022 at Perth in the State of Western Australia,
Vida REID dealt with money or other property, it being reasonable to suspect that such money or
other property was proceeds of crime and at the time of dealing the value of the money or property
was more than $100,000, contrary to section 400.9(1) of the Criminal Code (Cth).

Charge 3 of 4

That between 30 November 2021 and 08 April 2022 at Perth in the State of Western Australia,
Vida REID caused payment requests to be submitted, which he knew to be false, to the National
Disability Insurance Agency, a Commonwealth entity, with the intention of dishonestly causing a
loss, contrary to section 135.1 (3) of the Criminal Code (Cth).

Charge 4 of 4

That between 18 November 2021 and 19 April 2022 at Perth in the State of Western Australia,
Vida REID caused payment requests to be submitted, which he knew to be false, to the National
Disability Insurance Agency, a Commonwealth entity, with the intention of dishonestly causing a
loss, contrary to section 135.1(3) of the Criminal Code (Cth).
