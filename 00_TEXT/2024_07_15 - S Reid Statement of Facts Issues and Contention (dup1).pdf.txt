Statutory Framework

  10.   Section 73ZN(2) of the NDIS Act gives the Commissioner the discretion to make a
        banning order prohibiting or restricting a person employed or otherwise engaged
        by an NDIS provider:

        'The Commissioner may, by written notice, make an order prohibiting or restricting
                a person who is or was employed or otherwise engaged by an NDIS provider,
                or a person who is or was a member of the key personnel of an NDIS provider,
                from engaging in specified activities if:

    94. The central allegation against Mr and Mrs Reid relates to FFG and Hybrid Hive
                wrongfully making claims against NDIS. However, multiple persons were involved
                in those claims. Yet only Mr and Mrs Reid have been banned.
    95.         Section 73ZN(2) of the NDIS Act allows for banning orders in relation to "a person
                who is or was employed or otherwise engaged by an NDIS provider, or a person who is
                or was a member of the key personnel of an NDIS provider",
    96.         Section 11A defines 'key personnel' as:

                 "(a) a member of the group of persons who is responsible for the executive decisions
                        of the person or entity;


          (c)      any other person who has authority or responsibility for (or significant influence
                   over) planning, directing or controlling the activities of the person or entity."

    97.         The above means that other people may be subject to sanction for wrongdoing, not
                just directors.
    98.         Mr Reid relied upon and was entitled to rely on others as to the accuracy and
                correctness of claims, especially the Chief Financial Officer and accounting staff of
                the corporations, as is specifically provided for in section 189 of the Corporations
                Act 2001 (Cth).
    99.         Yet it appears the Respondent has failed, possibly deliberately so, to investigate any
                other key personnel of FFG or Hybrid Hive.
    100. Indeed, there has been no evidence produced that the Chief Financial Officer of the
                two companies, Mr Rejaul Alam, was ever questioned by the Respondent's
                investigators.
    101. Currently before the Tribunal, there is no evidence as to who actually produced the
                invoices in question. No evidence as to why the said invoices were issued or the basis

                invoice.
