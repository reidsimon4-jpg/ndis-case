# Holes in the Commission's case against Simon Reid (2023/3363)
**Date:** 25 Sep 2026
**Sources:** the AGS disclosure of 7 May 2026 (all 59 files, witness statements read by OCR), the internal review (IR) of 21 Apr 2023, and the NDIA Statement of Material Facts (SoMF).
**Verification:** every quote below was checked against the source text. OCR can garble words, so re-check each quote against the PDF before using it in a filing.

---

## A. The case has no personal act by you. This is the core hole.

1. **The NDIA never identified who submitted any claim.** The SoMF says the invoices were submitted by "an unknown person using FFG software 'Xero'" (SoMF paras 46, 50, 57 and 61). The Goodwin FFG claims went through "the ALAM PRODA Account". The only link to you is that you were a **joint bank signatory** (paras 45, 53, 64).
2. **The decision brief rests on your position only.** It relies on your "position of responsibility and authority over Hybrid" and says you "likely benefited" (EC22-002521 para 9). Every specific act it describes is attributed to Vida or Alam.
3. **The staff witnesses point to Vida and Rejaul Alam, not you.**
   - Danica Hall: "false invoices were sent by Rejaul under the direction of Vida"; invoicing was "overseen by Rejaul".
   - Terri Roberts: "Vida wanted us to bill … 15 hours per day".
   - Neither says you directed any billing.
4. **Goodwin clears you on services.** "Simon never was involved with providing the services. I think he was in finance." She also says: "I was never aware of which company was charging for services."
5. **Only Vida was charged.** The SoMF says "V REID and S REID caused 81 false 'payment requests'", but only Vida was charged. Her charges were dismissed on 23 Feb 2024. The IR tied the length of your ban to the "ongoing investigation" and "processes … in relation to Mr Reid" (IR paras 46–47). The decision brief itself says the order "may be varied or revoked" on "a determination of a court" (para 13).

## B. The Commission's own documents contradict each other

6. **Three different versions of who ran Hybrid:**
   - SoMF paras 2 and 22: "The sole Director of HYBRID is V REID"; you were director of **The Hive**, the shareholder company.
   - Decision brief para 3.1: you were "sole director and shareholder" of Hybrid.
   - IR para 29(c): co-director from 17 Feb 2022, sole director from 22 Aug 2022.
7. **Jessica Smith's evidence is misstated.**
   - IR 31(c) says "Hybrid had invoiced her on five separate occasions" and that she "had not heard of Hybrid". In fact:
     - Two of the five invoices (INV-1468 and INV-1475, the latter wrongly cited as "1075") are **FFG** invoices. Only three are Hybrid invoices.
     - Her statement says the payments were ones "Forward Focus Group had submitted".
   - Attachment M labels her issue "FFG, Infinity Living".
   - The notice of intention and the briefs call her "Jessica Harrison".
8. **Regali's evidence is outside your time and outside the IR's own rule.** The only Hybrid invoice (INV-0116) is dated 24 Dec 2021, for 1–21 Dec 2021, which is before your 17 Feb 2022 directorship. IR 32(a) says conduct from periods when you held no office was excluded, yet IR 31(a) relies on it.
9. **The Goodwin/Hybrid claims start before your directorship.** They run from 25 Jan to 10 May 2022, so the claims before 17 Feb 2022 predate your office. The SoMF gives no date split.
10. **The "11 suspicious invoice" reports cannot be split by company.** SoMF para 35 describes them as invoices "by HYBRID and FFG". The IR used them only "to the extent that it relates to Hybrid" (29(h)(v)), but that split exists nowhere. They are also *reports of suspicion*, not findings.
11. **The SoMF has errors of its own.**
    - The date range reads "26 November 2021 and 22 June **2020**", so it ends before it starts. The decision brief silently changed it to 2022.
    - The "81 payment requests" total only works if Clulow's invoices are counted instead of claims. Counted as claims, it is about 65.

## C. The evidence overstates "services not provided"

12. **Goodwin received services, on her own account.**
    - She confirms: stays at the Clipper hotel ("early April for about 5 days" and "22 April to 2 May 2022"), a weekly psychologist, carers including Donna, and cooking and cleaning.
    - She disputes only specific items: short-term accommodation on other dates, self-managed capacity building, yard maintenance, and having two carers at once.
    - The NDIA's "no service" figure ($127,475.30) equals **100% of everything paid**.
    - The FFG case notes (Apr–May 2022) back her account of services received.
13. **Yvonne Harrison and Jessica Smith dispute amounts, not delivery.**
    - Harrison: support workers came "Tuesday, Thursdays and Fridays" and a gardener fortnightly. Her complaint is about how many hours were billed.
    - Smith: "Charlie received this respite"; her complaint is "I don't agree with the hours claimed".
14. **Shapiera was left out of the decision documents.**
    - He signed a service agreement with Hybrid through you: "At HH I dealt with Simon REID … I signed a service agreement with HH".
    - He confirms Hybrid delivered services.
    - He says he was "extremely happy with services I have received from HH and FFG".
    - This contradicts IR 30(a), which says Hybrid claimed without service agreements. Attachment M reduces him to "Control of Hybrid Hive – Simon Reid".
15. **Every witness statement was prepared for a prosecution of Vida, not an inquiry into you.**
    - All are headed "R v Vida Reid", except Shapiera's ("Project ARCHER").
    - Several are built on investigator prompts ("Tim JARRATT has told me that HH have claimed…").

## D. Dream Big and "circumvention" (IR 41–43)

16. **The ASIC form was a company registration, not an NDIS provider registration.** The IR itself says the form was submitted "to ASIC" (IR 11(h)(i) and 42(a)) but then calls Dream Big "an NDIS provider".
17. **Everything happened before the ban.** The ASIC form was lodged on 29 Oct 2022; Burke's events are dated 15 Nov, 1 Dec and 10 Dec 2022; the ban was made on 21 Dec 2022. You cannot "circumvent" an order that did not yet exist.
18. **Burke's evidence is second- and third-hand.**
    - It rests on what she "had heard", on things Shapiera said, and on things Vida said.
    - She never says she dealt with you.
    - She never says Dream Big was registered with the NDIS Commission, delivered any support, or was owned by you.
    - She had twice reported "Vida and Simon" to the NDIA, and she admits lying about her salary (para 68).
    - IR 42(c) ("Mr Reid had established or was the owner … operated as an NDIS provider") goes beyond what her statement says.

## E. Documents withheld and mis-described (from the earlier review)

19. **AGS's reasons for withholding don't match the briefing note.** AGS withheld Attachments A–G and Q as "unrelated companies". The briefing note lists B as the notice to **Hybrid**, and D–G as notices to companies where it records you as director, secretary or shareholder. Q is a risk assessment of Hybrid. Attachment P is the NDIA's conditions on using its evidence, not an internal email chain.
20. **Attachment M embellishes and mis-dates evidence.**
    - It dates Terri Roberts' evidence 11 Oct 2022 but includes the drug allegation that appears only in her 18 Oct statement.
    - It adds "first year of operation" and "Benefiting from fraud – V and S Reid" to Danica Hall's evidence, which her statement does not say.

## F. What still hurts you (be ready for these)

- **Benediktsson is the clean allegation.** Seven Hybrid invoices (services 2 May–24 Aug 2022) fall inside your directorship. He says "never heard of Hybrid Hive or received any services from them at all", and you were his coach and point of contact.
- **Jessica Smith's three Hybrid invoices** (INV-1070, 1073, 1074: self-care 24 Mar–15 Jul 2022) fall inside your directorship. She says Charlie never received self-care.
- **You were operationally involved at Hybrid.** Shapiera "dealt with Simon REID" there. That undercuts any "I was just finance" line.
- **The Regali Hive invoice** is payable into the ANZ account on which you were a joint signatory.
- **Personal allegations in Attachment M and Roberts.** All are unparticularised or hearsay, but the Tribunal decides afresh, so AGS could rely on them.
  - Whisky and sleeping tablets (Suzanne Hall complaint, 2021).
  - Drug use at social events, undated.
  - A Kalgoorlie visit charged to a participant's plan, undated, no amount.
  - Recovery coaching with only a two-day course.
  - A $140k salary.
- **Acting for FFG while not a director.** In 2021, while not an FFG director, you acted for FFG (Harrison meeting, ffg.net.au email, recovery coaching). The IR's "no office, no responsibility" reasoning can be turned against you here.
- **Post-ban conduct.** The Vitality recruitment email (9 Feb 2023) came after the ban. You also rejoined FFG as a director on 1 Nov 2022, after Vida's arrest.
- **Evidence you never produced.** You made no submissions at internal review, and the exculpatory evidence your lawyers said you held was never produced (IR 39–40). The Tribunal will expect it now.
