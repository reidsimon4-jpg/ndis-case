**From:** Vida Reid (vida@ffg.net.au) [forwarded to Tom & Co Legal in June 2023, but originally sent 30 September 2022]

Vida's contemporaneous account of a phone call she received at 4:12 pm that afternoon from a participant of FFG (Jonathan Shapiera, who was at the time residing in one of FFG's Supported Independent Living houses, and who later told the Commission's case officers that he had been "happy with the services I have received" from FFG and Hybrid Hive). Mrs Reid identifies the connections between the actors:

> *"1. **Amanda Damon** — Director of Nurse Assist 24/7
> 2. **Terri Roberts** — Business Advisor of Nurse Assist 24/7 — previously worked at FFG as a Service Manager but was called into a disciplinary meeting over drug use in the workplace.
> 3. **Sukhi Tear** — Director at Diversitas WA — Terri's friend and also used to work with Nathan Halls in NDIA and is allegedly a close friend of Nathans"*

Mrs Reid further records the operational impact: five FFG clients lost that day; two more "scared to talk to us because of all these rumours, emails and defaming remarks"; a staff member (Madeline Dawson) had been told at a separate medical appointment with Dr Marie Michelle at Wellard Medical that Dr Michelle had received a call from "Terri and Nathan Halls" stating that "FFG and the Hive are horrible people who should be put away for ever"; a participant (Colleen Hass) was reported to have cut herself during that appointment in response. Mrs Reid concludes: *"I'm trying to run a business and this has just deteriorated my disability and made me suicidal."*

### Significance — the most important single piece of pre-arrest context

The 30 September 2022 email is the single most significant piece of pre-arrest documentary evidence. It establishes, in contemporaneous applicant-side writing nineteen days before Mrs Reid's arrest, three matters:

(a) **The witnesses the Commission later relied on were embedded in a competing-business network.** Ms Terri Roberts — one of the principal named witnesses in the Commission's case (cited in EC22-002103 paragraphs 14.1 and 14.5; reproduced in the Statement of Reasons accompanying the Hybrid Hive Banning Order at paragraph 8(d)(v)) — was at the time of her statements (a) a Business Advisor of Nurse Assist 24/7 (a competing business of FFG); (b) a former FFG employee who left in circumstances involving a workplace disciplinary process; and (c) a personal friend of Sukhi Tear (Director of Diversitas WA), who was operating in concert against the applicants. **Amanda Damon** — Director of Nurse Assist 24/7 — also appears in EC22-002103 as a director and shareholder of N-Able Perth Kids Rehab Centre Pty Ltd, one of the seven entities recommended for banning notices on 17 November 2022. The Commission's own brief therefore named, as a co-director of one of the entities to be banned, a person who was at the same time a director of a competing business operating in concert against the applicants.

(b) **A named NDIA officer (Mr Nathan Halls), described as an Assistant Director of the NDIA, is alleged to have provided FFG's client list to a competitor** and to have been "personally helping" the competitor move FFG's clients across to her business. The Commission has at no point investigated or acknowledged this allegation.

(c) **The "complaints" the Commission relied on were sourced from a competitor network operating in concert with an NDIA insider** rather than from independent participants giving voluntary statements. The chronology — Sukhi Tear's call to Mr Shapiera on 30 September 2022; Mrs Reid's arrest on 19 October 2022; the Commission's 17 November 2022 brief listing the same Terri Roberts as a witness — supports the inference.

## 19. Pattern: pre-arrest harassment + alleged NDIA insider conduit + post-banning insurance / regulatory action

Taken together with Part 1 of this document, the Outlook excerpts in Part 2 establish a comprehensive chronology:

- **2021 (per Vida's 30 September 2022 email: "ongoing for over 1 year now")** — Pattern of harassment begins.
- **30 September 2022 (19 days pre-arrest)** — Vida documents to Bennett Law that competitor Diversitas WA (Sukhi Tear) is operating in concert with an NDIA Assistant Director (Nathan Halls), supplying FFG's client list and offering preferential NDIS plan approvals in exchange for "cooperation" against the applicants. Terri Roberts (later relied on by the Commission as a key witness) is identified as a Business Advisor of Nurse Assist 24/7, a competing business.
- **October–November 2022 (pre-arrest)** — Cybertrace investigation of anonymous social-media death threats. Yaran provider-exit concerns. Anonymous online attacks.
- **19 October 2022** — Mrs Reid arrested. Same-day FFG suspension and notices of intention.
- **8 November 2022** — Vida transmits "email from Nathan Halls" to Bennett Law.
- **17 November 2022** — Commission issues notices of intention naming Amanda Damon (Director of Nurse Assist 24/7) as a co-director of N-Able for banning purposes. No disclosure of her competing-business role.
- **30 November 2022** — Tom Monks files written submissions in response.
- **7 December 2022 (eight days after the deadline)** — applicants receive the November 2022 notices by registered post. Vida emails Tom Monks recording the receipt time and identifying the banned entities including "Infinity Living Options — Terri" (Terri Roberts).
