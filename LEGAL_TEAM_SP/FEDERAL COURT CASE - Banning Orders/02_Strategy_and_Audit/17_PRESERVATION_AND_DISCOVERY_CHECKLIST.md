## Evidence-quality controls

For every proposed fact, record:

- native source;
- author;
- date;
- first-hand or hearsay;
- completeness of thread;
- authentication route;
- privilege;
- confidentiality;
- whether the delegate possessed it at the relevant time;
- whether it arose only on review;
- whether it is later current-state evidence;
- the precise proposition it proves; and
- adverse context.
