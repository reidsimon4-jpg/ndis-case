## 9. Native preservation checklist

For each relied-upon message:

1. export as native `.eml` or `.mbox`;
2. retain all transport headers;
3. retain the complete thread, not only the latest forward;
4. preserve attachments in their original binary form;
5. calculate SHA-256 hashes;
6. record the export date and account;
7. retain a PDF viewing copy separately;
8. identify whether content is first-hand, forwarded or hearsay;
9. record privilege and confidentiality status; and
10. do not alter filenames or metadata in the preservation copy.
