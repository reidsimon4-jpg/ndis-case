## EX-10 — Inducement of a participant: son's NDIS access + funding increase, conditioned on leaving FFG (Sept–Oct 2022)

- **Participant:** Jonathan Shapiera (elderly; recent heart surgery/stroke per EX-5); **son Nikolaus** — **refused NDIS access**, said to be legally blind.
- **(a) Primary, first-hand — Jonathan Shapiera's own email, 14 October 2022** (to Channel 9 News, cc Vida & Simon Reid): *"My funding has been increased by **$200,000 over two years** just to move house … He further stated that I would receive an upgrade to my plan **'if I played' along** with these proceedings … Mr. Halls advised that I was to be contacted by Ms. Sukhi Tear … who would be my Service Provider."* In the same chain, to Ms Lundgren: *"my own NDIS plan has increased by some **$100,000 per year** for the simplicity of moving house … **Or is this considered as SHUT UP MONEY?**"* Re the son: the son's NDIS approval *"was promised by Nathan Halls in the consideration that I co-operate."* **Status: the participant's own contemporaneous written allegation — to be confirmed by his signed statement.** (Local copy: `shapiera_complaint_20221014.eml`.)
- **(b) Corroborating relay — Vida → Bennett Law, 30 September 2022** (EX-5), Shapiera relaying Sukhi Tear: *"Nathan Halls has told me that your son Nik wants to get an NDIS plan. **If you cooperate with us we will approve his plan and Nathan will personally ensure this even though he has been rejected** … I will give you the weekend to think over it."* **Status: double hearsay (Tear → Shapiera → Vida).**
- **(c) Primary, partial corroboration — Sukhi Tear → Halls, 30 September 2022** (EX-4): Tear pursuing the son's access reassessment with Halls — *"Is it possible for the NDIS Access Team to reassess Nick's application … I will get this Nathan and his team"* — and seeking Jonathan's current plan and *"Finding a new support provider."* **Status: PRIMARY** (corroborates the Tear↔Halls coordination over both the son's access and the move off FFG; the *conditionality* is Mr Shapiera's characterisation).
- **Sequence (the point):** the son was **refused** access first; the offer to **grant** his access **and increase Jonathan's funding** came **conditioned on leaving FFG**. That sequence is what marks it as an inducement rather than ordinary advocacy.
- **Significance:** using NDIS **funding and access decisions** (government benefits) as the currency to detach a provider's client is a purpose wholly foreign to the protective banning power and to the Scheme's choice-and-control objects; supports both the improper-purpose ground and the contention that participants were **moved by inducement and direction, not free choice** (see EX-3, EX-4, EX-7 and the assault/threats material).

## How the exhibits fit together

EX-6 records the applicants identifying the improper-purpose/ultra-vires theory in real time. The throughline: an NDIA officer steering FFG's removal and reallocating its participants to competing providers — **a purpose foreign to the protective banning power** — months before the December 2022 orders.
