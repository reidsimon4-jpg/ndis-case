**The *Sunflower* case, simply:** the Federal Court (Feutrill J, 21 Nov 2025) held that a ban must name **specific activities** in words clear enough to obey. A ban on "indirect" involvement is **beyond power** ([128], [131], [139]). Your varied order ("any involvement ... in any capacity ... directly or indirectly") is **worse** than the Sunflower wording, so the argument is strong.
**The catch:** [134] says a clearly specified activity is valid even if it only *indirectly* touches supports. Labour hire probably survives. Because the Tribunal remakes the decision itself, the realistic result is a **re-worded, narrower ban, not no ban**.

## 5. Errors to fix before filing (verified)

**Legal errors**
1. **s 24 is the wrong section** (direction 1(d)). It only covers extra findings and reasons, not "we don't hold this document". **Use s 79(2)(a).**
2. **s 53 is the wrong power** for a preliminary hearing (direction 9). It limits the scope of a review. **Use s 79(1) and (4) and s 80**, or better, drop direction 9 altogether.
3. **Add s 79(3)** for directions 4–5. The Tribunal can order the decision-maker to state the grounds supporting its position. It's exactly on point.
4. **Missed weapon: s 30.** The document-production rules "have effect despite any rule of law relating to legal professional privilege". So the Commission's privilege claim alone doesn't excuse giving documents **to the Tribunal**. Use it in the s 70 fight. (It doesn't apply to s 25.)

**Factual and drafting errors**
5. **Para 6, Dream Big.** The internal review says the form was lodged **by Mr Reid ... to ASIC** to register a company ([28(h)(i)], [42(a)]). Your wording reads like an NDIS registration. It is misleading and it backfires. **Delete the example, or state it accurately.**
6. **Para 7, attribution.** "Each claim period falls within the interval he held no office in FFG" fights a point **already won**: the reviewer excluded FFG-period conduct ([29(i)], [32]–[33]). It also invites the Hybrid answer ([29(c)]: co-director of Hybrid from 17 Feb 2022, sole director from 22 Aug 2022). The Goodwin claims run **25 Jan–10 May 2022** and overlap your Hybrid directorship. The only real timing point is the **Regali invoice of 5 Jan 2022** ([31(a)]), which predates your Hybrid office. **Rewrite.**
7. **Para 5, Sunflower.** Add the stronger holding (indirect wording is beyond power: [128], [134], [139]).
8. **FFG para 5.** FFG was banned under **s 73ZN(1)(a)**, not (2). Sunflower's "indirect" holding is about (2) ([127]–[128]). Rely only on the "must be clear enough to obey" reasoning ([131]).
9. **FFG para 3.** The FFG instruments are consistent ("until 12:00am on 21 December 2025"). The only inconsistency is the Tribunal's own para 11. Say that. Also explain that 10 years was cut to 3, so it doesn't clash with the s 114 letter.
10. **Directions 12 and 15 are circular.** Each date is defined by the other. Direction 15 also needs the direction 6 schedule 14 days before that schedule exists.
11. **Direction 13, "after 4 Dec 2026".** It can't be met on your own day counts. The earliest realistic date is about late Jan 2027 before the preliminary steps even start.
12. **Headings.** AGS's minute is **dated 12 Aug**, served 14 Aug. Fix all three documents.
13. **Vida's order date (20 vs 21 Dec 2022) and the "(2)(a)(i)→(iii)" claim.** Her instruments aren't in the folder, so these are **unverified**. Check them or delete them.
14. **Vida para 4, "prosecution stating an intention to recharge".** An own goal: it props up the reviewer's "until legal processes finish" reason for the ban's length. **Delete.**
15. **Direction 15 references** (116694, 116695, INV22-080) aren't in any folder document. **Verify them before citing.**
16. **s 114 letter.** It relies on the 31 Jul reasons, which aren't in the folder, so it can't be checked here. Check each item against the decision PDF (the Gmail attachment, 31 Jul).
