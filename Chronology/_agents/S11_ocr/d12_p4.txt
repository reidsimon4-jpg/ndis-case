The NDIS Quality & Safeguards Commission failed to provide the organisation’s Director with disclosure
on allegations made and what evidence was relied on to make the original decision. The Director was
unable to properly address any allegations and was not given the opportunity to work with the

evidence made available by the Commission on 20 March 2023 to assist with the internal review process.

lack of evidence provided and denying the Director the opportunity to respond to the allegations made
against the organisation.
