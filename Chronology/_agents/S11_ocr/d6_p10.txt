Dear Registrar

Given the late filing of the applicant's SFIC in Simon Reid's application (no
further evidence was filed), the respondent requests that the direction for the
filing of the respondent's SFIC in Simon's application be amended as follows:

2. In direction 3 by changing the date that the Respondent file with the Tribunal
and serve on the Applicants a Statement of Facts, Issues and Contentions and
any further evidence on which the Respondent proposes to rely at the hearing
from 'On or before 17 July 2024' to 'On or before 31 July 6 August 2024".

The applicant's solicitor has indicated that the applicant, Simon Reid, would not
oppose this request.

The respondent otherwise notes that no SFIC or further evidence has been filed
in Vida or FFG's applications (noting they were due to be filed by 10 July 2024).
We have been following up the applicant's solicitor and have been told
yesterday that they "intend to update the Tribunal shortly."

In those circumstances, the respondent has not yet prepared for filing a SFIC or
further evidence with respect to Vida Reid's and FFG's applications.

Given the applicants’ non-compliance with the Tribunal directions, the
respondent is also amenable to a further Directions hearing for the parties to be
heard on compliance with the current directions and progression of the 3
applications.
