NATIONAL DISABILITY INSURANCE SCHEME ACT 2013 (CTH)
SECTION 73ZN BANNING ORDER

  

|, Sian Leathem, as delegate of the Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission
under the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (the NDIS Act), make this banning order
with respect to Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd.

| make the banning order under section 73ZN(1)(a) of the NDIS Act on the basis that the registration
of Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd as a registered NDIS provider was revoked on 20 December 2022.

Pursuant to section 73ZN(1)(a) of the NDIS Act, | prohibit Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd from
providing supports or services, directly or indirectly, to NDIS-funded participants for a period of
three (3) years from the date that this order takes effect.

My reasons for making this order are set out in the enclosed statement of reasons.
This order varies the banning order made on 20 December 2022.

This order takes effect from 20 December 2022 until 12:00am on 21 December 2025 (AWST).
