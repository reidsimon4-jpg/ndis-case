Engaging in conduct that breaches a banning order is a contravention of section 73ZN(10) of the
NDIS Act. A contravention of a banning order may attract a civil penalty of up to 1,000 penalty units,
however for a corporation this may be up to 5 times the amount of the maximum penalty,
amounting to $1,375,000.
