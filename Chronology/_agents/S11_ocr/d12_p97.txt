(m) Our job is not case management as we are support coordinators. We will only connect you

(n) We ask that you have realistic expectations and work with us to achieve a good outcome.

(0) Provide the Services in a manner consistent with all relevant laws, including the National
Disability insurance Scheme Act 2013, and the Australian Consumer Law; and (I) Provide
you with regular statements of the Services we deliver to you.

(p) We will not provide transport assistance, pick or drop you off to places as that is outside of
our role. We will only meet you where you have asked us to and then leave. Most of our
work will be done over the phone as we are here to connect you to supports. We are not
support workers.

(q) From time to time as required we may have to work with other organisations for our
complex case clients and as such we will also charge the costs incurred for sourcing
additional supports.

2. Your obligations

 

You agree that, during the duration of this Services Agreement, you will:
(a) Inform us of how you would like your Services to be provided by us to meet your needs;
(b) Always treat FFG Support Coordinators with courtesy and respect.

(c) Communicate with FFG Support Coordinator if you have any concerns about the Services
being provided in accordance with our procedure for the reporting of complaints.

(d) Comply with all of the terms of this Services Agreement; and

(e) Immediately inform FFG Support Coordinator if your NDIS plan is suspended or replaced
by a new NDIS plan or if you stop being a participant in the NDIS.

(f) Not ask us to provide supports outside of our role.
