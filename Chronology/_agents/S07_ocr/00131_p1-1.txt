Opportunity to make submissions about a proposed banning order under section 73ZN of the
National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (Cth)

1am a delegate of the Commissioner for the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission
(the Commission).

Section 73ZN of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (Cth) (the NDIS Act) gives me, as
the Commissioner's delegate, the power to make an order known as a banning order. A banning
order can prohibit or restrict specified activities by an NDIS provider.

Proposed Banning Order

| have formed the view that it may be appropriate in all the circumstances to make a banning order
prohibiting Malibuhouse Pty Ltd for a period of ten (10) years from being involved in the provision of
supports or services to people with disability.

The basis for this view is that | reasonably believe that Malibuhouse Pty Ltd is not suitable to be
involved in providing supports or services to people with disability. | am therefore empowered to
make a banning order against Malibuhouse Pty Ltd under section 73ZN(2A) of the NDIS Act.

The purpose of this letter is to inform Malibuhouse Pty Ltd of relevant facts and the matters that are
of concern to me and to give you the opportunity to make submissions about this before | make a
final decision,
