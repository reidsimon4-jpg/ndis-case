Section 73ZN of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (Cth) (the NDIS Act) gives me, as
the Commissioner's delegate, the power to make an order known as a banning order. A banning
order can prohibit or restrict specified activities by an NDIS provider.

Proposed banning order

| have formed the view that it may be appropriate in all the circumstances to make a banning order
prohibiting Infinity Living Options Pty Ltd for a period of ten (10) years from providing supports or
services to people with disability.

The basis for this view is that | reasonably believe that Infinity Living Options Pty Ltd is not suitable
to be involved in providing supports or services to people with disability. | am therefore empowered
to make a banning order against Infinity Living Options Pty Ltd under section 73ZN(1)(b)(iii) of the
NDIS Act.

The purpose of this letter is to inform Infinity Living Options Pty Ltd of relevant facts and the matters
that are of concern to me and to give Infinity Living Options Pty Ltd the opportunity to make
submissions about this before | make a final decision.
