NATIONAL DISABILITY INSURANCE SCHEME ACT 2013 (CTH)
SECTION 73ZN BANNING ORDER

|, Sian Leathem, as delegate of the Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission
under the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (the NDIS Act), make this banning order
with respect to Simon Reid of 96 Amethyst Crescent, Mount Richon, WA 6112.

| make the order believing on reasonable grounds that Simon Reid is not suitable to be involved in the
provision of supports or services to people with disability.

Pursuant to section 73ZN(2)(a)(iii) of the NDIS Act, | prohibit Simon Reid from having any
involvement with or in the NDIS sector, in any capacity, for a period of five (5) years. This includes
Simon Reid being prohibited from:

a) providing disability supports and services, directly or indirectly, to NDIS-funded participants;
b) providing labour hire workers to support NDIS providers; or
c) providing support, funding, guidance or assistance of any form to an NDIS provider.

My reasons for making this order are set out in the enclosed statement of reasons.

This order, which varies the banning order made on 21 December 2022, takes effect from 12.00am
on 22 April 2023 until 12.00am on 22 December 2027 (AWST).

The original banning order made on 21 December 2022 remains effective from that date to the date
of this variation.

Sian Leathem
Complaints Commissioner
Delegate of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commissioner
