8. Payment for the Services

 

The Services provided by FFG must be paid to FFG by:
(a) NDIA
(b) the Participant, if your NDIS plan is self-managed; or (c) a nominated plan
manager if your NDIS plan is managed.
[delete/cross out whichever is not applicable].
If you or your nominated plan manager are responsible for payment of the Services:
(a) FFG will issue fortnightly invoices for the Services; and
(b) Payment for the Services must be made within 14 days of the date of each invoice.

You may also request an activity statement at any time.
Where the Services are paid by the NDIA:

(a) FFG will issue weekly invoices to the NDIA.

(b) The pricing in your plan is inclusive of the time required for all activities related to the
Service including assessment, planning and preparation, liaison, report writing, training,
direct contact (e.g. face to face, phone, teleconference, skype, email) and indicate the
potential hours for assessment, planning and intervention. For example, NDIS will pay for a
physiotherapist to write a report to NDIA, or for the time taken to liaise with other service
providers/health professional regarding your care/services or research on your behalf.

(c) If the NDIA change the price payable to service providers for the Services:
a. your plan will be automatically adjusted to account for the increase in prices; and

b. we may increase the prices we charge to you in accordance with any corresponding
increase in the prices payable to service providers by the NDIA.

(d) There can be a delay in between the expiry of this plan and the completion of a new service
agreement for your renewed plan. FFG will continue to provide Services in accordance with
this Service Agreement after the expiry date of your current plan to ensure continuity. If you
decide to use another service provider for your renewed plan, please notify FFG of this
immediately. You will need to ensure that FFG receive payment for the Services during this
period, in accordance with this Service Agreement.

(e) If you receive notification that the NDIA has, for whatever reason, withdrawn funding for the
Services, you must notify FFG within 7 days. If you do not notify FFG within 7 days, you
may be personally be liable for payment of any Services provided by FFG.
