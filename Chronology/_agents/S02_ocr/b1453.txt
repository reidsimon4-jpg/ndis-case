10. Changes to this Service Agreement

 

If changes to the Services, or their delivery, are required, FFG and the Participant agree to discuss
and review this Service Agreement.

From time to time, FFG can make alterations to this Service Agreement due to changes in the
policy and procedure of the NDIA.

FFG and the Participant agree that any changes to this Service Agreement will be in writing.

11. Ending this Service Agreement

 

Either Party may terminate this Service Agreement by giving the other Party one month’s notice in
writing.

12. Feedback, Complaints and Disputes

 

Feedback, complaints and disputes The FFG welcomes feedback.
How to raise your concerns and complaints:

1. Tell your support staff or your case manager. This is often the quickest way to address and
resolve your concerns.

2. You can also raise your concerns and complaints with the Manager of the service.

3. FGG’s website https://forwardfocusedgroup.com.au also has a Feedback Form you can
complete. This form will be sent to the FFG’s Manager Policy, Planning & Development.

If you are not satisfied or do not want to talk to this person, you can contact the National Disability
Insurance Agency by calling 1800 800 110, visiting one of their offices in person, or visiting the
website ndis.gov.au for further information.

13. Goods and Services Tax (GST)

 

All prices are GST inclusive (if applicable).
For the purposes of GST legislation, you agree that:

The Services described in this Service Agreement are reasonable and necessary supports
specified in the statement of supports in the Participant's NDIS plan currently in effect under
section 37 of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013;

The Participant's NDIS plan is expected to remain in effect during the period the Services are
provided; and

The Participant/Participant’s representative will immediately notify the FFG if the Participant's
NDIS Plan is replaced or the Participant stops being a participant in the NDIS.
