15. Section 73ZN(9) sets out the various requirements surrounding any banning order made as
follows:

‘The Commissioner must:
(a) give the banning order to the person against whom the order is made; and
(b) include in the banning order a statement of reasons for the order; and

(c) if the banning order is against a person who is employed or otherwise engaged by an NDIS
provider or is a member of the key personnel of an NDIS provider—notify the NDIS provider of
the order as soon as possible.’

Procedural history of matter

16. On 17 November 2022, the original decision-maker issued Mr Reid with a notice of intention to
make a banning order against Mr Reid. In the letter, the original decision-maker invited Mr Reid
to make submissions in relation to the proposed banning order by no later than 5pm on 30
November 2022.

17. On 22 November 2022, Mr Monks wrote to the original decision-maker to confirm he
represented Mr Reid and to seek an extension of 28 days to provide submissions. Mr Monks
requested the original decision-maker provide a response in relation to the extension request
by 12pm on 25 November 2022.

18. The original decision-maker did not provide a response by the requested date.
19. On 30 November 2022, Mr Monks provided submissions on behalf of Mr Reid.

20. On 2 December 2022, the original decision-maker wrote to Mr Monks confirming receipt of the
submissions made on 30 November 2022 and granting an extension of time to make further
submissions. The new deadline for providing submissions was by 5pm on 14 December 2022.

21. On 7 December 2022, Mr Monks wrote to the original decision-maker informing him that Mr
Reid did not wish to make any further submissions beyond those already made on 30 November
2022.

22. On 21 December 2022, the original decision-maker made a banning order against Mr Reid,
prohibiting him from providing disability supports and services to NDIS-funded participants for a
period of 5 years, under section 73ZN(2)(a)(iii) of the NDIS Act, on the basis that he had
reasonable grounds to believe that Mr Reid was not suitable to provide supports or services to
people with disability. The banning order took effect from 5pm (AWST) on 21 December 2022.

23. On 25 January 2023, Mr Monks, on behalf of Mr Reid, requested an internal review of the
decision to make a banning order on 21 December 2022.

24. On 6 February 2023, the NDIS Commission emailed Mr Monks a letter of acknowledgement in
relation to the request for an internal review. The letter invited Mr Reid to make submissions to
