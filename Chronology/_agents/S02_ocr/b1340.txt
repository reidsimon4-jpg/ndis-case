f. On 19 October 2022, Mr Reid’s wife and business partner, Mrs Vida Reid, was arrested
and charged with several offences under sections 135.1(3) and 400.9(1) of the Criminal
Code (which is contained in a Schedule to the Criminal Code Act 1995 (Cth). Those
charges related to Mrs Reid allegedly defrauding the National Disability Insurance
Scheme (NDIS).

g. | acknowledge that the criminal charges Mrs Reid is facing, in and of themselves, are not
relevant to the suitability of Mr Reid to engage in the NDIS sector. However, some of
the evidence lodged in the criminal proceedings against Mrs Reid included information
that related to Mr Reid and was relevant to an assessment of his suitability.

h. In particular, the NDIA lodged a Statement of Material Facts (dated 19 October 2022) in
the criminal proceedings against Mrs Reid, which contained a number of allegations
that Mr Reid had engaged in fraudulent activity in the NDIS sector. Specifically, the
Statement of Material Facts relevantly provided that:

i. Mr Reid and Mrs Reid are joint signatories to a company bank account for
Hybrid, namely: ANZ Bank account numbered 016253 154173449 (the Hybrid
ANZ account).

ii. Zettai Pty Ltd (trading as Assist Plan Managers) (Assist) is a registered NDIS
provider that performs the services of a Plan Manager, and at various times,
Assist received invoices from Hybrid for services purportedly provided to
various NDIS participants. Assist would then make claims to the NDIA for those
services, receive funds from the NDIA, and then forward those funds to Hybrid.

iii. between 25 January 2022 and 10 May 2022, Assist submitted 20 separate
claims to the NDIA for services purportedly provided by Hybrid to an NDIS
participant, Ms Meghan Goodwin. The claims resulted in the payment of
$78,491.06 to Assist, who subsequently transferred those funds to the Hybrid
ANZ account for which Mr Reid is a joint signatory. The NDIA asserts that Mr
Reid, therefore, received money reasonably suspected of being the proceeds of
crime.

iv. Ms Goodwin has provided a witness statement that she did not receive the
services purportedly provided in the claims referred to above.

v. between 26 August 2022 and 21 September 2022, Assist submitted to the NDIA
11 separate reports of suspicious invoices it had received from FFG and Hybrid. |
acknowledge that Mr Reid was not the director of FFG during this period of
time. However, Mr Reid was the sole director of Hybrid during this period of
time. Accordingly, | have only taken into consideration this information to the
extent that it relates to Hybrid.
