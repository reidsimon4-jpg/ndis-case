30. Turning to the other material before the original decision-maker, | note the original decision-
maker had regard to numerous sworn statements provided to the NDIA by NDIS participants (or
their carers) and former employees of FFG and Hybrid. Some of those statements support a
finding that:

a. Hybrid submitted claims against the NDIS plans of participants, in circumstances where
it had not entered into service agreements with those participants (rather FFG was their
service provider). Such claiming occurred without the knowledge or consent of the
impacted participants; and

b. Hybrid submitted false claims against the NDIS plans of participants for supports or
services not delivered.

31. The following statements, in particular, provide examples of such conduct:

a. The statement of Gina Regali, dated 11 October 2022, which alleges that FFG was the
service provider for Ms Regali’s children, however, on 5 January 2022, Ms Regali
received an invoice (Invoice number: INV-0116) from Hybrid for supports and services
purportedly provided to her child (Mr Angelus Regali-Park). Ms Regali had not heard of
Hybrid and alleges the services claimed in the Hybrid invoice were not provided.

b. The statement of Hrimnir Benediktsson, dated 12 October 2022, which alleges that FFG
was the service provider for Mr Benediktsson, however, in August 2022, Mr
Benediktsson discovered Hybrid had invoiced him on 7 occasions (between 1 June 2022
and 24 August 2022) for supports and services purportedly provided to him (Invoice
numbers INV-0956, INV-0957, INV-0958, INV-0955, INV-1144, INV-1145 and INV-1146).
Mr Benediktsson had not heard of Hybrid and alleges he did not receive any of the
supports or services listed in the invoices.

c. The statement of Jessica Rose Smith, dated 14 October 2022, which alleges that FFG
was the service provider for Ms Smith’s child, Charlie Harrison, however, on 16 August
2022, Ms Smith discovered Hybrid had invoiced her on five separate occasions
(between 31 May 2022 and 10 August 2022) for supports and services purportedly
provided to her child (Invoice numbers INV-1468, INV-1070, INV-1073, INV-1074 and
INV-1075). Ms Smith had not heard of Hybrid and alleges the supports and services
claimed in the Hybrid invoices were not provided.
