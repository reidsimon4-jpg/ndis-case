Findings and Reasons

Consideration of material before original decision-maker

 

26. | have considered the material before the original decision-maker that the original decision was
based upon.

27. |am of the view that, at the time the original decision was made, there was sufficient
information available for the original decision-maker to form a reasonable belief that Mr Reid
was not suitable to be involved in the provision of supports or services to people with disability.

28. | have found that the original decision-maker arrived at the correct or preferable decision,
however, | disagree with:

a. some aspects of how the original decision-maker assessed and weighed the evidence
before him; and

b. some of the findings of fact reached by the original decision-maker as a result.

29. In my view, the evidence before the original decision-maker, when properly assessed and
weighed, enables only the following findings to be made:

a. Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd (Hybrid) (ABN 96 651 140 108) and Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd
(FFG) (ABN 98 643 878 639) are NDIS providers with whom Mr Reid is associated.

b. Hybrid was formerly an unregistered NDIS provider operating in Western Australia, until
a delegate of the NDIS Commissioner made a banning order on 21 December 2022
permanently prohibiting Hybrid from providing supports and services to NDIS-funded
participants.

c. Mr Reid has been the director of Hybrid since 17 February 2022 to present date.
Specifically, he was a co-director of Hybrid between 17 February 2022 and 21 August
2022 and the sole director of Hybrid from 22 August 2022.

d. FFG was formerly a registered NDIS provider operating in Western Australia, until a
delegate of the NDIS Commissioner revoked the registration of FFG, and made a
banning order prohibiting FFG from providing supports or services to NDIS-funded
participants for a period of 10 years, on 20 December 2022.

e. Mr Reid was a co-director of FFG from 28 August 2020 to 3 January 2021 and returned
to the role on 1 November 2022 and presently remains a co-director.
