Notice of internal review decision
Section 100 of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (NDIS Act)

Dear Mr Monks

Il refer to the email received by the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission (the NDIS Commission)
on 25 January 2023 requesting an internal review of the decision made on 21 December 2022 to
impose a banning order against your client, Mr Simon Reid, for a period of 5 years under section
73ZN(2)(a)(iii) of the NDIS Act (the original decision).

lam the delegate of the NDIS Commissioner for the purposes of section 100 of the NDIS Act, and am
authorised to make a decision on this internal review.

The internal review decision
| have decided to vary the original decision. This means the banning order made against Mr Reid
remains in place, however, it is changed or altered in some respect.

The variation | have decided to impose is to broaden the scope of the banning order to make it
explicit that Mr Reid is to have no involvement with or in the NDIS sector, in any capacity. This
includes Mr Reid being prohibited from:

a) providing disability supports and services, directly or indirectly, to NDIS-funded participants;
b) providing labour hire workers to support NDIS providers; or
c) providing support, funding, guidance or assistance of any form to an NDIS provider.

| enclose the banning order as varied, and the reasons for my review decision at Attachment A to
this notice.

Consequences of review decision
The original banning order made against Mr Reid is not disturbed and continues to have effect from
5pm on 21 December 2022 (AWST).
