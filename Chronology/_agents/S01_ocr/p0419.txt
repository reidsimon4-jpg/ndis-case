7. SECURITY DEPOSIT

7.1 A security Deposit may be requested to be paid by the Client on signing this
agreement. The Security Deposit is maintained in FFG’s Account.

7.2 FFG's Disability deserves the right to recover the unpaid rent from the Client’s
Security Deposit.

8. RENT INCREASE

8.1 FFG's Disability Services may increase the rent in March and September each
year to match the published disability pension adjustments from the
(Australian Government) Department of Human Services.

9. WATER SERVICES AND WATER USAGE COSTS

9.1. The rent is not inclusive of water services and water usage. The Client is
required to pay water consumption costs.

10. ELECTRICITY, GAS AND OTHER UTILITIES

10.1. The rent is not inclusive of the cost of electricity and gas consumption. The
Client is required to pay the consumption of electricity and gas.

11. PETS
11.1 Pets are not permitted to be kept on the premises unless approved by the
Manager.

11.2 If itis agreed that a pet may be kept at the premises, then a Pet
Agreement must be signed in conjunction with the Accommodation
Agreement.

12. SUBLETTING

12.1 Clients are not permitted to sublet the whole or any part of the property. It
should not be expected this permission will readily be given.

13. RIGHT OF CLIENT TO AFFIX AND REMOVE FIXTURES

13.1 The Client must not affix any fixture or make any renovation, alteration or
addition to the premises.

13.2 The Client may only affix any fixture or make any renovation, alteration or
addition to the premises with the Regional Managers written permission.

14, PROPERTY CONDITION REPORTS

14.1. A report on the condition of the property may be offered to a Client upon
Client's request.

14.2 If the Client disagrees with any information contained in the property
condition report, the Client must note his or her disagreement on a copy of
the property condition report and return this to the Manager within 7 days
