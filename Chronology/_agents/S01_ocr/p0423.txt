30. CLIENT LIABILITY

30.1 FFG’s Disability Services will, when allocating tenancy, take into
consideration the ability of a Client to understand the law of liability. If the
Client has, or develops, behavioural issues that could impact on their ability
to remain in the tenancy, FFG’s Disability Services will negotiate with the
Client, their family, Guardian or advocates a suitable re-location.

31. CONFIDENTIALITY

31.1 Every employee and volunteer of FFG’s Disability Services is required to sign
a declaration of Confidentiality and Privacy prior to commencing their
employment or involvement with FFG’s Disability Services.

32. CLIENTS RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

32.1 FFG's Disability Services acknowledges the Clients’ right to:

a) fair and non-discriminatory treatment;
b) be treated with respect;

c) have access to safe and secure housing;

d) be consulted on housing needs and preferences;
e) the maintenance of confidentiality of personal information provided;
f) be consulted on substantial changes in the way the tenancy is managed;

g) security of tenure within the terms of the tenancy agreement and funding
guidelines;

h) complain and appeal against decisions made within FFG’s Disability
Services;

i) use of an advocate at any time;

j) have access to their own information held by FFG’s Disability Services in
any form.
