28. ENDING THE FIXED-TERM AGREEMENT

28.1 This is a fixed-term agreement which will end as described in the Schedule;
or by agreement in writing between FFG’s Disability Services and the
Client; or if either FFG’s Disability Services or Client does not want to renew
the agreement, by giving written notice of termination. The notice must be
given to the other party at least 30 days prior to the date on which vacant
possession of the premises is to be delivered to FFG’s Disability Services.
The notice may be given at any time up until the end of the fixed term but
cannot take effect until the term ends.
