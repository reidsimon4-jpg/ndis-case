15. RIGHT TO OCCUPY THE PREMISES

15.1 The Client has the right to exclusive occupation of the apartment (as
described the Schedule) in the premises and quiet enjoyment of the
Residential premises during the tenancy.

16. COPY OF AGREEMENT

16.1 FFG's Disability Services must give the Client/representative a copy of this
agreement when this agreement is signed by both the
Client/representative and FFG's Disability Services.

17. USE OF THE PREMISES BY CLIENT
17.1 The Client must:
a) use the premises as a place of residence; and
b) not use or allow the premises to be used for any illegal purpose; and

c) not cause or permit a nuisance; and not intentionally or negligently
cause or permit damage to the Residential premises; and

d) advise the property manager as soon as practicable, if any damage
occurs; and

e) keep the premises in a reasonable state of cleanliness; and

f) not cause or allow to be caused injury to the representative of FFG's
Disability Services, Co-Clients or any person lawfully on adjacent
premises; and not allow anyone who is lawfully at the premises to
breach the terms of this agreement.

17.2 The Client is responsible for the conduct or omission of any person lawfully
on the premises that results in a breach of the agreement.

19 SMOKING

tenancy, if the Client breach this clause.
