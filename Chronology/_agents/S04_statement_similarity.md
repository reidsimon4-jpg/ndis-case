## 9. Assessment: templated or led? (honest reading of the data above)

**Short answer.** The text shows the statements were drafted in a common investigator-built format, and several groups are drafted from near-identical investigator templates. It does NOT, on this analysis alone, show that witnesses were fed a common false narrative. The data supports a "templated structure / investigator-drafted" finding; a "led as to substance" finding needs the drafts, interview notes and recordings (FOI / disclosure request) to test.

**What is expected and neutral.**
- Every statement uses the one Commonwealth form (heading block, para 1 "This statement made by me accurately sets out the evidence that I would be prepared, if necessary, to give in court as a witness", paras 2-3 identity/address, closing declaration). That is boilerplate and was excluded from the scores.
- Annexed FFG service agreements/invoices are shared documents, not statement text; also excluded.
- Witnesses describing the same provider (FFG/Hive), the same director (Vida Reid) and the same plan-manager process will share some wording naturally.

**What points to investigator-driven drafting (observed).**
1. **Taker-specific templates (strongest).** Christine Bruckner's 7 statements (Wilson, Jerusha Jetoh, Monbo Jetoh, Cimafranca, Hammond, Harrison, Lombard; 12-20 Oct) follow the same paragraph order: age -> access request date and diagnosing doctor -> acceptance date and NDIS number -> first plan dates and dollar value -> current plan dates and dollar value -> the same sentence "My understanding of the NDIS is to provide funding to pay for services in assisting [me/X] with everyday living" -> "On [date] October 2022, Christine Bruckner, an investigator from the NDIS contacted me to ask about my claims for the NDIS. We had a brief conversation over the phone about these claims" -> "[she] showed me a list of claims which were submitted by FFG ... I can confirm I have never received these services from FFG". Plan values quoted to the cent (e.g. $285,015.23; $189,544.11) are the kind of figures held in NDIA records, which suggests the plan-history paragraphs were populated from NDIA data rather than witness recall (inference, not stated in the source). Mean shared 8-grams within this group: 56.7 (vs 1.4 for pairs with different takers; 0.15% vs 1.67% mean containment).
2. **Claim-by-claim commentary structure.** Tim Jarratt's group (Odi, Shapiera, Trantham, Abau Haidar): repeated paragraph opening "Tim Jarratt has told me that FFG/HH have claimed/charged my NDIS plan for [item]" followed by the witness's comment. Fiona Lane's group (Cutler, Yavarzadeh-Tehrani, Craig): "Investigator Lane has asked me to comment on ...". Brenda Ryan's (Crane, Goodwin, Clulow): "I had a conversation with NDIS investigator Brenda Ryan ... making enquiries into the claiming that had occurred against my plan". This is ordinary fraud-interview practice (investigator puts claims data to witness) but it means the claim content originates with the investigator, and the witness response is framed around it.
3. **Identical odd phrasing across takers.** "As Reid is an unregistered provider I am aware the invoices for her services are put though [sic] a plan management agency" appears in four statements (Crane, Goodwin, Clulow - Ryan; Abau Haidar - Jarratt), and the variant address line "known to the National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA)" appears in the same four plus Burke (Graydon). The shared typo suggests a common drafting source (a template or copy-forward between investigators), not independent recall. Abau Haidar (taken by Jarratt) carries the Ryan-group wording, indicating drafting text circulated between investigators.
4. **Jetoh couple.** The statements of Monbo and Jerusha Jetoh were both taken by Bruckner at 1:15pm on 13 Oct 2022 at "an address in Perth"; ~35% of the shorter statement's 8-grams are shared, including 95-word and 72-word identical passages (plan values, "my son and I have never received these services from FFG"). Consistent with one joint interview/one drafted narrative, with the spouse's pronouns swapped. Visible differences are mainly pronouns; Monbo's is longer (about 2,270 vs 1,359 body words; 7 vs 5 pages) and has extra narrative. Amber Wilson (Bruckner, 12 Oct, 1:15pm "an address in Perth") has the same 1:15pm slot.
5. **Volume/tempo.** 35 statements: 8 on 11 Oct, 6 each on 12, 13 and 14 Oct, 1 on 15, 2 on 17, 4 on 18, 1 on 20 Oct 2022 (plus Cassandra Burke on 16 Feb 2023, Graydon). Nine named takers (plus one blank). Locations range from Perth metro to Mandurah, Dalyellup and Jurien Bay. 11 Oct: 8 statements across 5 takers.
6. **Unfilled template.** Pieter Rossouw's jurat (11 Oct, 19:00 "Perth") reads "Insert name of Investigator (insert location)" - the taker line was never completed in the form as OCR'd (a handwritten name is not legible in the OCR). Terri Roberts (Baldivis, 7:40pm, Sian Harsley) and Leah Norman (Baldivis, 7:40pm, Gerard Righetti) have the identical time and place on 11 Oct - two investigators at one location at the same minute (or "about" time copied between forms).
7. **Annexure timing.** Paul Gilbert's statement is timed 1:00pm 13 Oct (Rockingham, Amanda Laing) but his exhibit emails were forwarded to Laing at 4:16pm that day; Abau Haidar's statement is timed 12pm on 14 Oct (Thornlie, Jarratt) but annexure BH-01 is an email to Jarratt sent 2:40pm that day. Benign explanation: exhibits supplied after the interview and attached afterwards; but the "I now produce exhibit" wording implies they were in hand at signing. Flag, don't over-read.

**What does NOT indicate templating.**
- The former-staff / insider group show virtually no verbatim overlap with anyone: Terri Roberts 11 Oct (Sian Harsley) 1.4% shared; Janine Hall 0.3%; Danica Hall 0.4%; Karen Marasigan 0.3%; Leah Norman 1.2%; Liz Odi 1.0%. Their narratives are in their own idiom; zero overlap between the two Roberts statements except form text. No evidence in the text of a common script for the "complaint" witnesses (Roberts / Hall / Clulow / Nuttall circle).
- 416 of 595 pairs share zero non-boilerplate 8-grams. Outside the Bruckner group, the Ryan trio, and the Jetoh couple, overlap is incidental (shared service names, "the Hive", "plan manager").
- Participant witnesses reciting that they never received services is the content of the allegation, so some repetition of "I never received these services from FFG" is expected from true accounts as well as led ones.

**Caveats.** OCR errors depress exact-match scores (a single OCR error breaks every 8-gram that spans it), so the figures are minimums for verbatim overlap; paraphrase-level copying is not measured; the Janine Hall OCR file omits statement pages; Cassandra Burke is a Feb 2023 statement by a different taker (Bruce Graydon) and is shown for completeness; takers were read from the OCR'd jurat block and may be mis-OCR'd (James/Fiona Lane are distinguished only by the OCR). Next evidence to request: investigators' notes and drafts, statement-version metadata, interview recordings, the NDIA plan-data extracts used to populate plan histories.
