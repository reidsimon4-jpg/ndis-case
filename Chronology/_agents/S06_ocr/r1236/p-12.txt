METHODOLOGY

39. The following methodology was adopted to (1) Calculate the value of the alleged
fraudulent Successful Payment Request Claims submitted by FORWARD FOCUSED
GROUP PTY LTD, HYBRID HIVE PTY LTD and INFINITY LIVING OPTIONS PTY
LTD; and (2) Reconstruct and consolidate the bank accounts and trace the credit
transaction (deposits) of NDIS funds in relation to the fraudulent Successful Payment
Request claims identified in step (1). This was achieved by the following:

a) Extract from the “Successful Payment Requests” report data exhibited in the
witness statement of James WIDLAKE (marked as “VRJ/JW01”, “VRJ/JW02”,
“VRJ/JW03”) (Annexure A Items 39 — 42) the individual Payment Request Claims
in relation to each participant that has been identified as having alleged fraudulent
claims submitted against their NDIS plans by FORWARD FOCUSED GROUP
PTY LTD, HYBRID HIVE PTY LTD or INFINITY LIVING OPTIONS PTY LTD.

b) Review witness statements and other documentation obtained by investigators in
relation to each identified participant. The witness statements provide evidence
that has been relied upon to calculate the value of alleged fraudulent Payment
Request Claims.

c) Based on the information in the witness statements, identify each Payment
Request Claim that is allegedly fraudulent and calculate the total alleged fraud
for each identified participant (Annexure B.1 to B.26).

d) Provide a summary of the total alleged fraudulent amounts claimed and received
by FORWARD FOCUSED GROUP PTY LTD, HYBRID HIVE PTY LTD, THE
HIVE PTY LTD and INFINITY LIVING OPTIONS PTY LTD (Annexure B).

e) Reconstruct the transactions conducted on the bank accounts held in the name
of FORWARD FOCUSED GROUP PTY LTD (Annexure C).

f) Reconstruct the transactions conducted on the bank accounts held in the name
of HYBRID HIVE PTY LTD (Annexure D).
