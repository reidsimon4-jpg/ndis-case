The Tribunal DIRECTS that:

1. Application 2023/3361 of Vida Reid be adjourned pending the termination or
resolution of criminal proceedings against her relating to matters the subject of
the decision under review.

referred to in paragraph 1.

Tribunal and serve on the Respondent any further submissions and evidence
upon which he intends to rely in response to the Respondent's application that
Application 2023/3363 of Simon Reid be adjourned or stayed pending the
termination or resolution of criminal proceedings against Vida Reid referred to in
paragraph 1.

4. By no later than 28 February 2024, the Respondent is to file with the Tribunal
and serve on the Applicant Simon Reid any further submissions and evidence
upon which she intends to rely in reply in support of her application for an
adjournment or stay of proceedings in Applicant Simon Reid’s Application for
review.
