            SECTION 56 OF THE NATIONAL DISABILITY INSURANCE SCHEME ACT 2013 (NDIS Act)

Pursuant to section 55A(1) of the NDIS Act I, Philip Halton, delegate of the Commissioner of the NDIS
Quality and Safeguards Commission (the Commission), require you to:
         give the information contained in Schedule 1 of this notice in writing; and
         produce the documents described in Schedule 2 of this notice
to the Commissioner by no later than 5:00pm on Monday 6 February 2023.
Basis for this notice
I give this notice in accordance with section 56 of the NDIS Act. I require you to give information and
produce documents that I reasonably believe you have, or have custody or control of, that may be relevant
to one or more of the matters listed in section 55A(2) of the NDIS Act. In respect of this notice, the matters
are:

         whether Ms Vida Reid (Ms Reid), a person subject of a banning order made under section 73ZN of
          the NDIS Act, is providing or has provided supports or services in contravention of the order
          (section 55A(2)(e));
         whether Mr Simon Reid (Mr Reid), a person subject of a banning order made under section 73ZN
          of the NDIS Act, is providing or has provided supports or services in contravention of the order
          (section 55A(2)(e));
         whether Dream Big Employment Pty Ltd (Dream Big), as an NDIS provider, is complying or has
          complied with the requirements of the NDIS Code of Conduct, particularly sections 6(a)1 and 6(d)
          2
           , in the provision of supports and services to Ms Annette Ogden (Ms Ogden) and other NDIS
          participants, as required by section 73V of the NDIS Act (section 55A(2)(d));
         whether Ms Tori Fitton (Ms Fitton), as a person employed by a NDIS provider (Dream Big),
          complied with the requirements of the NDIS Code of Conduct, particularly sections 6(a) and 6(d),
          in the provision of supports and services to Ms Ogden, as required by section 73V of the NDIS Act
          (section 55A(2)(d)); and
         the functions of the Commissioner with respect to monitoring, compliance and enforcement of
          Dream Big’s, Ms Reid’s, Mr Reid’s and Ms Fitton’s obligations under the NDIS Act (section
          55A(2)(f)).

A copy of the relevant legislative provisions is contained in Schedule 3 to this notice.
