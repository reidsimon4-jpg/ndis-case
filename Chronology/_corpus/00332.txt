We write in relation to Mr Reid’s addendum reply of 19 March 2026, and the Commission’s
supplementary submissions of the same date which preceded it.

The Commission’s supplementary submissions proposed that an updated tender bundle
would be served by 10 April 2026.

Mr Reid’s addendum reply sought directions reflecting that date, together with a
supplementary disclosure statement and an express statement as to whether any Simon-
specific decision brief, executive brief, delegate submission, minute, or equivalent record
exists (paragraphs 14(a) to (d)).

The 10 April 2026 date has passed. The updated bundle has not been received. No
supplementary disclosure statement has been provided. The question whether a Simon-
specific decision brief exists remains unanswered.

Could you please advise whether the non-delivery is the result of an administrative
oversight, or whether there is a particular reason for the delay. If the latter, we would be
grateful to understand what it is and when service can be expected.

Mr Reid’s addendum reply set out why the material is significant.

Much of his case turns on whether the Commission conducted an individualised assessment
of his personal suitability to provide supports or services to people with disability, as s
73ZN(2)(a)(iii) of the NDIS Act requires.

It is Mr Reid’s hope that, when produced, the documents will contain a comprehensive
individualised assessment of his personal suitability, separate and distinct from the
assessment of Mrs Vida Reid and of Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd.

To meet the statutory threshold, such an assessment would need to:

1. identify Mr Reid’s personal conduct rather than the conduct of related persons or
companies attributed to him through key-personnel principles;
2. consider the matters prescribed by the NDIS Rules for the purposes of s 73ZN(2)(a)(iii);
3. engage with the specific time periods during which Mr Reid held directorships of each
relevant entity, as established by the ASIC records the Commission itself has tendered;
4. engage substantively with the submissions filed on Mr Reid’s behalf by Tom & Co Legal
on 30 November 2022;
5. consider the proportionality of the specific period of banning as it applies to Mr Reid
individually; and
6. identify the evidentiary basis supporting the s 73ZN(2)(a)(iii) finding of personal
unsuitability independently of the prosecution material lodged against Mrs Reid.

Point 6 has particular weight in light of the Commonwealth Director of Public Prosecutions’
discontinuance of all four charges against Mrs Reid for no evidence in February 2024.

Mr Reid himself was not charged at any stage. The Statement of Material Facts lodged in
support of the charges against Mrs Reid was material the decision-makers expressly relied
upon (paragraph 11(f)(v) and paragraph 29(h) of the internal review statement of reasons).

An individualised assessment of the kind described above is the natural place for the
Commission to identify how its findings against Mr Reid are sustained independently of that
material.
