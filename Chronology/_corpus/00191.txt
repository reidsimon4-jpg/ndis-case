The NDIS and this Service Agreement
                       This Service Agreement is made for the purpose of providing support
                        services (Supports or Services) under the Participant’s National Disability
                        Insurance Scheme (NDIS) Plan.
                          The Parties agree that this Service Agreement is made in the context of
the NDIS, which is a scheme that aims to:
    •   Support the independence and social and economic participation of people with a disability;
        and
    •   Enable people with a disability to exercise their choice and control in pursuit if their goals,
        and the planning and delivery of their supports.
The terms and conditions contained in this Service Agreement (as varied from time to time in
writing) will apply to any Services provided by FFG.


Glossary of Terms
FFG means the provider of Services under this agreement (otherwise known as we or us).
NDIA means the National Disability Insurance Agency.
Service Agreement means this agreement and any attached documents or schedules.
You means a person who is a receiver of Services or a person such as a family member or friend
who has authority to act on behalf of the service recipient under the National Disability Insurance
Scheme, as the context requires.


1. FFG’s responsibilities


    We will use our best efforts to:
    (a) Provide the Services described in clause 4 of this Service Agreement, and/or the attached
        Schedule of Services, as varied from time to time;
    (b) Plan with you for the provision of the Services as outlined in your NDIS plan;
    (c) Consult with you about how Services will be provided and times for these Services;
    (d) Review the provision of your Services quarterly;
    (e) Communicate any changes, and discuss with you any barriers, to delivering any Supports
        or Services as per the plan, including those outside of our control;
    (f) Provide you with information about our procedure for the reporting and management of
        complaints;
    (g) Provide you with details of our Cancellation Procedure – See Point 6.

   (h) Provide a minimum of 24 hours’ notice where possible, when we have to change a
       scheduled appointment to provide the Services;
   (i)   Comply with all rules and regulations regarding the privacy of your personal information;
   (j)   Keep accurate records on the Services provided to you;
   (k) We only work 9 am to 5 pm Monday to Friday. We will not respond to sms or calls after or
       before these hours.
   (l)   We can only link you to supports that fall into the guidelines of the NDIS act 2013 of
         reasonable and necessary criteria.
   (m) Our job is not case management as we are support coordinators. We will only connect you
       to the supports you ask us to and link you to them.
   (n) We ask that you have realistic expectations and work with us to achieve a good outcome.
   (o) Provide the Services in a manner consistent with all relevant laws, including the National
       Disability insurance Scheme Act 2013, and the Australian Consumer Law; and (l) Provide
       you with regular statements of the Services we deliver to you.
   (p) We will not provide transport assistance, pick or drop you off to places as that is outside of
       our role. We will only meet you where you have asked us to and then leave. Most of our
       work will be done over the phone as we are here to connect you to supports. We are not
       support workers.
   (q) From time to time as required we may have to work with other organisations for our
       complex case clients and as such we will also charge the costs incurred for sourcing
       additional supports.


2. Your obligations


                           You agree that, during the duration of this Services Agreement, you will:
   (a) Inform us of how you would like your Services to be provided by us to meet your needs; (b)
   Always treat FFG Support Coordinators with courtesy and respect.
   (c) Communicate with FFG Support Coordinator if you have any concerns about the Services
       being provided in accordance with our procedure for the reporting of complaints.
   (d) Comply with all of the terms of this Services Agreement; and
   (e) Immediately inform FFG Support Coordinator if your NDIS plan is suspended or replaced
       by a new NDIS plan or if you stop being a participant in the NDIS.
   (f) Not ask us to provide supports outside of our role.

12. Feedback, Complaints and Disputes


Feedback, complaints and disputes The FFG welcomes feedback.
How to raise your concerns and complaints:
1. Tell your support staff or your case manager. This is often the quickest way to address and
   resolve your concerns.
2. You can also raise your concerns and complaints with the Manager of the service.
3. FGG’s website https://forwardfocusedgroup.com.au also has a Feedback Form you can
   complete. This form will be sent to the FFG’s Manager Policy, Planning & Development.
If you are not satisfied or do not want to talk to this person, you can contact the National Disability
Insurance Agency by calling 1800 800 110, visiting one of their offices in person, or visiting the
website ndis.gov.au for further information.
