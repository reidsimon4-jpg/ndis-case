## 3. How to cite (and how not to)

1. **Cite judgment [96]–[97] pp.37–38** for protective character / *Briginshaw* not required / comfortable satisfaction / PF “cured” on tested independent evidence.  
2. **Do not** cite footnote 97 (only `(1938) 60 CLR 336`) as the holding.  
3. **Do not** treat *Abdullah* as a *Briginshaw* shield for applicants.  
4. **Lead *Sunflower*** on facial invalidity of the varied Reid wording; use *Abdullah* only defensively (method + distinguish).  
5. Put IR attribution discard + **$1.485m minimal weight** in the distinguish box every time *Abdullah* is mentioned.
