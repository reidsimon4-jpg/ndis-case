We write further to Vida Reid’s submissions in reply dated 23 September 2025.

By way of background, Mrs Reid sought in her original submissions, lodged with the
Tribunal on 16 August 2025, to stay her application (and that of Forward Focussed
Group [FFG]), seeking a review of the Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and
Safeguards Commission’s decision to affirming the issue of a Banning Order under
section 73ZN(2) of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 made on 20
December 2022.

Simon Reid lodged his reply to these submissions on 9 September 2025 and the
Respondent on 17 September 2025.

In her reply to these submissions (dated 23 September 2025), Mrs Reid now seeks a
stay of the Commissioner’s decision to issue a Banning Order made on 20 December
2022 (pages 1 and 17). Prior to these submissions, Mrs Reid’s request for a stay has
been limited to her ART application and as such, this is a new application for a stay.
Accordingly, in the event that it is Mrs Reid’s intention to seek a stay of the
Commissioner’s decision (rather than the ART application as previously submitted),
we would be grateful if this could be confirmed as a matter of urgency.

Further, in the event that Mrs Reid confirms that it is her intention to seek a stay of
both the ART application and the Commissioner’s decision, the Respondent
respectfully requests additional directions from the Tribunal as follows:

1. Mr Simon Reid and the Respondent to have an additional period of 14 days to
   make any further submissions in response to Mrs Reid’s reply submissions dated
   23 September 2025 to stay the Commissioner’s decision dated 21 April 2023
   affirming the decision to issue a Banning Order on 20 December 2020.
2. Mrs Reid to have a period of 7 days following receipt of any further submissions
   as directed by (1) to reply to those submissions.

This correspondence has been copied to both Mrs and Mr Reid.
