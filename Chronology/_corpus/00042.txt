The election this schedule forces: (A) if there is no investigation and no maintained intention to re-charge, the Commonwealth can say so in writing - the criminal limb closes, and the protective rationale for the ongoing administrative orders and the non-disclosure claims collapses; or (B) if the investigation or intention is live, the 11 March 2025 assurance was empty as given, Vida Reid’s preserved jeopardy is confirmed on the Commonwealth’s own records, and her matters cannot fairly be heard jointly; or (C) silence - which is itself a fact the Tribunal can be asked to weigh. Each limb advances the applicants’ position; no limb assists the Respondent.
