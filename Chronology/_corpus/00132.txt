1. The situation, in three sentences
The ART matter is on foot but reaches only the NDIS Commission; the conduct that actually drove this — the “fraud” framing, the coordinated provider exit, the installation of competitors, the payment freeze — was the NDIA’s, through officers Nathan Halls and Barbara Lundgren. The AGS litigates the Commission’s banning orders as a shield to keep the NDIA’s conduct off-stage. This action pack opens three coordinated fronts to pull the NDIA, Halls and Lundgren into the picture — each on its own timeline.

3. Why the timing differs
Package 1 now. FOI runs a 30-day clock and 12–24-month review queue — value is starting it today; it gates nothing. The preservation notice cannot wait either: log rotation would destroy the custodians’ records while the Tribunal deliberates.
Package 2 held. Issuing summonses while the Deputy President has two decisions reserved risks antagonising the bench and a stay of the summonses; and the s 70 (LPP) decision will define what is privileged, which directly shapes summons scope. Hold until both land, then move.
Package 3 soon. The federal case is the only forum that makes the NDIA a respondent and exposes Halls and Lundgren personally. It is WIP because counsel must settle it — but lodge soon: the misfeasance limitation (≈6 years from the 2022 conduct) is finite and the Respondent is rewarded by delay.
