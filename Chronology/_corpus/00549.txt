                                     RESPONDENT’S SUBMISSIONS IN REPLY

1.     These submissions have been prepared in accordance with the orders of Deputy
       President Mischin dated 17 October 2023 and in response to the Applicants’
       submissions (AS) dated 16 November 2023.
Observations

2.     We acknowledge paragraph 2 of the AS, in particular, that:

            a) Ms Vida Reid does not oppose the application of a stay in relation to her AAT
               application (2023/3361) and,

            b) at paragraph 19(b), that “the Applicants largely agreed with the Commissioner’s
               application for section 35 orders. They will consent to appropriate orders for a
               private hearings, non-publication and non-disclosure.” Noting if the stay
               applications are granted that application need not be determined at this time.

3.     The Respondent otherwise refutes the allegation (paragraph 16 of the AS) that she
       has repeatedly breached Tribunal orders and delayed in requesting that these matters
       be stayed. The Respondent has, at all times, engaged with the Applicants’
       representatives with a view to progressing the applications in an expeditious and
       efficient manner.

Submissions in response

4.     In response to paragraph 9 of the AS, and the submission that “it cannot be said
       similar proceedings against Mr Reid are ‘on the cards’ or a ‘reasonable possibility’ in
       sense referred to by Robson J in Re AWB Limited [2008] VSC 473”, the Respondent
       submits that delay itself does not negate the “reasonable possibility” of charges being
       brought.

5.     The Respondent draws attention to T8 of Ms Vida Reid’s Tribunal bundle, (“Tribunal
       documents”), a document entitled “Statement of material facts pursuant to section 35

      OVERVIEW

      1. On 19 October 2020, the National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA) approved Forward
         Focused Group Pty Ltd, trading as Forward Focused (FFG), to provide services to
         National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) participants in Western Australia. Mrs Vida
         REID (V REID) is the Director, shareholder and joint signatory to the company bank
         accounts. Mr Simon REID (S REID) is a shareholder, joint signatory to the company bank
         accounts and former Director.

      2. Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd (HYBRID) is an unregistered provider of services to NDIS participants
         in Western Australia. The sole Director of HYBRID is V REID. The sole shareholder of
         HYBRID is The HIVE Pty Ltd (HIVE). The sole Director of HIVE is S REID. The
         shareholders of HIVE are V REID and S REID. The joint signatories for the HYBRID bank
         account are V REID and S REID.

      3. It is alleged that, between 26 November 2021 and 22 June 2020, V REID and S REID
         caused 81 false ‘payment requests’ to be submitted against the funding of two disabled
         persons participating in the NDIS, with the intention of dishonestly causing a loss to the
         Commonwealth.

      4. As a result of these false claims, V REID and S REID received money reasonably
         suspected of being proceeds of crime to the sum of $247,484.78.

6.   In light of this overview the Respondent submits that similar proceedings against Mr
     Reid are ‘on the cards’ or a ‘reasonable possibility’ as referred to by Robson J in Re
     AWB Limited [2008] VSC 473.

7.   In response to paragraph 13 of the AS, where it is stated “there is also no evidence
     before the AAT as to when any criminal trial concerning Ms Reid and FFG may occur”,
     as Ms Reid is represented in her criminal prosecution by the same solicitor that is
     representing her (and Mr Reid and FFG) in these AAT applications, the Applicants
     must be aware that the criminal proceedings against Ms Vida Reid have been delayed
     as a result of a claim of legal professional privilege over electronic information that
     they themselves have lodged. Further, that the Court directed the parties to resolve
     this dispute by consent before Christmas and that the matter be listed for mention in
     February 2024.

8.   It is the Respondent’s submission that if the Tribunal would be assisted by further
     detail in relation to the criminal charges brought against Ms Vida Reid and the
     subsequent prosecution, including the likely timeframe to trial, then the Applicants
     should be directed to provide this information.

9.   In response to paragraph 19(a) of the AS, where it describes the Respondent’s
     submission as a “generalised and vague assertion” that “criminal proceedings will be
     potentially jeopardised if witnesses to those proceedings also given evidence in these
     AAT proceedings”, the Respondent reiterates that there are numerous references

      jeopardised if witnesses to those proceedings also given evidence in these AAT
      proceedings.

11.   Finally, the Respondent acknowledges the affidavit of Mr Simon Reid and the
      statements contained therein. In the absence of any supporting documentation of
      financial hardship and in light of the, not insignificant matters of which Mr Reid is
      alleged to have been involved in, the Respondent submits that the affidavit should be
      given little weight.

Summary and Order sought

12.   Having regard to the matters referred to above, the Commissioner continues to seek
      an order that these AAT applications be stayed until the concurrent criminal
      proceedings or any future criminal proceedings arising from the same set of
      circumstances has been determined.
