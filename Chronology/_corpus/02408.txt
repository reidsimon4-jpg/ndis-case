2. What We Can Do — Value Engineering Measures
We have identified seven areas where we can reduce costs without undermining the integrity or longevity of the building. Each measure is detailed below with the saving and the practical impact.

3. What We Will Not Compromise On
There are elements of our build system that we will not reduce below a certain quality threshold. These are not about margin — they are about structural performance, longevity, and our ability to warrant the building. Cutting these items would save money upfront but cost you significantly more in maintenance, repairs, and risk over the building's life.
3.1 Structural Steel Grade & Coatings
All steel in our system — whether LGS framing or the WF primary members — is specified at a minimum G550 MPa yield strength for LGS and Grade 250 for WF sections. The LGS profiles carry a Z275 galvanised coating (275 g/m² zinc) as standard. This is critical in a coastal North Bali environment where salt air and humidity will aggressively attack unprotected or under-protected steel.
The alternative quote specifies WF steel with no mention of corrosion protection, coating specification, or steel grade. In Pemuteran — metres from the ocean — uncoated or paint-only steel will show corrosion within 2-3 years and require ongoing maintenance. Our galvanised system is designed for 25+ years with zero maintenance in coastal environments.
3.2 Wall System Integrity
Our wall system uses engineered cladding panels on LGS frames — a complete dry-build envelope that requires no bricklaying, no plastering, no rendering, and no painting. The alternative uses batako (concrete block) walls with sand-cement plaster and aci (skim coat), which is a wet-trade process that:
Takes 3-4x longer to complete due to curing times between coats
Is highly dependent on mason skill — uneven plaster is the most common quality issue in Indonesian construction
Requires painting immediately and repainting every 2-3 years in a coastal environment
Cracks at joints between block and concrete elements (sloof, kolom) within 12-18 months as the structure settles
We will not substitute our wall system for masonry. It is one of the key reasons our build is faster, more consistent, and lower-maintenance. The wall system saving we already offer you (our walls are IDR 34M cheaper than the alternative's masonry approach) demonstrates this is not a premium item — it is genuinely better value.
3.3 Foundation Engineering
Our foundation system is engineered to match the superstructure. We will not revert to a traditional batu kali (river stone) foundation as specified in the alternative quote. Batu kali foundations rely on manual stone placement with sand-cement mortar — the quality is entirely dependent on the skill of the tukang (mason) on the day, and there is no engineering calculation behind the sizing.
Our foundations are lighter, faster to install, and engineered with calculated load paths. They cost less (IDR 13.5M vs IDR 37M in the alternative) and perform better. There is no VE benefit in downgrading here — we are already the cheaper option.
3.4 Concrete Specification
Even with the VE reduction to 80mm fibre-reinforced slab, we maintain a minimum K225 concrete grade for all structural concrete. The alternative uses K175 for footplates, sloof, and columns. K175 is a lower-strength mix that is more porous, more susceptible to water ingress, and less durable in the long term. For a building near the coast with potential moisture exposure, K225 is the minimum responsible specification.
