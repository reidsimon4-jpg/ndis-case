B. The preliminary statutory issue
4.       The banning order made against the Second Applicant on 21 December 2022 prohibited
         him from "providing disability supports and services to NDIS-funded participants". On
         internal review on 21 April 2023 the operative prohibition was broadened to "any
         involvement with or in the NDIS sector, in any capacity", including providing disability
         supports and services "directly or indirectly", providing labour hire workers to support NDIS
         providers, and providing "support, funding, guidance or assistance of any form" to an NDIS
         provider.
5.      In Sunflower Care Services Pty Ltd v Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards
        Commission (No 2) [2025] FCA 1442 at [127]–[141], the Federal Court held that an order
        under section 73ZN of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (Cth) must
        prohibit or restrict engagement in specified activities, and must express those activities in
        terms sufficiently clear that the order is reasonably capable of being obeyed.

6.      The Second Applicant does not submit that Sunflower automatically determines every
        issue. A preliminary determination should identify the operative prohibition in force at each
        date on which conduct is alleged, whether the impugned wording was authorised and, if
        not, whether it is severable, and whether the conduct particularised was legally capable of
        contravening the operative prohibition. Whether the conduct occurred, and what
        relevance, if any, it has to present suitability, are contested merits questions reserved to
        the hearing. The utility of the exercise is illustrated by the Respondent's own record: the
        internal review reasons record that the registration application for Dream Big Employment
        Pty Ltd was submitted on 29 October 2022 — before the notice of intention of 17
        November 2022 and before the banning order of 21 December 2022.

7.      The reasons of 31 July 2026 record at paragraphs 4 and 34 that the Second Applicant held
        no office in Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd between 3 January 2021 and 1 November 2022,
        that the individual applicants held their FFG directorships "albeit not at the same time", and
        that he is recorded as a director of Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd from 17 February 2022. Each claim
        period identified at paragraph 35 falls within the interval in which he held no office in
        Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd. Those facts do not of themselves resolve attribution; they
        are why an applicant-specific schedule is necessary.

     internal review decision and reasons of 21 April 2023, the order as varied, the instrument of

      sector, in any capacity" and "directly or indirectly", and the inclusive labour-hire and

14.   Otherwise, the Respondent's proposed orders 1 to 4 be made in a form conformed to the
