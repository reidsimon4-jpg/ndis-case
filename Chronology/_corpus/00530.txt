Terri about getting support coordination services for Ben through FFG.

6. Terri prepared a service agreement between me as Ben's nominee, and FFG for

7. | was due to have a meeting with Terri the day after | signed the service agreement
in October 2021 to discuss Ben’s plan funding. This meeting was to take place at
the offices of a company called Enable who are NDIS service providers. The
meeting also involved a representative of the NDIS whose name | can’t remember.

8. Enable were providing supports to Ben at the time, | just needed a support
coordinator and that why | engaged FFG. Terri didn’t turn up at the office of Enable
so we proceeded without her.

9. When Terri didn’t turn up at the meeting, | called FFG to speak to her. The phone
call was answered by a woman called Vida who told me that Terri no longer worked
for them. | later ascertained that her full name was Vida Reid. | rang FFG the same
day again still trying to find Terri and spoke to woman who also told me that Terri no
longer for FFG.

10. | decided not to proceed with FFG as Terri no longer worked there. Within a two-
day period after the meeting with Enable, | was contacted by a woman from FFG
who had been assigned by Terri to be Ben’s support coordinator. This woman told
me FFG could not continue with Ben’s services to which | agreed. At that stage,
FFG had not provided any services to Ben anyway. FFG did not have a
representative in the meeting with Enable and NDIS.

11. On Tuesday the 11 October 2022, | was contacted by Gerard Righetti from Fraud
Investigations Team at the NDIA. Gerard told me that FFG had claimed a payment
from Ben’s plan consisting of 11 hours of household cleaning. | was blown away by
this and immediately thought ‘That Bitch’ referring to Vida.

12. | never got any services from FFG or asked them to provide cleaning for Ben. It
was only ever supposed to be a support coordinator which never proceeded
anyway.

13. Ben is very neat and tidy and cleans his own house with help from his support

workers.
