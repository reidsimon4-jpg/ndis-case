Hi Pam,

Happy to do this on your behalf if need be. What need to lodge is a S48 which is a change of circumstance. We can use this even though no changes have been made but
the funding is running out. Another form to activate a review is s100 but this must be lodged within 100 days of the current plan being approved in this case, we are over
the required days.

If you would like to lodge s48 more than happy to but I would advise to do this very soon as it takes roughly 1.5 months for this to be viewed by the NDIA. Once this hits
the emails on the NDIA side, they will reject or approve.
Once approved it will then be 14 business days for someone to contact for review appointment which pushes this out to a further 3 to 4 weeks.

The Functional Capacity Assessment that has been completed, This is a great starting point as we need all reports from all mainstream supports for the possible plan review.
