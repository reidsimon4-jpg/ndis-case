We refer to the above project and the additional works and scope adjustments arising during the
course of execution.

This notice is to formally record that a number of items now constitute variations to the original
agreed scope of works. These changes have arisen during site execution and have affected the civil
works scope, sequencing, and associated construction methodology.




                                                                             Variation Items
This Variation Order applies exclusively to the following civil works scope adjustments and associated
impacts arising from revised site conditions, design development, and execution requirements. No
other scope is included within this current VO unless separately instructed and agreed.




A. Civil Works Variations Covered Under This VO

   1.​ Revised slab geometry and levels, including adjustments to previously issued slab dimensions,
       slab interface conditions, and level changes between upper and lower areas.
   2.​ Additional formwork requirements arising from revised slab layout, level changes, and altered
       edge conditions.
   3.​ Revised step construction, including updated step quantity, dimensions, heights, widths, and
       associated concrete / formwork works.
   4.​ Garden / planting / non-concreted cut-out areas introduced into the slab layout, requiring
       additional set-out, formwork, edge detailing, and coordination.
   5.​ Raised wall / retaining wall / seating edge treatment affecting slab edge conditions, retaining
       requirements, and associated civil interface works.
   6.​ Drainage adjustments, including relocation / installation of drain pipework, tie-in to waste line,
       and associated civil works required to accommodate the updated design intent.
   7.​ Any rework, resequencing, or abortive civil works caused by late design amendments, revised
       instructions, or updated issued drawings after civil preparation had commenced.
