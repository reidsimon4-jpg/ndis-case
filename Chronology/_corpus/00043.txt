SCHEDULE - COMMONWEALTH STATEMENTS AND CONDUCT vs THE DOCUMENTED RECORD (2022-2026)
Working draft v3, 2 July 2026 - extended with the freeze-era primaries recovered from the FFG Outlook tenant and the 2 July deep-dive findings. For counsel review. Rows marked TO BE VERIFIED or UNVERIFIED must be confirmed against primary sources before filing or service; the Dec 2024 - Jan 2025 row is included for counsel’s judgement. Prepared in support of: severance/sequencing; response to the non-disclosure application; the verification letter to the government’s solicitors; the prosecutors’ decision-demand; statement-of-reasons requests; the prospective federal proceeding.
The election this schedule forces: (A) if there is no investigation and no maintained intention to re-charge, the Commonwealth can say so in writing - the criminal limb closes, and the protective rationale for the ongoing administrative orders and the non-disclosure claims collapses; or (B) if the investigation or intention is live, the 11 March 2025 assurance was empty as given, Vida Reid’s preserved jeopardy is confirmed on the Commonwealth’s own records, and her matters cannot fairly be heard jointly; or (C) silence - which is itself a fact the Tribunal can be asked to weigh. The 2022 rows force a second election in any forum that reaches the merits: the payment stop of October 2022 was either authorised (then identify the power and the decision-maker - none is documented) or unauthorised (then the State Manager’s 6 October denial was accurate only because her own officers were acting without authority). Each limb advances the applicants’ position; no limb assists the Respondent.
