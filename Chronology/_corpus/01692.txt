Please find attached my Defence Submission, served pursuant to the direction issued by
Deputy President McMillan KC in the matter of Reid v NDIS Commission – Case No.
2023/3361.



1. Compliance Under Protest

This submission is being served strictly under protest and without waiver of any legal
rights or objections.

My concerns are as follows:

   1. The document was initially intended solely for the Tribunal and AGS.
   2. It was already subject to a serious privacy breach, when AGS forwarded it to a third
      party with a known conflict of interest.
   3. That breach remains unaddressed by the Tribunal, and instead I have been
      instructed to re-serve the same document on parties adverse to me, compounding the
      risk and harm.

This service is made solely in compliance with the Tribunal’s direction, and should not be
interpreted as voluntary disclosure, informed consent, or abandonment of my right to pursue
complaints or review of this process, including via the Commonwealth Ombudsman, the
Privacy Commissioner, or superior courts including the high court of Australia.



2. Request for Interpreter

I also formally request that all future hearings and directions hearings be conducted with the
assistance of a qualified interpreter.
English is not my first language. While I am able to communicate in basic conversational
English, I have struggled greatly to keep up and fully understand the proceedings,
especially due to the fast pace of speech and the legal terminology (“legalese”) used during
the hearings.

For procedural fairness, the interpreter assigned should:

   •   Be fluent in both English and my native language Persian/Farsi;
   •   Understand legal language and concepts; and
   •   Be able to interpret accurately in a tribunal/legal context.

This request is made to ensure that I can meaningfully participate in the proceedings and fully
understand the case against me.
