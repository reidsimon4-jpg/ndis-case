STATUS: LODGE NOW — INDEPENDENT OF EVERY OTHER STEP
EXPLAINER FOR THE LEGAL TEAM
What this is: Seven send-now instruments: six FOI requests across the agencies whose records matter, a Privacy Act fallback, and an evidence-preservation (litigation-hold) notice to the NDIA.
Why now: FOI runs a slow clock (30 days statutory; 12–24 months on review) so the only cost of waiting is lost time. Preservation cannot wait at all — the NDIA’s mailboxes, Teams and logs for Halls and Lundgren rotate, and waiting for the stay/LPP decision risks losing them.
Who sends: Each FOI goes from the relevant applicant’s own email (personal-information access right). Counsel to settle the preservation notice and the authority attachments.
Verify first: Before Email 2 (NDIS Commission), confirm the 6 March 2025 FOI was not already lodged from Vida’s Yahoo account. Fill the [DOB]/[address] fields; attach a signed authority where a spouse or FFG is a co-applicant.
Expected pushback: s 37 (law enforcement) on CDPP/AFP/NDIA; s 47F (third-party privacy); a “seek it through the Tribunal” deflection — each is pre-empted in the drafted text.

Instrument 8 — Evidence-preservation (litigation-hold) notice to the NDIA
Send now. This is the one non-FOI instrument in this package. It is here, not with the summonses, because it must go before the records rotate — it does not wait for the stay/LPP decision or the federal filing.
To:  The General Counsel, National Disability Insurance Agency [confirm address]
Cc:  C. Land / K. Dickinson, Australian Government Solicitor
Dear General Counsel,
The applicants are parties to ART proceedings 2023/3361, 2023/3363 and 2023/4494 and contemplate proceedings in the Federal Court under s 39B of the Judiciary Act 1903 (Cth) concerning the conduct of the NDIA. The NDIA holds documents likely to be relevant.
The NDIA is asked to immediately suspend any routine or automated destruction, deletion or archiving of, and to preserve in native form with metadata, the following for the period 1 January 2022 to date:
(a) all emails, Microsoft Teams and other messaging records, file/case notes, CRM and participant-record entries, allocation and “request for service” records, system “alerts”, audit logs and call recordings concerning FFG or the Reids;
(b) the mailboxes, Teams accounts and mobile messaging of Nathan Halls, Barbara Lundgren, Bruce Graydon and Tim Jarratt;
(c) the “Provider Exit Protocol”, the Tactical Intelligence Report (18 August 2022), the Statement of Material Facts (19 October 2022) and the “Project Archer” file.
Please confirm within 14 days that a litigation hold is in place. The applicants reserve the right to rely on any failure to preserve, including as to inferences from the destruction of relevant documents.
