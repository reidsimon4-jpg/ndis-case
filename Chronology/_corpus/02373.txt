                                                       Project Scope of Works

The scope of works for the proposed gym development in Canggu, Bali includes the
following:

   ●​ Full land excavation and site preparation works
   ●​ Installation of a sufficient and compliant site drainage system
   ●​ Construction of a reinforced concrete slab foundation suitable for a
      commercial gym facility
   ●​ Structural design, manufacture, and installation of a Light Gauge Steel (LGS)
      frame for the gym building
   ●​ Base electrical fit-out to support lighting, power, and essential gym equipment

All timelines outlined in this schedule are effective from the moment the client’s
downpayment has been received and cleared.




                                 Design & Engineering Phase: 1 Week

The project commences with the design and engineering phase, which is expected
to take approximately one week. During this period, the concrete slab foundation will
be structurally designed, and full engineering for the LGS structural system will be
completed. This phase ensures that all civil and structural elements are properly
coordinated prior to fabrication and site works, reducing risk and ensuring efficient
execution during construction.

             Manufacture of LGS Structural Frames: 1-2 Weeks

Following completion of the structural design, the LGS frames will be manufactured
off-site. This process is expected to take between one and two weeks. The
manufacturing stage includes detailed shop drawings, fabrication of wall frames,
roof trusses, and primary structural components in accordance with the approved
engineering design. Where possible, this phase may overlap with early-stage civil
works to optimise the overall project timeline.




                      Land Preparation & Civil Works: 2-3 Weeks

Site preparation and civil construction works are anticipated to take between two
and three weeks, inclusive of concrete curing time. This phase includes site clearing,
excavation, ground preparation and compaction, installation of drainage systems,
and construction of the reinforced concrete slab. Adequate curing time is allowed to
ensure the slab achieves the required structural strength before the superstructure is
installed.




                                  Installation of LGS Structure: 1 Week

Once the slab has cured and the LGS components have been fabricated, the
structural frame installation will commence. The installation period is expected to
take approximately one week. This stage involves delivery of the prefabricated LGS
components to site, erection of the structural frame, and final alignment, fixing, and
bracing of all primary structural elements. The modular and lightweight nature of the
LGS system allows for rapid and efficient installation with minimal site disruption.

                                                         Electrical Fit-Out: 3 Days

Following completion of the structural installation, the electrical fit-out will be carried
out over an estimated period of three days. This includes installation of essential
electrical infrastructure, lighting, power points, and provisions required to support
gym equipment and basic operational needs.




                                               Overall Programme Summary

Subject to site conditions, weather, and timely approvals, the overall project duration
is estimated to be approximately five to seven weeks from receipt of the client’s
downpayment through to completion of the electrical fit-out. This programme has
been structured to provide clarity, efficiency, and certainty while maintaining
appropriate construction and quality standards for a commercial gym facility.

*This schedule is indicative and may be adjusted in response to unforeseen site conditions or
changes to project scope.*
