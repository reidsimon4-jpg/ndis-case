1. PREAMBLE
WHEREAS the Borrower is a limited liability company (PT) established under Law No.
40 of 2007 regarding Limited Liability Companies of the Republic of Indonesia,
engaged in the design, manufacture, and construction of light gauge steel modular
buildings;
WHEREAS the Lender is a limited company incorporated in indonesia, engaged in
design, construction, and real estate development, which intends to establish PT
INFINITY LIVING INDONESIA (PT PMA) in accordance with Indonesian Investment
Law No. 25 of 2007;
WHEREAS the Borrower requires funding for its operations, equipment acquisition,
and business expansion; and
WHEREAS the Lender has agreed to provide financing to the Borrower subject to
certain commercial arrangements as outlined herein;
NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the
parties agree as follows:

2. LOAN TERMS
2.1 Principal Amount
The Lender agrees to provide to the Borrower a loan facility of up to USD 500,000
(United States Dollars Five Hundred Thousand) (the "Loan").

2.2 Drawdown
The Loan shall be made available in tranches upon written request by the Borrower,
subject to the Lender's approval, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

2.3 Interest Rate
The Loan shall bear interest at a rate of 7% per annum, calculated on the outstanding
principal. This rate is established in accordance with the arm's length principle
required by Indonesian tax regulations (Peraturan Menteri Keuangan No.
213/PMK.03/2016).

2.4 Repayment
The Borrower shall repay the Loan in accordance with the following schedule:
(a) Interest payments: Quarterly, commencing three (3) months after the initial
drawdown;
(b) Principal repayment: In equal annual installments over a period of five (5) years,
commencing one (1) year after the initial drawdown.

2.5 Withholding Tax
All interest payments shall be subject to withholding tax in accordance with Article 26
of the Indonesian Income Tax Law and the Singapore-Indonesia Tax Treaty, currently
at a rate of 10%. The Borrower shall be responsible for withholding and remitting
such tax to the Indonesian Directorate General of Taxes and providing the Lender
with certificates of payment.

2.6 Security
As security for the Loan, the Borrower pledges the following:
(a) Equipment and machinery owned by the Borrower;
(b) Manufacturing facility assets; and
(c) Any other assets as may be reasonably required by the Lender.
All security arrangements shall be properly documented and registered with the
Fiduciary Registry Office (Kantor Pendaftaran Fidusia) in accordance with Law No. 42
of 1999 regarding Fiduciary Security.

3. EXCLUSIVE COMMERCIAL RIGHTS
3.1 Exclusive Production Rights
In consideration of the Loan, the Borrower hereby grants to the Lender and/or its
nominated Indonesian PT PMA exclusive rights to all products designed,
manufactured, or constructed by the Borrower during the term of this Agreement
and for a period of ten (10) years thereafter. The Borrower shall not enter into any
agreement with any third party that would conflict with these exclusive rights.

3.2 Exclusive Distribution Rights
The Borrower grants to the Lender and/or its nominated Indonesian PT PMA the
exclusive right to market, distribute, and sell all products manufactured or
constructed by the Borrower. The Borrower shall fulfill all production orders placed
by the Lender in priority to any other commercial arrangements.

3.3 Profit Sharing
In return for the marketing, distribution, and sale of the Borrower's products, the
Lender shall pay to the Borrower a fee equal to five percent (5%) of the net profit
derived from such sales. For the purposes of this Agreement, "net profit" shall mean
the gross revenue from sales less all direct costs, marketing expenses, financing
costs, depreciation, and applicable taxes.
This arrangement shall be documented in a separate Technical Service Agreement to
be registered with the Indonesian Tax Office to ensure appropriate tax treatment.

3.4 Transfer Pricing Compliance
The parties acknowledge that all transactions between them are subject to transfer
pricing regulations under Indonesian Tax Law No. 36/2008 and Regulation No. PER-
32/PJ/2011. The parties agree to maintain appropriate transfer pricing
documentation and to ensure that all inter-company transactions are conducted at
arm's length prices.

3.5 Financial Reporting
The Lender shall provide to the Borrower quarterly statements outlining all sales of
the Borrower's products, associated costs, and the calculation of the 5% profit share.
Payment of the profit share shall be made within thirty (30) days of the end of each
quarter.

4. ACQUISITION OPTION
4.1 Option Grant
The Borrower hereby grants to the Lender an irrevocable option to acquire all issued
shares in the Borrower through the Lender's Indonesian PT PMA at a nominal
consideration of IDR 10,000,000 (Indonesian Rupiah Ten Million) plus the forgiveness
of any outstanding loan amounts at the time of exercise (the "Acquisition Option").
4.2 Exercise Conditions
The Lender may exercise the Acquisition Option upon the occurrence of either of the
following events:
(a) The Borrower's annual revenue reaches IDR 50,000,000,000 (Indonesian Rupiah
Fifty Billion, approximately USD 3.2 million) or total assets reach IDR 10,000,000,000
(Indonesian Rupiah Ten Billion, approximately USD 640,000), which classifies it as a
"Large Business" under Indonesian regulations; or
(b) Changes to Indonesian foreign investment laws and regulations that would
subject the Borrower to the same level of regulatory oversight as a PT PMA in the
construction and manufacturing sectors.

4.3 Regulatory Compliance
The parties acknowledge that the exercise of the Acquisition Option shall be subject
to obtaining all necessary governmental and regulatory approvals, including from:
(a) The Indonesian Investment Coordinating Board (BKPM);
(b) The Ministry of Law and Human Rights;
(c) The Construction Services Development Board (LPJK), if applicable; and
(d) Any other relevant authorities.
The Borrower agrees to fully cooperate in obtaining such approvals.

4.4 Negative Investment List Compliance
The parties acknowledge that foreign investment in the construction sector is subject
to the restrictions as outlined in Presidential Regulation No. 10 of 2021 regarding
Investment Business Fields. The Lender confirms that its PT PMA will comply with all
applicable ownership limitations, local partnership requirements, and special
licensing conditions.

4.5 Tax Implications
The parties acknowledge that the exercise of the Acquisition Option may trigger tax
obligations under Indonesian tax regulations. Any applicable taxes, including but not
limited to income tax, value-added tax, and stamp duty shall be paid in accordance
with applicable laws. The parties agree to obtain a tax opinion from a reputable
Indonesian tax consultant prior to exercising the Acquisition Option.

4.6 Valuation
For the purposes of regulatory filings and tax compliance, the parties agree that a fair
market valuation of the Borrower shall be conducted by an independent valuer
approved by the Indonesian Financial Services Authority (OJK) at the time of exercise
of the Acquisition Option. The parties shall share equally the cost of such valuation.

5. GOVERNANCE AND MANAGEMENT
5.1 Board Representation
The Lender shall be entitled to appoint one (1) director and one (1) commissioner to
the Borrower's Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners, respectively, in
accordance with Law No. 40 of 2007 regarding Limited Liability Companies.

5.2 Reserved Matters
The following matters shall require the prior written consent of the Lender:
(a) Amendment to the Articles of Association of the Borrower;
(b) Issuance of new shares or creation of new classes of shares;
(c) Declaration or payment of dividends;
(d) Incurrence of additional debt beyond USD 10,000;
(e) Sale, transfer, or disposal of material assets;
(f) Entry into any joint venture or partnership; and
(g) Any transaction with related parties.

5.3 Information Rights
The Borrower shall provide to the Lender:
(a) Monthly financial statements within fifteen (15) days of month-end;
(b) Quarterly management accounts within thirty (30) days of quarter-end;
(c) Audited annual financial statements within ninety (90) days of year-end; and
(d) Any information reasonably requested by the Lender.

5.4 Compliance with Thin Capitalization Rules
The parties acknowledge that under Indonesian tax regulations (PMK-
169/PMK.010/2015), the maximum allowable debt-to-equity ratio is 4:1. The
Borrower agrees to maintain a debt-to-equity ratio below this threshold to ensure
full tax deductibility of interest expenses.

6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
6.1 Borrower's Representations
The Borrower represents and warrants to the Lender that:
(a) It is duly incorporated and validly existing under Indonesian law;
(b) It has the power and authority to enter into this Agreement;
(c) This Agreement constitutes valid and binding obligations;
(d) The execution of this Agreement does not violate any law, regulation, or other
agreement;
(e) It holds all permits and licenses required for its operations, including a proper
construction business license (IUJK) issued by the Construction Services Development
Board;
(f) Its financial statements fairly represent its financial condition; and
(g) There are no pending or threatened legal proceedings against it.

6.2 Lender's Representations
The Lender represents and warrants to the Borrower that:
(a) It is duly incorporated and validly existing;
(b) It has the power and authority to enter into this Agreement;
(c) This Agreement constitutes valid and binding obligations; and
(d) It has or will obtain all necessary permits and approvals for its planned PT PMA in
Indonesia.

7. GENERAL PROVISIONS
7.1 Term and Termination
This Agreement shall commence on the date of execution and continue until the
later of:
(a) Full repayment of the Loan; or
(b) Exercise of the Acquisition Option by the Lender.

7.2 Confidentiality
Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of all information obtained from the
other party and shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior
written consent, except as required by law or regulation.

7.3 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of
the Republic of Indonesia.

7.4 Dispute Resolution
Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be resolved by
arbitration in Singapore in accordance with the Arbitration Rules of the Singapore
International Arbitration Centre. The arbitration shall be conducted in the English
language.

7.5 Language
This Agreement shall be executed in both English and Indonesian languages. In the
event of any inconsistency, the Indonesian language version shall prevail, in
accordance with Law No. 24 of 2009 regarding National Flag, Language, Emblem and
Anthem.

7.6 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with
respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements,
negotiations, and discussions.

7.7 Amendments
No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by
authorized representatives of both parties and properly notarized.

7.8 Assignment
Neither party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the
prior written consent of the other party, except that the Lender may assign its rights
to its Indonesian PT PMA upon its establishment.

7.9 Notices
All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses
specified above or to such other address as may be designated in writing by the
relevant party.

7.10 Notarization
This Agreement shall be executed in the form of a notarial deed before an
Indonesian notary public.

7.11 Registration
The security arrangements contemplated in this Agreement shall be registered with
the relevant Indonesian authorities, including the Fiduciary Registry Office.

7.12 Counterparts
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an
original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.

their duly authorized representatives as of the date first written above.
