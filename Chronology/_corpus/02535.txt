## Margin Analysis Framework

### Gross Margin Calculation

```
Revenue per tonne: Rp 76,109,728 (installed, delivered)
COGS per tonne: [Material cost + Labor + Overhead]
Gross Margin = Revenue - COGS
```

### Target Margins

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Gross Margin** | >30% | Before overhead allocation |
| **Project Margin** | >20% | After project-specific costs |
| **Minimum Acceptable** | 15% | Flag for approval if below |

## Pricing Strategy Notes

### Bali Market Premium
- Bali customers accept higher prices for quality/speed
- Emphasize engineering + compliance + predictable delivery
- Don't compete on price; compete on value

### Volume Discounts
- >20 tonnes: Negotiable
- Repeat customers: Negotiable
- Strategic projects: Case-by-case

### Price Escalation
- Steel price volatility clause recommended
- Trigger: >5% change in coil prices
- Notification: 7 days before adjustment
