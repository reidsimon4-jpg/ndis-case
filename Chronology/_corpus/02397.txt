Z275 galvanized coating (275 g/m²) provides corrosion resistance for high-humidity tropical environments. The strategy includes: factory galvanized coating on all LGS components; connection checks to ensure coating integrity; periodic corrosion monitoring. 3. Foundation System — Helical Screw Piles
Helical screw pile foundations provide an integrated engineering solution for unstable soils including peat, swamp, and flood zones. Unlike solar ground screws, helical piles rely on end-bearing through helical plates embedded in deep stable strata. [Source: strategy.txt, technical-specifications.html]

[ASSUMPTION] Foundation design is based on generic peat/alluvial soil profiles typical of West Sumatra. Site-specific geotechnical investigation is required prior to installation.
