Light Gauge Steel (LGS) Framing Supply & Installation
This Construction Contract Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on [Date, Month, Year] (the "Effective Date") by and between:

The Client and the Contractor are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party."
1. Introduction and Agreement
1.1. Project Description The Project involves the supply, fabrication, and installation of the Light Gauge Steel (LGS) / Cold-Formed Steel (CFS) framing system for the structure located at [Project Site Address in Bali, Indonesia] (the "Project Site").
1.2. Scope of Work (LGS Frames) The Contractor shall execute the works as detailed in Schedule 1 – Scope of Works, which primarily consists of, but is not limited to: structural design review, procurement of LGS materials, pre-fabrication, delivery to the Project Site, and final assembly/installation of the LGS/CFS frame structure.
1.3. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Indonesia. Any disputes arising under these terms shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Bali, Indonesia only if amicable resolution and mandatory arbitration (as detailed in Section 15) fail to resolve the matter.
2. Contract Price and Payment
2.1. Contract Price The total fixed lump-sum price for the entire Scope of Work defined in Section 1.2 is [Amount in Currency] ([Amount in Words]) (the "Contract Price"). This price includes the cost of all materials, labor, equipment, taxes (excluding any specifically excluded taxes), and all other items necessary to complete the LGS frame supply and installation.
2.2. Payment Terms Payments shall be made in installments according to the milestones and schedule specified in Schedule 4 – Payment Schedule.
2.3. Price Adjustments The Contract Price is fixed. Any adjustments or variations to the Scope of Work must be formally agreed upon in writing by both Parties in accordance with Section 7 (Variations and Changes).
3. Project Timeline
The Contractor shall achieve Substantial Completion of the LGS frame installation works in accordance with the milestones and completion date specified in Schedule 2 – Project Timeline. Time is of the essence for this Agreement.
4. Contractor’s Obligations
The Contractor shall: a) Execute the Scope of Work in a professional and workmanlike manner, conforming to the structural drawings and specifications in Schedule 6 – Technical Specifications. b) Comply with all applicable Indonesian laws, regulations, and permits related to construction services (Law No. 2 of 2017) and labor. c) Be responsible for the safety of all its personnel and subcontractors on the Project Site. d) Coordination with Third-Party Consultants and Design Liability The Contractor agrees to coordinate its LGS works with designs, drawings, and specifications provided by the Client’s appointed third-party consultants (e.g., MEP consultants, Interior Design consultants, and any other appointed consultants). However, the Contractor is not responsible for, and assumes no liability for, the content, fitness for purpose, accuracy, completeness, or constructability of any design, specification, or coordination detail provided by these third-party consultants. The Contractor’s acceptance of such coordinated details is solely for the limited purpose of installing the LGS framing and does not constitute a warranty or certification of the consultant's work. The Client retains full liability for the integration, performance, and suitability of all third-party consultant designs and specifications.
5. Client’s Obligations
The Client shall: a) Provide site access and adequate working space for the Contractor. b) Ensure all permits and approvals necessary for the foundation and general construction works are in place before the Contractor commences installation. c) Make payments to the Contractor strictly in accordance with Section 2.2 and Schedule 4.
6. Materials and Workmanship
All materials supplied, specifically the LGS/CFS components, shall be new, of high quality, and conform to the standards detailed in Schedule 6. The workmanship shall meet industry standards and best practices for steel framing in the region.
7. Variations and Changes
Any change, modification, addition, or deletion to the Scope of Work must be requested in writing and only implemented upon the mutual written agreement of both Parties, detailing the change to the Contract Price and/or the Project Timeline.
8. Warranties
The Contractor warrants that the LGS/CFS framing system supplied and installed under this Agreement will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for a period of [Twelve (12)] months following the date of Substantial Completion. This warranty is subject to mandatory Indonesian laws regarding liability for building failure.
9. Limitation of Liability and Indemnity
9.1. Limitation of Liability (The Contractor) - ABSOLUTE CAP THE CONTRACTOR'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT, REGARDLESS OF THE CAUSE (INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE, BREACH OF CONTRACT, OR STATUTORY LIABILITY) AND IRRESPECTIVE OF FAULT, SHALL IN NO EVENT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES ACTUALLY PAID BY THE CLIENT TO THE CONTRACTOR SPECIFICALLY FOR THE LGS/CFS FRAMING SUPPLY AND INSTALLATION SCOPE OF WORK (THE "CAP"). This Cap excludes fees paid for third-party consultancy coordination, design fees, and taxes. This stated limit is the maximum aggregate liability cap for the Contractor for all claims arising under this Agreement, subject only to mandatory, non-waivable legal liabilities under Indonesian law, such as for Building Failure (as defined in Law No. 2 of 2017).
9.2. Indemnity The Contractor shall indemnify and hold harmless the Client against all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses arising out of the Contractor’s negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Scope of Work.
10. Insurance
Each Party shall be responsible for maintaining adequate insurance coverage, including, but not limited to, [Specific Insurance, e.g., Construction All Risks (CAR) / Employer’s Liability], to cover its respective liabilities and obligations under this Agreement.
11. Force Majeure
Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure to perform its obligations (other than payment obligations) if such delay or failure is due to an event of Force Majeure (e.g., acts of nature, government action, war, or epidemic/pandemic-related restrictions not already in existence at the Effective Date).
12. Default and Termination
12.1. Termination for Default Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches any term or condition of this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice thereof.
12.2. Termination for Convenience The Client may terminate this Agreement for its convenience by providing sixty (60) days’ written notice to the Contractor. In such an event, the Client shall pay the Contractor for all work executed up to the date of termination, plus all demobilization costs and a reasonable, pre-agreed termination fee of [Percentage]% of the uncompleted portion of the Scope of Work.
13. Notices
All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the contact addresses specified in the preamble.
14. Prevailing Language
This Agreement is executed in the English language. In the event this Agreement is translated into Bahasa Indonesia for compliance with Indonesian Law No. 24 of 2009, the Parties agree that the English language version shall prevail in the event of any inconsistency or dispute regarding interpretation.
15. Dispute Resolution
15.1. Amicable Settlement (Deliberation) The Parties agree to attempt to resolve any dispute through good faith negotiations between senior representatives within sixty (60) days of the notification of a dispute.
15.2. Mandatory Arbitration (BANI) Failing amicable settlement under Section 15.1, the Parties agree that any unresolved disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity, or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by binding arbitration administered by the Badan Arbitrase Nasional Indonesia (BANI) in accordance with the BANI Rules of Arbitration.
The seat and venue of the arbitration shall be Bali, Indonesia.
The language of the arbitration shall be English (or Bahasa Indonesia, as mutually agreed).
The number of arbitrators shall be one (1).
The award rendered by the arbitrator(s) shall be final and binding upon the Parties, and the Parties hereby waive the right to recourse to the courts save for the enforcement of the award.
15.3. Jurisdiction (Residual) Only disputes relating to the enforcement of a BANI arbitration award, or matters that cannot legally be subjected to arbitration under Indonesian law, shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the District Court of Bali, Indonesia.
