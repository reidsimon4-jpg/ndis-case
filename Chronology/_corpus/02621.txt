### ► The webhook URL, explained simply

The apps need an "address" to send messages to. Teams gives you that address —
it's just a special link. To get it (5 minutes, once):

1. Open **Teams** → go to the channel where you want the messages
   (e.g. General, or make a channel called **ilo-feed**).
2. Click the **⋯ (three dots)** next to the channel name → choose
   **Workflows**.
3. Pick the template **"Post to a channel when a webhook request is
   received"** → click through to create it.
4. Teams shows you a long **URL** — that's the address. **Copy it.**
5. **Paste that URL to me in chat** — I'll plug it into all three apps and
   test that a card lands in your channel.

Until that URL exists, the feature sits silent (nothing breaks).
