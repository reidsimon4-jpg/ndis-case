Key Points
1. On 17 November 2022, you signed a notice of intention to make a banning order, in respect of
   Mr Simon Reid for a period of five (5) years. The notice was issued by both email and post to
   Mr Reid (Attachment B).
2. Mr Reid’s legal representatives, Tom & Co Legal, note that Mr Reid received the notice on
   18 November 2022.
3. The notice provided 13 days for submissions to be made on the proposed banning order.
4. On 22 November 2022, Tom & Co Legal issued correspondence on Mr Reid’s behalf to the NDIS
   Commission seeking an extension of time of 28 days (Attachment C). This letter was not made
   available to the Director, Compliance until 30 November 2022.
5. On 30 November 2022, Tom & Co Legal issued correspondence on Mr Reid’s behalf to the NDIS
   Commission making submissions in respect of the proposed banning order (Attachment D).
   These submissions make representations concerning the timeframe provided for submissions,
   as well as an assertion that Mr Reid is in custody of relevant evidence to this matter.
6. Attached for your consideration is a proposed response to Tom & Co Legal (Attachment A). The
   response is consistent with that provided to Tom & Co Legal concerning Forward Focused
   Group Pty Ltd and Ms Vida Reid. Further, the letter makes a direct request for evidence
   allegedly held by Mr Reid that is material to this matter and the proposed banning order.
7. Under section 73ZN(7) of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (NDIS Act), you as
   delegate of the Commissioner, are required to only make a banning order against a person

    after giving the person an opportunity to make submissions to the Commissioner on the
    matter.
    7.1. It is considered that the notice dated 17 November 2022 and the letter at Attachment A
         meets this statutory requirement.
    7.2. Further, it is not considered that section 73ZN(8) applies in this circumstance, that would
         set aside the obligation in section 73ZN(7).
Sensitivities
8. It is considered that the provision of 14 days for submissions is appropriate in the
   circumstances, noting:
    8.1. It is consistent with the approach for Ms Reid; and
    8.2. The NDIS Commission does not hold any information that identifies any immediate danger
         to the health, safety or wellbeing of a person with disability posed by Mr Reid.
Consultation
9. Note that LSB advice has not been sought on this matter.
