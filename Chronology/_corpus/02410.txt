                                                          Chapter 2: Understanding the Concept




The Beach Shack at Mimba Bay | Schematic Design Package                                          6
                                     The Beach Shack is meant to be a contemporary retreat that reflects the authenticity of Pemuteran.
                                    The interior does not seek to replicate tradition or compete with it. Instead, it reinterprets the humility
                                      of coastal village life through honest materials, subtle craftsmanship, and beauty of imperfection.


                                                                   The concept is guided by three core ideas...




The Beach Shack at Mimba Bay | Schematic Design Package                                                                                           7
                                                                                        Unpretentious
                                     Inspired by the humble and grounded character of Pemuteran village, the interior embraces a sense of rawness and authenticity.
                                            Materials are presented in their natural state, allowing their true color, texture, and character to be fully expressed.
                                                                         The design prioritizes atmosphere, tactility, and function.




The Beach Shack at Mimba Bay | Schematic Design Package                                                                                                                8
                                                                                 Coastal Village Inspired
                       The interior draws inspiration from the surrounding coastal villages and the fishermen culture of Pemuteran. Coastal elements such as traditional boat hulls,
                                                          reclaimed wood, and old house windows are reinterpreted and elevated within the design.
                                          These elements are transformed into interior features such as headboards, partitions, wall panels, and furniture details.
                                 This serves as a subtle reference to the name Beach Shack, where found and unused objects are restored, refined, and given new purpose.




The Beach Shack at Mimba Bay | Schematic Design Package                                                                                                                                9
                                                                                            Wabi Sabi
                      The concept embraces the beauty of imperfection and impermanence. Natural aging, irregular textures, and handcrafted details are intentionally preserved
                       rather than concealed. Artefacts and fragments of larger objects such as boats, doors, or gongs are incorporated to honour their history and previous life.
                     The space remains restrained and understated, allowing materials to speak quietly. While the materials reflect imperfection, the spatial planning and detailing
                            remain deliberate and carefully considered. The balance between simplicity, authenticity, and precision defines this interpretation of wabi sabi.
