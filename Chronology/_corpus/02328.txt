Method Statement — Helical Screw Piles
Helical Screw Pile Foundation Installation
Version 1.0 | 12 February 2026

1. Scope of Work
Installation of 10–14 helical screw piles per housing unit
Achievement of stable strata depth through peat/soft soil
Torque recording on every pile for capacity verification
2. Equipment Required
Mini-excavator with hydraulic torque head
Survey equipment (level, total station)
Digital torque logger
Pile extension sections (1.5m / 2m)
PPE: helmet, steel-toe boots, gloves, hi-vis vest
3. Installation Procedure
Set out pile grid per foundation drawing
Position excavator and attach torque head
Place first helical pile vertically at marked point
Begin rotation — monitor torque and penetration rate
Add extension sections as needed to reach stable strata
Record final torque when target reached
Cut pile to design elevation
Install pile cap / bracket
Repeat for all piles
4. Quality Control
Torque recorded for 100% of piles
Proof load testing on 2–5% of piles
Depth and elevation verification
Visual inspection of galvanized coating
Level and alignment checks
