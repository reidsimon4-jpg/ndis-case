# Wedding Warehouse - Revised Proposal

**Client:** Tristan  
**Project:** Wedding Warehouse  
**Date:** 22 January 2026  
**Prepared by:** PT Infinity Living Options

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This revised proposal has been prepared following a detailed review of the project budget and the client's target selling price. In order to align the design and construction scope with the approved budget, several elements of the original build strategy have been refined.

The intent of these revisions is to maintain structural integrity, functionality, and overall project quality, while making pragmatic adjustments to materials, scope, and construction approach where appropriate.

---

## BUDGET ALIGNMENT STRATEGY

To achieve the required cost reduction and deliver the project within budget, the design and construction team undertook a **value-engineering exercise**. This process focused on identifying elements that could be modified or optimized without compromising safety, performance, or long-term usability of the building.

The key outcome of this exercise is a revised scope that balances cost efficiency with a clean, functional, and durable finished product.

---

## SUMMARY OF KEY CHANGES

### Exterior Wall Cladding
The exterior cladding system has been revised from the original specification to a more cost-effective solution. This change reduces overall material and installation costs while still providing adequate weather protection and a suitable external appearance for the building.

### Civil Works Adjustments
Civil works have been reduced and rationalised to suit the revised exterior cladding and building envelope strategy. These changes optimise excavation, foundation interfaces, and external works, contributing to overall cost savings while maintaining compliance with structural requirements.

### Doors and Windows
Doors and windows have now been formally included within the scope. Their inclusion provides clarity on the full building package and ensures the project pricing reflects a more complete and coordinated delivery.

### Price Reductions Across Multiple Elements
A number of individual line items across structural, architectural, and MEP works have been reviewed and adjusted. These reductions were achieved through:
- Material substitutions
- Scope efficiencies
- Construction sequencing improvements

All aimed at supporting the overall budget without undermining build quality.

---

## REVISED SCOPE OF WORKS OVERVIEW

The revised scope includes, but is not limited to:

- Preparation works and site setup
- Optimised civil works and foundations
- Concrete works
- Structural framing
- Revised exterior wall cladding
- Flooring, stairs, and roofing
- Fixings and fastenings
- Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing installations
- Doors and windows

This scope reflects the updated design intent and coordinated pricing structure.
