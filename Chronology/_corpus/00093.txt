Proposed banning order

| have formed the view that it may be appropriate in all the circumstances to make a banning order
prohibiting Exact Financial Solutions (PM) Pty Ltd for a period of ten (10) years from being involved

Basis for making a banning order

Based on the matters listed above, my preliminary view is that the banning order should prohibit
Exact Financial Solutions (PM) Pty Ltd from being involved in the provision of services and supports
to people with disability for a period of ten (10) years.

The conduct set out above, engaged in by V REID and R ALAM, persons who have authority or
responsibility for (or significant influence over) planning, directing or controlling the activities of Exact
Financial Solutions (PM) Pty Ltd, strongly suggests that Exact Financial Solutions (PM) Pty Ltd is not
suitable to provide supports or services to people with disability for the following reasons:

Section 3 of the NDIS Act contains the objects of the NDIS Act, which includes:

°

supporting the independence and social and economic participation of people with
disability; ‘

enabling people with disability to exercise choice and control in the pursuit of their
goals and the planning and delivery of their supports;

promoting the provision of high quality and innovative supports that enable people
with disability to maximise independent lifestyles and full inclusion in the
community; and

protecting and prevent people with disability from experiencing harm arising from
poor quality or unsafe supports or services provided under the NDIS.

In giving effect to the objects of the NDIS Act:

ie}

section 3(3)(b) of the NDIS Act requires regard to be had to the need to ensure the
financial sustainability of the NDIS. Similarly, as set out in section 4(17) of the NDIS
Act, it is the intention of Parliament that the Commissioner, amongst others, have
regard to the need to ensure the financial sustainability of the NDIS in performing
functions and exercising powers under the NDIS Act;

sections 4(4) and 4(6) of the NDIS Act, provide principles guiding actions under the
NDIS including:

= People with disability should be supported to exercise choice, including in
relation to taking reasonable risks, in the pursuit of their goals and the
planning and delivery of their supports; and

o VREID and RALAM have deliberately engaged in and directed Forward Focused Group
Pty Ltd staff to engage in aggressive and dishonest conduct, including the submission
of falsified claims for supports and services against NDIS plans, driving a culture of
inflating claims and hours for services delivered, authorising payments without
participant consent and entering into new service agreements without the knowledge
or consent of NDIS participants and their nominees; and

o The conduct set out above and the offences for which V REID has been charged,

participants, as victims of fraud. Such conduct takes funds from NDIS participant
plans, stopping participants from being able to purchase the supports and service they
genuinely need. Further, fraud makes it harder for participants to pursue their goals
and outs additional pressures on carers, family, friends and support networks.

My view is that is that the need to protect the interests of people with disability, who may be
particularly vulnerable, and ensure that they are kept safe, requires that in matters of this kind, a
provider with key personnel identified as engaging in such conduct, including fraudulent activity, in
contravention of the NDIS Act must be prevented from delivering supports and services to people with
disability. My preliminary view is therefore that Exact Financial Solutions (PM) Pty Ltd is not currently
suitable to provide supports or services to people with disability.
