                                                                           Project Overview
Thank you for the opportunity to provide a proposal for the Light Gauge Steel (LGS) bathroom pod
structures. Based on the referenced design, we propose the fabrication and supply of prefabricated
LGS frames fully cladded with 9mm calcium silicate board, ready for the next stages of waterproofing,
tiling, and internal fit-out.

Prefabricated pods offer improved construction speed, consistent quality, and reduced on-site labor,
helping streamline the overall project timeline.




                                                                             Project Phasing
To support proper evaluation and ensure the system aligns with project expectations, we propose
delivering the works in two structured phases:


Phase 1 – Mock Ups

Quantity: 1 Unit (for one building)

This phase allows the project team to review build quality, dimensions, installation methodology, and
overall suitability before proceeding with full-scale production.




Phase 2 – Main Contract

Quantity: 28 Units (serving 14 buildings)

Following approval of the mock-up unit, we will proceed with full fabrication to maintain consistency
across all buildings while benefiting from production efficiency.

                                                                              Scope of Work
Our scope includes the following:

   ●​ Supply of Light Gauge Steel (LGS) structural framework
   ●​ Full external cladding using 9mm calcium silicate board
   ●​ Factory fabrication for improved accuracy and finish
   ●​ Preparation for easy on-site installation

Exclusions (unless otherwise agreed):

   ●​ Waterproofing
   ●​ Floor screed
   ●​ Plumbing and electrical
   ●​ Tiling and finishes
   ●​ Fixtures and sanitaryware
   ●​ Installation on site
