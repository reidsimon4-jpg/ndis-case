Appendix — parallel Privacy Act requests (optional, cheap)
For Simon’s and Vida’s own personal information, a request under Australian Privacy Principle 12 to each agency’s privacy team runs in parallel to FOI, under a different exemption regime and often faster. Short template:
“I request access to all personal information you hold about me under Australian Privacy Principle 12 and the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Please confirm the timeframe and any verification you require. This request is in addition to, and not a substitute for, any request under the FOI Act.”
Send to the NDIA and NDIS Commission privacy teams; pair with Emails 2 and 3.
