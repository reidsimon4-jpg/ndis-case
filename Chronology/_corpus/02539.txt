   A.   SCOPE OF WORK
        This scope of work provided by TRES ARCHITECT covers architectural design
        only starting from design development phase up to working drawing phase.

   B.   SCOPE OF SERVICES
        1. Design Development Phase
           Based on the approved layout concepts prepared design developments to
           include all firm dimensions, profiles etc., to suit approved area in respective
           buildings.
           Deliverables:
           - Image moodboard,
           - 3D concept report for owner’s review,
           - 3D realistic visual.

C.    TIME FRAME
      The length of time required to complete the concept design and plan is to be no
      longer than 3 months.

D.    BASIC COMPENSATION
      The basic compensation for providing the services as outlined in the

E.     TERMS OF PAYMENT
     1. Down payment: 30% or IDR 24.300.000 of the total fees shall be paid after
        signing the contract.
     2. Termyn I: 40% or IDR 32.400.000 the total fees shall be paid after development
        design work finished.
     3. Last payment: 30% or IDR 24.300.000 of the total fees shall be paid after all
        working drawings are finished.

F.    EXCLUSIONS
      The following services are not included in the scope of work:
      a. Project management
      b. Engineering design and technical specification including mechanical/ electrical,
         structural and civil design.
      c. Landscape design.
      d. Interior and furniture design.
      e. Special lighting design.
      f. Documents/materials required for design for purpose (e.g. mapping, measuring,
         topographical survey, soil investigation, pilling test, etc).
      g. Fees associated with building permit process, including expediting fees.
      h. Site supervision.
      i. Supervision of the building construction shall be done by a supervision team
         hired by owner.
      j. Fees associated with building permit process, including expediting fees.
