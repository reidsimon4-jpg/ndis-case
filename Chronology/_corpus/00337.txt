Bottom line
The FOI release confirms you were right: there were real breaches, and several were
never addressed. The single strongest point for the ART is that the factual foundation of
Vida's ban has collapsed: the internal review expressly sized the ban to the criminal case,
and that case was withdrawn by the CDPP in Feb 2024.

The release (15 documents, about 1,930 pages, all read, scanned pages OCR'd) supports
five lines of attack:

 1. Foundation gone. The ban term (to 21 Dec 2027) "corresponds with the amount of time
      it is reasonably expected to take for the criminal proceedings to be finally determined"
      (doc 11, PDF p33, para 63). Those proceedings ended in Feb 2024 (doc 2 p74). On merits
      review the ART decides on the facts as they stand now.
 2. Pre-determination and bias in the Commission's own brief. The Executive Brief says
      the Commission "should be prepared for a retaliatory response" after Vida met Federal
      Ministers, and that inaction "may reflect poorly on the Commission" (doc 11 pp166, 186).
      The same brief describes the complaints as "administrative and personal errors"
      (p166).
 3. An intelligence-led, coordinated case. The NDIA "Project Archer" report (18 Aug
      2022), Commission requests for AFP criminal histories (23 Aug 2022) and witness
      statements captioned "R v Vida Reid" from 11 Oct 2022, eight days before the charge.
      The Archer report itself is still withheld.
 4. Disclosure failures, some admitted. AGS admitted in writing that documents were
    "inadvertently omitted" and "should have been provided previously" (May 2026), and
      that the first bundle held 3 documents "confidential to another criminal investigation"
      (Aug 2023). 343 pages remain withheld on a criminal-proceedings ground that no
      longer exists.

 5. Tribunal process failures. The privacy breach (the Commission sent your confidential

Effect on the broader ART case: points 1 and 2 go to the merits (the correct or preferable
decision now). Points 3 and 4 support applications to lift the confidentiality orders and to
compel full s37 disclosure. Point 5 is mainly evidence for complaints and compensation,
and context for any future appeal on procedural fairness. The compensation avenues are
in section 7.
