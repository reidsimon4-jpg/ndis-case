4. The AI practice direction: what you must do
It applies to every party, including self-represented parties, from 20 August 2026.
Before you sign, personally check every quotation, date and reference against its source, using the Source Check Sheet. Having one AI check another AI's work doesn't count.
Disclosure: it is mandatory where AI affects evidence, such as summarising documents a witness relies on. For submissions it is optional but recommended, so v6 includes one line. Keep that line only if you have actually done the checks.
Your evidence (SFIC facts and witness statements) must be in your own words. AI must not add to, exaggerate or change evidence (para 3.9).
Don't put protected material into a public AI tool unless you are satisfied it is lawful and consistent with every obligation (para 3.20). That covers T-documents, participant details, documents under non-disclosure orders and privileged material. Check each AI tool's data and training settings.
If the Tribunal asks how a document was prepared, answer truthfully. In a decision on the papers it can require disclosure (para 3.16).
