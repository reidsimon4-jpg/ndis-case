The NDIS and this Service Agreement
This Service Agreement is made for the purpose of providing support services
(Supports or Services) under the Participant’s National Disability Insurance
Scheme (NDIS) Plan.

The Parties agree that this Service Agreement is made in the context of
the NDIS, which is a scheme that aims to:

¢ Support the independence and social and economic participation of people with a disability; and

¢ Enable people with a disability to exercise their choice and control in pursuit if their goals, and the
planning and delivery of their supports.

The terms and conditions contained in this Service Agreement (as varied from time to time in writing) will
apply to any Services provided by FFG.

Glossary of Terms

FFG means the provider of Services under this agreement (otherwise known as we or us).
NDIA means the National Disability Insurance Agency.

Service Agreement means this agreement and any attached documents or schedules.

You means a person who is a receiver of Services or a person such as a family member or friend who has
authority to act on behalf of the service recipient under the National Disability Insurance Scheme, as the
context requires.

1. FFG’s responsibilities

We will use our best efforts to:

(a) Provide the Services described in clause 4 of this Service Agreement, and/or the attached Schedule of
Services, as varied from time to time;

(b) Plan with you for the provision of the Services as outlined in your NDIS plan;
(c) Consult with you about how Services will be provided and times for these Services;
(d) Review the provision of your Services quarterly;

(e) Communicate any changes, and discuss with you any barriers, to delivering any Supports or Services as per
the plan, including those outside of our control;

(f) Provide you with information about our procedure for the reporting and management of complaints;

(g) Provide you with details of our Cancellation Procedure — See Point 6.

(h) Provide a minimum of 24 hours’ notice where possible, when we have to change a scheduled appointment
to provide the Services;

(i) Comply with all rules and regulations regarding the privacy of your personal information;
(j) Keep accurate records on the Services provided to you;

(k) We only work 9 am to 5 pm Monday to Friday. We will not respond to sms or calls after or before these
hours.
