DISCOVERY PLAN & CONNECTION MAP — “WHY NURSE ASSIST 24/7?”
Companion to Reid v NDIA — working memo to brief counsel — 14 June 2026
Status and caveat.  Working analysis to brief counsel; not legal advice and not for filing. Its purpose is to frame the single most important discovery target: why an NDIA officer steered the Third Applicant’s participants to Nurse Assist 24/7 / Diversitas WA, and what connection existed. Throughout, [DOCUMENTED] marks facts with a primary record in hand; [ALLEGED — to establish] marks matters that discovery is sought to prove or disprove. The applicants do not assert the connection as proven; they identify a documented basis to investigate it. Nothing concedes any fraud or wrongdoing by any applicant; individuals are not asserted to be corrupt — these are questions for the Court’s process.
A.  The six questions discovery must answer
The improper-purpose case turns on whether the NDIA’s choice of destination provider was a bona fide, arm’s-length safeguarding decision, or a steered outcome favouring a connected and conflicted competitor. Discovery is directed at six precise questions:
Who, within the NDIA, decided that the Third Applicant’s participants should be moved, and to which providers — and on what recorded criteria?
Why Nurse Assist 24/7 and Diversitas WA specifically, when each had its own legal/compliance issues and was staffed or advised by the Third Applicant’s former personnel and their associates?
What communications passed between Mr Halls (or any NDIA officer) and Nurse Assist 24/7, Diversitas WA, Ms Tear, Ms Damon, Ms Roberts or Mr Rossouw, before and during the exit?
What was the employment/association history between Mr Halls and Ms Tear within the NDIA (to test the asserted prior connection)?
What did the NDIA and the Commission know, at the time, about Nurse Assist’s and Diversitas’s own compliance/registration/legal status?
Were any conflict-of-interest declarations made (or required and not made) by Mr Halls in relation to the providers he selected?
B.  The connection map (documented vs to-establish)
The actors and the links presently on the record. The map is the reason the discovery is targeted, not a fishing expedition.
C.  Why this is not a fishing expedition (the documentary predicate)
A Registrar will ask what reasonable basis supports the requests. The basis is specific and on the record:
The NDIA itself named the destination. Mr Bhandari’s 17 Oct 2022 email cancels FFG’s role and states “I have identified Diversitas WA Pty Ltd… Sukhi or Adrian… is calling to you” — the Agency chose the competitor (EM-030).
The officer described his own role as orchestration. Mr Halls, 29 Sep 2022: “I am leading a coordinated provider exit on behalf of the NDIA” (EM-029).
The destination was conflicted. Nurse Assist 24/7 was advised by a former FFG Service Manager (Ms Roberts) who had been in a legal dispute with FFG, and whose partner (Mr Rossouw) was a Commission complainant — i.e. the beneficiary provider and the complainant pool overlap.
A specific, named allegation of connection exists. A participant (Mr Shapiera) reported, contemporaneously, that Ms Tear claimed to “know Nathan Halls… very well” and to be moving FFG’s clients with his help — enough to warrant testing, not to assert as fact (EM-031, EM-032).
The destination had its own legal baggage. A 31 Jul 2023 report records a “Nurse Assist” vehicle following Ms Reid; and the Commission’s own infringement list records notices against a “Nurse Assist Pty Ltd” entity (ABN to be confirmed) — going to whether the NDIA steered participants to a troubled provider.
D.  The production / discovery schedule
Vehicles: ART production duties (ART Act ss 23–28) and summons (s 74) within the Tribunal; in the Federal Court, FCR r 7.23 (preliminary discovery), r 20.15 (category discovery once on foot) and r 20.23 (non-party discovery) against Nurse Assist 24/7 and Diversitas; plus targeted FOI. Each request is tied to one of the six questions.
E.  Preservation and sequencing
Issue preservation notices now to the NDIA, the NDIS Commission and (via solicitors) to Nurse Assist 24/7 and Diversitas WA — emails, Teams/chat, mobile messages, call/case notes, CRM and allocation records, audit logs, and metadata. Fast-rotating logs are a reason to act today, not an excuse.
Sequence: inside the ART, serve the s 74 summons / production for items 1, 5 and 8 (the records the Commission/NDIA hold); in parallel prepare the s 39B proceeding so r 20.15 and the r 20.23 non-party requests (items 2, 3, 6) can issue once on foot. Move first to discharge/qualify the 26 July 2024 non-disclosure orders so the Project Archer / TIR provenance can be used.
Build the “source-is-the-beneficiary” point as the spine: the providers that received the participants (Nurse Assist/Diversitas) and the witnesses who supplied the Commission’s case (Roberts/Rossouw and associates) overlap — which, if the documents bear it out, converts “the NDIA protected participants” into “the NDIA delivered them to the very people running the campaign against FFG.”
F.  Discipline and caveats
Disambiguate the two “Amandas.” Amanda Damon (Nurse Assist) and Amanda Manni (Diversitas) may be different people; pin identity before pleading.
Pin the Nurse Assist ABN. Infringement notices on the Commission’s list are against a “Nurse Assist Pty Ltd” — confirm the ABN matches the 24/7 entity before relying on them.
Frame Roberts’ departure neutrally. The “drug-use” disciplinary characterisation is the applicants’ account; rely on the documented dispute (DFG Legal correspondence) and the employment record, not the characterisation.
Connection is to be established, not assumed. The Halls↔Tear “worked together / close friends” claim is currently a reported assertion; an earlier review noted public profiles did not show shared employment — discovery (item 3) is precisely how to resolve it. Do not plead the friendship as fact.
Participant PII. Name participants only where unavoidable and under confidentiality; the point is the pattern, not the individuals.
No fraud conceded. Nothing here concedes any wrongdoing by any applicant; the Commission’s case is contested in its entirety.
