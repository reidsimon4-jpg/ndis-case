Post-legal advice considerations of Evidence, information and considerations.

1. Summary of tip-Offs
   1.1 The NDIA received fifty-seven tip-offs between 3 February 2021 and 16 August
       2022, alleging:
      a. Serious allegations including intimidating and provoking participants.
      b. Claiming for services not provided.
      c. Directing staff to create false invoices.
      d. Overcharging for services.
      e. Misuse of funds – claiming for services from incorrect line items and supports
         not reasonable and necessary.
      f. Forgery – cutting and pasting participant/nominee signature onto service.
         agreements and forgery of a signature
      g. Bullying and harassment of participants and staff.
      h. Exhausting funds from expired plans.
      i. Participant neglect – required supports were not provided.

2. Company structure
   2.1 In an email dated 31 May 2022 (Att 15) the Founding Executive Director of
       Forward Focused Group advised that at the end of 2021 the executive team
       comprised of “Simon, Rejaul and I”. It includes information about new staff being
       brought in and an attached file to show the company future.
   2.2 The email is issued to “ffg.net.au and hybridhive.com.au” email addresses.
   2.3 The attached file (Att 16) shows Parent Company “Forward Focused Group” with
       linking arrows to four sub groups including: Forward Focused with I [heart] NDIS
       logo, and The Hive Support Services with “The Hive” logo.
   2.4 Based on this and additional information provided by staff about access to
       accounts of both Forward Focused and The Hive it is reasonable to conclude
       that the entities the Commission knows as Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd (Att
       17), Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd (Att 18) form part of “Forward Focused Group”.
   2.5 The news section of the Forward Focused Group website includes a note about
       the expansion into employment services under the banner of “Workforce
       Australia Employment Services” which is included on the company subgroups
       (Att 16)
   2.6 The Forward Focused Group website links to a Facebook page (Att 33).
   2.7 Service Agreements for participant and provider “Forward Focused Group”
       (Att 41 and Att 46) includes no ABN, ACN or registration number.
   2.8 Emails of 23 June 2022 shows email from Teams Coordinator @hybridhive
       email address to both @hybridhive.com.au and @ffg.net.au staff including
       Rejaul Alam and Vida Reid.

   Given the information presented it is reasonable to conclude that Forward Focused
   Group (FFG) is a catch all term for the companies which include the known entities
   referred to as Forward Focused and The Hive.


3. Companies interrelated
The information available both publicly and provided to the Commission demonstrates
that Forward Focused Group (FFG) is a name-only entity led by Vida Reid, Simon Reid
and Rejaul Alam. The name-only entity has a number of businesses including the NDIS
registered Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd also known as “Forward Focused”, and the
non-registered entity Hybrid Hive also known as The Hive.
