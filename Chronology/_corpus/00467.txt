Dear Registrar
I am the Second Applicant. I write to draw to the Tribunal's attention four apparent errors in the text of the decision and statement of reasons published on 31 July 2026, and respectfully to invite the Tribunal to consider exercising the power conferred by section 114 of the Administrative Review Tribunal Act 2024 (Cth) to alter the text.
I do not by this letter seek to reopen or challenge any part of the Tribunal's decision. Each matter identified below is a matter of transcription or of the record, and in each case the source document is identified so that the position can be checked.
1.  The date in the decision
The decision records: "The Tribunal dismisses the applications to sever the applications and affirms the original order of 19 September 2023."
Paragraph 14 of the reasons records that the order joining the three applications was made by Deputy President Mischin on 19 July 2023. The Respondent's submissions of 17 September 2025 state at paragraph 52 that "The Tribunal made orders to have the three matters joined and heard together on 19 July 2023." The reference to 19 September 2023 appears to be an error for 19 July 2023.
2.  Tribunal file numbers in orders A and C
Orders A and C at paragraph 40 refer to application "3023/4494". The correct number is 2023/4494, as it appears in the heading to the decision and in order D.
3.  Paragraph 10 — the operative period of the Second Applicant's banning order
Paragraph 10 records: "The operative period of the ban including the original decision was from 22 December 2022 until 22 December 2025."
The notice of internal review decision in respect of the Second Applicant (Commission references IR (7) 22/23 and ICM23/100) records that the original decision of 21 December 2022 imposed a banning order "for a period of 5 years under section 73ZN(2)(a)(iii)", that the original order "continues to have effect from 5pm on 21 December 2022 (AWST)", and that:
"The variation takes effect from 12.00am on 22 April 2023 until 12.00am on 22 December 2027 (AWST)."
That is also consistent with paragraph 9 of the reasons, which quotes the varied order as prohibiting the Second Applicant from involvement in the NDIS sector "for a period of five (5) years". The banning order made against the Second Applicant remains in force. As presently drafted, paragraph 10 records that it expired on 22 December 2025.
4.  Paragraph 11 — the date of the Third Applicant's original banning order
Paragraph 11 records that the original banning order in respect of Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd was made on 22 December 2022. The notice of internal review decision in respect of that company (Commission references IR (10) 22/23 and ICM23/198) refers to "the decision made on 20 December 2022 to impose a banning order against your client, Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd, for a period of 10 years under section 73ZN(1)(a)". That date is consistent with the order quoted in paragraph 11 itself, which takes effect from 20 December 2022.
Request
I respectfully request that the Tribunal consider altering the text of the decision and the statement of reasons in respect of each of the four matters above. I have copied this letter to the solicitors for the Respondent and to the other Applicants.
