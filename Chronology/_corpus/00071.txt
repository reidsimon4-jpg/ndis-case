Conclusion:

FFG has been the victim of a coordinated and malicious effort led by key individuals
within the NDIA to damage its business, reputation, and financial stability. The
malicious prosecution and harassment by the NDIA, facilitated by staff members with
personal vendettas, led to Vida’s wrongful arrest and significant financial and emotional
hardship for both her and the business. The complete exoneration of Vida in February
2024 highlights the groundless nature of the accusations against her.

This brief seeks legal redress for the malicious prosecution, with a focus on recovering
damages and holding the involved parties accountable for their actions. Supporting
documentation, witness statements, and communications are available to substantiate
these claims.
