Proposed banning orders against providers
15. Section 73ZN(1)(b) of the NDIS Act relevantly provides that the Commissioner may, by written
    notice, make an order (a banning order) prohibiting or restricting specified activities by an NDIS
    provider, or by a person who was an NDIS provider, if the Commissioner reasonably believes that
    the person:
         •    has contravened, is contravening, or is likely to contravene the NDIS Act; or
         •    is not suitable to provide supports or services to people with disability, having regard to
              any matters prescribed by the National Disability Insurance Scheme rules for the
              purposes of this subparagraph.
16. Section 73ZN(2A) of the NDIS Act relevantly provides that the Commissioner may, by written
    notice, make an order (a banning order) prohibiting or restricting a person from being involved
    in the provision of specified supports or specified services to people with disability if:
         •    the Commissioner reasonably believes that the person is not suitable to be so involved,
              having regard to any matters prescribed by the National Disability Insurance Scheme
              rules for the purposes of this paragraph; and
         •    the person has not previously been an NDIS provider; and
         •    the person has not previously been employed or otherwise engaged by an NDIS
              provider; and
         •    the person has not previously been a member of the key personnel of an NDIS
              provider.
