Allegations
1. It is alleged that Mr Reid is not suitable to be involved in the provision of supports or services to
   people with disability on the basis that Mr Reid, a person who has authority or responsibility for
   (or significant influence over) planning, directing or controlling the activities of Hybrid Hive Pty
   Ltd (Hybrid):
    1.1. Has authority or responsibility for (or significant influence over) planning, directing or
         controlling the activities of Hybrid as a Director, an entity under which fraudulent claims for
         services were submitted to the NDIS; and
    1.2. Mr Reid is a joint signatory to the bank accounts of FFG and Hybrid, to which monies for
         fraudulent claims were paid.

Compliance advice
7. Section 73ZN(2)(a)(iii) of the NDIS Act relevantly provides that the Commissioner may, by written
   notice, make an order (a banning order) prohibiting or restricting specified activities by a person,
   if the Commissioner reasonably believes that the person is not suitable to provide supports or
   services to people with disability.
8. As Branch Head NCW, you are delegated in accordance with the Instrument No.2 of 2022, to
   make decisions under section 73ZN of the NDIS Act.
9. It is considered that it is open to you to make banning orders in respect of Mr Reid, on the basis
   that:
    9.1. He is a person who is in a position of responsibility and authority over Hybrid, an entity for
         which significant fraud against the NDIS has occurred; and
    9.2. He is a joint signatory to the bank accounts to which monies paid as a result of fraudulent
         claims submitted by FFG and Hybrid were paid, and has likely benefited as a result.
    Further details of the proposed basis for this banning order is contained in Attachment A.
10. As noted above, section 73ZN(7) requires the Commissioner to give the person an opportunity to
    make submissions to the Commissioner on the matter. On 17 November, you issued an intention
    to make a banning order to Mr Reid (Attachment B), fulfilling this requirement.
    10.1. On 22 November 2022, Mr Reid, through his legal representatives, issued a letter is
          response, requesting an extension of time.
    10.2. On 30 November 2022, Mr Reid, through his legal representatives, issued a letter
          responding to the intention notice issued on 17 November 2022 (Attachment C). The letter
          makes submissions that are addressed in Attachment A.
    10.3. On 2 December 2022, you issued a response to Mr Reid, providing an extension of time,
          being to 14 December 2022 (Attachment D).

11. Section 73ZN(9) requires you, as a delegate of the Commissioner, to:
    11.1. give the banning order to the person against whom the order is made; and
    11.2. include in the banning order a statement of reasons for the order.
    The banning order at Attachment A meets these obligations.
12. Further, it is open to you to determine the period in which a banning order is in force. It is
    recommended this be for five years. This time period has been proposed as a proportionate
    period and consistent with the proposed banning order for The Hive Pty Ltd.
13. Dependent on any new evidence obtained and/or a determination of a court, the banning orders
    may be varied or revoked.
14. It is considered that the recommended banning order is consistent with the NDIS Commission’s
    banning order policy. Particularly, the proposed action seeks to mitigate ongoing significant risk
    to people with disability and are preferred as other actions are not appropriate in the
    circumstances. For example, the nature of conduct is not conducive to remedial actions being
    preferred.
