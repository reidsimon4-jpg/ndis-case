# FINAL RESPONSE TO AGS (Hybrid Strategy)

**Rationale:** This version keeps your excellent "staged approach" (which makes you look reasonable and organized to the Tribunal) but injects the **"Killer Evidence"** (admitted lack of charges + witness intimidation) to show *why* the case against Simon is weak and must be resolved first.

---

Dear Ms Dickinson,

Thank you for your letter dated 13 February 2026.

Notably, this is the first occasion on which AGS (and by extension, the Commission) has sought to engage with me directly in a constructive case-management manner, rather than progressing the matter through adverse steps. The timing is noted.

### 1. “No prejudice or loss” is not accepted
I do not accept the assertion that, because the public register now records that FFG’s banning order expired on 20 December 2025, “there can be no relevant prejudice or loss”. Use of the "expired" label does not cure the defect of an unlawful order, nor does it mitigate the indefinite reputational damage caused by its continued publication.

**We therefore decline the invitation to withdraw.**

### 2. The "Trust" Deficit & Threshold Issues
You ask us to rely on the Commission’s administrative processes. However, we must be frank: our trust in the Commission’s bona fides has been severely eroded by recent events which go to the heart of the "threshold issues" in these proceedings:

1.  **Service Failure / Invalidity:** My Banning Order was made on 21 December 2022. I have since obtained proof from Australia Post that the "Show Cause" notice (dated 17 Nov) was not delivered until **22 December 2022**. This means I was banned *without* being afforded the opportunity to respond—a fundamental denial of procedural fairness that likely renders the decision void *ab initio*.
2.  **Contradictory Admissions:** In the Respondent's own submissions (17 Sep 2025, Para 47), your client admitted *"There is no evidence of any criminal charges at this stage"*. Yet, the bans against myself and Vida Reid remain in force, despite their duration being explicitly "pegged" to those now-non-existent proceedings.
3.  **Witness Concerns:** We are aware that in January 2025—despite your client’s submission that "no investigation" was on foot—Commission investigators attended the home of a former employee, Ms Virginie Fanny-Dulloo, and allegedly engaged in conduct that we consider coercive.

These matters raise serious questions about whether the Respondent has properly articulated a case that can be fairly met, or if these proceedings are being maintained for an improper purpose.

### 3. Staged Approach: Simon Reid First
To narrow the issues without abandoning our rights, we propose a staged determination:

**Priority 1: Simon Reid (ART 2023/3363)**
This matter must be listed first because:
1.  My banning order remains in force.
2.  The Respondent has **failed to file a responding SFIC** despite my SFIC being filed on 15 July 2024.
3.  The case against me is demonstrably weak given the admission of "no evidence of charges" and the lack of any brief provided to the DPP (as admitted in your Reply of 28 Feb 2024).

**Priority 2: Vida Reid (ART 2023/3361)**
To be listed following the outcome of my matter.

**Priority 3: Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd (ART 2023/4494)**
To be stood over pending the individual determinations.

### 4. Directives Sought for Case Management
If your client agrees to this staged approach, we ask that you consent to orders at the hearing on 20 March 2026 requiring the Respondent to:
(a) **File its overdue SFIC** for Simon Reid immediately;
(b) **Produce source materials:** Specifically, the interview transcripts and primary documents identified in my SFIC which have been withheld; and
(c) **Particularise the case:** Identify exactly *what* conduct is alleged against me personally, given the collapse of the criminal narrative.

### 5. Reservation of Rights
For the avoidance of doubt, this proposal is for case management efficiency only and does not constitute a waiver of any rights or an agreement to withdraw the FFG application. We expressly reserve our rights regarding the conduct of the investigation and the validity of the banning orders *ab initio*.

Yours sincerely,

Simon Reid
