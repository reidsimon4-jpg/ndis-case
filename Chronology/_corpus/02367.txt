Joint venture proposal/ business plan between, Forward focused Group, Nurse 24/7 assist and Dr
maina Kava and associates.

We hereby propose to conduct a joint venture arrangement between the above parties to provide a
holistic care service for NDIS participants. The below will outline the proposed obligations and
benefits provided and received from each party to the agreement. The below does not constitute
any legally binding or executable obligations, it is simply a draft for discussion to help
understanding towards a formal agreement.

Company vision:

We propose to form a company between the three parties, with equal shares beneficially held, with
the aim of conducting business of providing various supports funded under the National disability
insurance scheme. We aim to stand out in the market place by nature of the holistic approach that
we can offer: for example, a participant who has contacted Dr kava or her associates for assessment
or diagnoses, and has a formal NDIS approved plan, can directly through their trusted medical
practitioner, be referred to a service provider that has oversight by the same medical practitioner,
and that medical professional can delegate oversight down to all levels of the support network, from
highly qualified nurse practioners, to speech pathology, to Occupational therapy, down to daily
support workers. This will give the participant a smooth continuation of care not offered by other
providers, and the knowledge that the provider who designs the care program is also instrumental in
proper execution of the plan, and different company methods and interests are not hindering the
participants expected outcomes.

Obligations: We propose the following obligations for the various parties:

Forward focused: Forward focused will employ and train a speech pathologist, an Occupational
therapist and a designated support coordinator for the company. Forward focused will oversee the
maintenance and ongoing training( with the assistance of all parties) of all educational standards
required to maintain allied health credentials. The OT and SP may be a graduate student, Nurse
assist and Dr kava will mentor and supervise their development. Forward focused will provide a
clean company shell with no debt or obligations to conduct operations from within.

24/7 nursing assist: will provide capacity and staffing of suitably qualified and experienced nursing
staff, Registered nurses, Enrolled nursing and and suitably qualified support staff to implement the
strategies and recommendations from Dr Kava and as allowable under reasonable and necessary
supports provided by NDIS or other private funding methods.24/7 will also assist with all training
and upskilling of support staff.

Dr Kava and associates will provide a flow of referrals to the company, based on choice and control,
and the trust they establish with clients in their daily practice. (please provide an estimate of the
potential referrals per month). They will also provide suitable office space in west perth and as
available or required within their capacity to do so, to execute operations from.

ALL parties: will contribute to company incidental startup costs and admin staffing overheads
equally, and have equal say on how much will be spent. All parties will contribute equally to any
further staffing required, such as a psychologist, and contribute equally. This will preferentially be
funded through the company income, though this can be discussed as something to set up as an
upfront cost. All decisions of this nature, and over $1000, are to be agreed up in a full quorum of
shareholders necessary to make an executive decision, as necessary under Australian company law.
Benefits: All parties will hold an equal beneficially held share of ordinary shares. The shares will
entitle all parties to a dividend of profits after expenses and tax. All profits will be paid out as
dividends equally among shareholders, unless an executive decision is made to further the interest
of the company through capital expenditure.



Please advise of your agreement to the general outline of terms, obligations and benefits as stated
above. Please advise of any additional terms you would like discussed, or added. This document will
be used to construct a formal joint venture agreement between the parties listed above.
