The NDIS and this Service Agreement
This Service Agreement is made for the purpose of providing supports under the Participant’s
National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) plan.

A copy of the Participant’s NDIS plan can be attached to this Service Agreement

Both participant and the PHCS agree that this Service Agreement is made in the context of the NDIS,
which aims to:

1. Support the independence and social and economic participation of people with disability.
2. Giving more choices to the participant.

3. Assisting the participant in achieving their goals and taking part in the community, and

4. Enable those with a disability to exercise choice and control in the pursuit of their goals and

the planning and delivery of their supports.

5. Promote independence

Schedule of Supports

Perth Health & Care Services agrees to provide the Participant

With the following supports (circle the relevant support categories)

Accommodation/Tenancy Assistance Group/Centre Activities (plan managed)
Domestic Help Household Tasks

Assist Personal Activities Participate in Community (plan managed only)
Assis-Travel/Transport Daily Living

Maintenance/Gardening

The supports will be provided within the guidelines of Perth Health & Care Services policies and
procedures with high levels of professionalism and compassion.

The supports will be delivered by an approved and “qualified” Perth Health & Care Services
employee, the participant will have an allocated support worker, and if in any circumstance the
support worker is not able to commit to provide the services a casual support worker will fill in. If
either the participant or the support worker do not feel comfortable in providing/receiving the
services, the support worker will be replaced (1 Week notice is needed to allocate new support
worker).

The services will be provided on a regular basis for the life of the service agreement unless the
participant or the provider agree otherwise.

The supports and their prices can be set out in the attached Schedule of Supports (if agreed by
participant). All prices are GST exempt and include the cost of providing the supports as per most
recent NDIS Price guide.

Additional expenses such as travelling will be subject to $0.80 a kilometre, and establishment fee will
be charged as per most recent NDIS price guide for every new service agreement. If the participant
does not have travel budget, PHCS will charge travel in hourly basis to cover the cost of travel (i.e,
km’s, tolls, parking fees etc). Things that are not included as part of a Participant’s NDIS supports are
the responsibility of the Participant / Participant’s representative and are not included in the cost of
the supports. Examples include entrance fees, event tickets, meals, etc.

PHCS Charges for Support

PHCS charges participant at the relevant NDIS price guide, if the NDIS increases the prices for
supports, PHCS will automatically charge in accordance with the “new” and/or updated price guide.
PHCS support charges are a minimum of 2 hours and are charged at 30 mins intervals after the first 2
hours.
