Dear Mr Monks,

Thank you for your letters dated 22 and 30 November 2022 advising that you act for Mr Simon Reid and providing representations and submissions on his behalf. I note that you are seeking additional time to provide submissions in reply to the NDIS Commission’s notice of intention to issue a banning order upon Mr Simon Reid. The notice was issued to Mr Reid on 17 November 2022.

I have considered your request for additional time. I have decided to grant your client an additional fourteen (14) days from the original due date of 5:00pm (AWST) on 30 November 2022. I will accept submissions made by your client on the proposed issuance of a banning order upon Mr Simon Reid, up until 5:00pm (AWST) on 14 December 2022. 

Further, I request that your client makes available, to the NDIS Commission, the evidence held and referenced in your letter of 30 November 2022. The evidence can be provided by way of written submissions and email transmittal to WACompliance@ndiscommission.gov.au. If your client requires an alternative method of providing this evidence, please provide this advice by 7 December 2022 to the above-mentioned email. 

As advised in the notices of intention, no decisions have yet been finalised. The NDIS Commission will closely consider all submissions received prior to this new deadline but, consistent with our original notice, will progress to making final decisions even if submissions are not provided. 

If you have questions about this letter, please email WACompliance@ndiscommission.gov.au.
