             SECOND APPLICANT'S AMENDED RESPONSE AND PROPOSED DIRECTIONS
                          In response to the Respondent's Proposed Minute of Order dated 14 August 2026


 A. SCOPE AND PURPOSE
 1. This document is filed by the Second Applicant, Mr Simon Reid, in ART 2023/3363 only. It does not represent or
     prejudice the positions, privileges, evidence or timetable submissions of the First or Third Applicants.
 2. The Second Applicant does not seek to reopen the severance applications dismissed on 31 July 2026. He accepts that
     the three applications remain associated and are to be programmed and heard together, with evidence in one
     application capable of use in another only subject to relevance and procedural fairness.

 3. The Second Applicant supports prompt production of the complete supplementary tender material and a timetable to
     final hearing. The present two-day estimate should be reserved until the Respondent identifies its witnesses and
     written accounts, the s 70 application is decided, the preliminary statutory issue is framed, and the effect of the First
     Applicant's interpreter request is known. No inference is drawn about the Respondent's intention to call or not call
     any witness.

 B. PRELIMINARY STATUTORY ISSUE
 4. The original order dated 21 December 2022 prohibited the Second Applicant from providing disability supports and
     services to NDIS-funded participants. On internal review dated 21 April 2023, the operative prohibition was
     broadened to "any involvement with or in the NDIS sector, in any capacity", including providing supports or services
     "directly or indirectly", providing labour-hire workers, and providing support, funding, guidance or assistance of any
     form to an NDIS provider.
 5. In Sunflower Care Services Pty Ltd v Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission (No 2) [2025] FCA
      1442 at [127]-[141], the Federal Court addressed the need for a direct statutory connection to specified activities and
      for a banning order to be sufficiently clear to be reasonably capable of obedience.

 6. The Second Applicant does not submit that Sunflower automatically determines every alleged contravention. A
     preliminary determination should identify the operative prohibition for each alleged conduct date, whether any
     impugned wording was authorised and severable, and whether the particularised conduct was legally capable of
     contravention. The factual occurrence of the conduct, and any relevance to current suitability, remain separate
     merits questions.

 7. Because the issue is primarily one of statutory construction, the written instruments and agreed chronology, the
     Second Applicant seeks a staged process: production and particularisation first; then short submissions on a possible
     referral under s 185 of the Administrative Review Tribunal Act 2024 (Cth) (ART Act); and, if no reference is made,
     preliminary determination by the Tribunal under ss 53 and 79 before factual merits.

 C. PRODUCTION, PARTICULARISATION AND EVIDENCE
 8. The pending s 70 application should not prevent immediate service of all non-disputed material. Sections 29 and 30 of
     the ART Act permit disputed documents to be lodged with the Tribunal while the question of party access is decided,
     including where legal professional privilege is asserted.

 9. The reasons of 31 July 2026 record that the Second Applicant was not a director of Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd
     between 3 January 2021 and 1 November 2022. Many identified claim periods fall within that interval. That fact does
     not by itself resolve attribution, but it makes an applicant-specific schedule necessary.
 10. The Respondent should also identify any intimidation or harassment allegation advanced against the Second
     Applicant, each alleged banning-order contravention by date and operative term, and any current investigation
     proposition on which it actually relies. Fresh evidence is not excluded merely because it was not before the original
     decision-maker, but it must be identified in time to answer.

 11. The severance reasons state at paragraph 10 that the Second Applicant's order operated only until December 2025.
     The internal-review instrument records operation until 12:00 am on 22 December 2027 (AWST). The complete
     instruments should be produced and the obvious textual error corrected under s 114 of the ART Act.

 D. DIRECTIONS SOUGHT
 1. Section 70 material and supplementary tender bundle
 Within seven days after these directions, the Respondent must:
      a. confirm that the complete set of documents required by ss 23, 26 and 29 of the ART Act, including an unredacted
          copy of each document subject to the Respondent's application dated 7 May 2026 under s 70, has been lodged
          with the Tribunal;
      b. serve every document not subject to a non-disclosure claim; and

      c. file and serve a document-specific schedule identifying each document withheld from an applicant, the statutory
           and evidentiary basis of the restriction sought, any legal professional privilege claimed, the privilege holder and
           dominant purpose, the confidentiality and waiver position, and whether any non-privileged factual material or
           annexure can be severed and disclosed.
 2. Determination and implementation of the section 70 application
      a. Each affected applicant may file a response to the schedule within seven days after service.
      b. The s 70 application be determined at the next directions hearing or as soon as practicable thereafter, with the
          Tribunal at liberty to inspect the documents privately.

      c. Within three business days after determination, the Respondent serve every document or severable part to which
          access is permitted.

      d. If access to adverse factual material is restricted, the Respondent must not rely on that material against an
           applicant without first providing any fair gist ordered by the Tribunal and a reasonable opportunity to answer it.
 3. Complete instruments and correction of the reasons
 Within seven days after these directions, the Respondent serve on the Second Applicant certified complete copies of the
 original written notice and banning order dated 21 December 2022, the internal-review decision and reasons dated 21
 April 2023, the varied written notice and order, and the delegation relied upon for each decision.

 Upon production of those documents, paragraph 10 of the reasons dated 31 July 2026 be corrected under s 114 of the
 ART Act to record that the varied instrument states an end time of 12:00 am on 22 December 2027 (AWST), unless a party
 identifies a contrary signed instrument within three business days.
 4. Respondent's proposed disposition in ART 2023/3363
 Within 14 days after these directions, the Respondent file and serve a statement identifying:
      a. the precise decision it asks the Tribunal to make under s 105 of the ART Act in ART 2023/3363;

      b. if it seeks a substituted order, its complete operative wording, specified activities, conditions, duration, statutory
           provision and proposed temporal effect;

      c. the version of s 73ZN of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (Cth) (NDIS Act), and each transitional
          provision, said to govern the original decision, internal review and the Tribunal's present decision; and

 7. Investigation or prosecution reliance
 Within the same period, the Respondent state whether it relies in ART 2023/3363 on the existence or continuation of any
 investigation, referral or proposed prosecution concerning the Second Applicant and, if so, identify the source, date and
 substance of the information relied upon, together with the status of any referral so far as known to the Respondent.
 8. Preliminary witness notice
 Within the same period, the Respondent file and serve a preliminary notice identifying each witness it presently intends to
 call, each written statement or personal account on which it intends to rely, whether the maker is presently intended to
 be called, and any known unavailability.
 9. Preliminary issues under ss 53 and 79
 After compliance with Orders 3 to 8, and before the factual merits hearing, the following issues be determined on the
 certified instruments, applicable statutory and transitional provisions, agreed chronology and legal submissions, without
 oral factual evidence:

      a. the exact operative prohibition during each period in which a contravention is alleged;
      b. whether the expressions "any involvement with or in the NDIS sector, in any capacity", "directly or indirectly", and
          the inclusive labour-hire and assistance wording identify specified activities authorised by s 73ZN(2), with the
          required direct nexus and certainty;

      c. if any wording was not authorised, whether it was severable and what complete operative prohibition, if any,
           remained, including the legal status of the original order;

      d. whether the conduct particularised under Order 6 was legally capable of contravening the operative prohibition
          on the alleged date; and

      e. the distinction between the legal capacity for past contravention and the potential use of the underlying conduct
          in the Tribunal's present assessment of suitability and prospective risk.

 10. Section 185 referral process
      a. Within seven days after compliance with Orders 3 to 8, the Second Applicant file proposed questions of law and
          submissions not exceeding five pages on whether, with the President's agreement, the questions should be
          referred to the Federal Court under s 185 of the ART Act.
      b. The Respondent may file submissions not exceeding five pages within a further seven days, and the Second
          Applicant may file a reply not exceeding three pages within a further three business days.

      c. The request be determined before a final factual merits hearing is fixed. If a reference is made, further
          programming be considered after the Federal Court gives directions. If no reference is made, Order 9 be listed for
          preliminary determination as soon as practicable.
 11. Targeted requests for attendance of statement-makers
 Within 14 days after service of the Respondent's Statement of Facts, Issues and Contentions and evidence, the Second
 Applicant may serve a schedule identifying each statement-maker sought for cross-examination, the document and
 disputed proposition, the materiality of testing, and a time estimate. Any dispute about availability or consequence be
 determined consistently with the applicable Practice Direction.
 12. Statements of Facts, Issues and Contentions
 The Second Applicant's updated Statement of Facts, Issues and Contentions and further evidence be due 28 days after the
 latest of:

      a. implementation of the s 70 ruling;
      b. complete compliance with Orders 3 to 8; and
      c. the preliminary decision under Order 9 or, if a reference is made, the Federal Court's answer and any
          consequential Tribunal direction.

 The Respondent file its responsive Statement and evidence within a further 28 days, and the Second Applicant file any
 reply within a further 14 days.
 13. Final hearing estimate and date
 The duration and date of the final hearing be fixed at a further case-management hearing after the preliminary legal
 process, pleadings, witness notices, targeted attendance requests and interpreter arrangements are known. The
 Respondent's proposed two-day estimate is reserved.
 14. Liberty to apply
 The parties have liberty to apply on reasonable notice.
