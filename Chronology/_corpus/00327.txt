Constitution of Tribunal and Referral to Alternative Dispute Resolution
This Direction is given under sections 19A, 19D, 19E and 34A of the Administrative Appeals Tribunal Act 1975 (AAT Act).
1. This Direction applies to the applications and events specified in the attached list for VIC, NSW, SA-NT, QLD, TAS, ACT & WA for the period 30 September
    2024 – 4 October 2024.

2. Pursuant to subsections 19D(1) and 19E(5) of the AAT Act, I revoke any previous direction given under subsection 19A(1) as to the members who are to
    constitute the Tribunal for the purposes of a proceeding in relation to any application specified in the attached list where the hearing of that proceeding has
    not yet commenced.

3. I direct that:

(a) pursuant to paragraph 19A(1)(a) of the AAT Act, the member or members listed in relation to an event, other than an alternative dispute resolution
process or directions hearing, is or are to constitute the Tribunal for the purposes of the relevant proceeding;

(b) pursuant to paragraph 19A(1)(b) and subsection 19E(4) of the AAT Act, if more than one member is listed in relation to an event referred to in paragraph
(3)(a) of this Direction, the member listed first or the presidential member in relation to the event is to preside for the purposes of the relevant proceeding;

(c) pursuant to subsection 34A(1) of the AAT Act, in relation to each application for which an alternative dispute resolution process is listed, the proceeding be
referred for that particular alternative dispute resolution process; and

(d) pursuant to subsection 34A(2) of the AAT Act, the member or officer of the Tribunal listed in relation to an alternative dispute resolution process referred to in
paragraph 3(c)of this Direction is to conduct that alternative dispute resolution process.




Deputy President Britten-Jones
