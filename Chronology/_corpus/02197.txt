B. The preliminary statutory issue
4.       The banning order made against the Second Applicant on 21 December 2022 prohibited
         him from "providing disability supports and services to NDIS-funded participants". On
         internal review on 21 April 2023 the operative prohibition was broadened to "any
         involvement with or in the NDIS sector, in any capacity", including providing disability
         supports and services "directly or indirectly", providing labour hire workers to support NDIS
         providers, and providing "support, funding, guidance or assistance of any form" to an NDIS
         provider.

5.       In Sunflower Care Services Pty Ltd v Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards
         Commission (No 2) [2025] FCA 1442 at [127]–[141], the Federal Court held that an order
         under section 73ZN of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (Cth) must
         prohibit or restrict engagement in specified activities, and must express those activities in
         terms sufficiently clear that the order is reasonably capable of being obeyed.

6.       The Second Applicant does not submit that Sunflower automatically determines every
         issue. A preliminary determination should identify the operative prohibition in force at each
        date on which conduct is alleged, whether the impugned wording was authorised and, if
        not, whether it is severable, and whether the conduct particularised was legally capable of
        contravening the operative prohibition. Whether the conduct occurred, and its relevance to
        present suitability, remain merits questions for the hearing.

7.      The reasons of 31 July 2026 record at paragraph 4 that the Second Applicant held no office
        in Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd between 3 January 2021 and 1 November 2022; he was a
        director of Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd from 17 February 2022. Each claim period identified at
        paragraph 35 falls within that interval. Those facts do not of themselves resolve attribution;
        they are why an applicant-specific schedule is necessary.
