As you know we act for Forward Focused Group Pty and Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd. We write in respect of
your conduct concerning our clients. Your conduct (at the very least) requires explanation.

We are instructed that you have contacted multiple participants who receive services from our
clients with the intention of transferring those clients to another service provider, specifically Sukhi
Tear of Diversitas WA. These actions have been taken with no explanation provided to either of our
clients. It is inappropriate to divert participants away from our clients without any explanation or
notification or opportunity to be heard.

Further, our client has instructed us one of their participants received a telephone call from you
during which you informed the participant that:

3.1 there is a “gag order” in place and that she is not to speak to Forward Focused Group or
their staff about this matter. We are unaware of any Court Orders requiring our clients’
participants not to communicate with our clients. Please provide us with a copy of any such
Orders or statutory power so that we may advise our clients further. If there is no such
order, please explain your conduct;

3.2 Forward Focused Group are fraudulent. We are unaware of any outcome of any
investigation or determination that our clients have been fraudulent nor notification as to this
effect; and

3.3 the NDIA are shutting our clients down. This self-evidently requires explanation.

We are further instructed that you have issued a direction to Assist Plan Managers to refuse
payment of any invoices submitted for payment by our clients. On 4 October 2022 we wrote to the
NDIS (as advised by Karen Ruth of Assist Plan Managers) and yourself requesting an explanation
for this decision and instruction to Assist Plan Managers. On 6 October 2022 we received an email
from Barbara Lundgren of the NDIA stating that “The Agency has not directed Assist Plan Managers
to cease all payments to Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd and Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd.”. However, Assist
Plan Managers maintain that the direction not to pay our clients’ invoices was given by you.
