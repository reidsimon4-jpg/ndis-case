               THIRD APPLICANT'S RESPONSE AND PROPOSED DIRECTIONS
          in response to the Respondent's Proposed Minute of Order dated 14 August 2026

A. Capacity and position
1.       This response states the position of Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd (FFG) in matter
         2023/4494, by Simon Reid, a director of FFG and its authorised representative. A current
         company extract and a signed resolution confirming that authority will be provided to the
         Tribunal.

2.       FFG adopts the procedural directions proposed by the Second Applicant at his directions 1,
         2, 10, 11 and 12 to 15, which apply to all applicants, with the timing in his direction 12
         applying equally to FFG's updated Statement of Facts, Issues and Contentions. It does not
         adopt any factual assertion or applicant-specific relief of the individual applicants. Nothing
         in this response, and no omission from it, is an admission or concession of any fact,
         allegation or contention advanced by the Respondent, whose case is contested in its
         entirety; and nothing in it waives any privilege or objection available to FFG.

B. Expiry, utility and relief
3.       The decision under review is the internal review decision of 8 June 2023, which varied the
         corporate banning order. The order as varied was made under section 73ZN(1)(a) of the
         NDIS Act, prohibited FFG from providing supports or services, directly or indirectly, to NDIS-
         funded participants for three years, and is recorded in the Respondent's correspondence
         and on the register as no longer in force. The recorded expiry dates and times are
         inconsistent as between documents; FFG does not ask the Tribunal to adopt any one of
         them, and seeks the authenticated instruments and register history.

4.       Expiry does not deprive the review of utility. The order remains recorded on the register as
         historical regulatory action; a banning order once in force bears on any future application
         concerning the company or its personnel; and, as set out in FFG's Position Statement of 6
         March 2026, a finding that the order should not have been made bears on the correction of
         that record. FFG seeks a final decision under sections 105 and 108 of the ART Act setting
         aside the decision under review and substituting a decision that no banning order be made,
         with the temporal effect of that decision addressed expressly, and asks that the Respondent
         state what correction, annotation or continuing-publication position would follow from
         such a decision.
C. FFG-specific preliminary question
5.       Whether the expression "providing supports or services, directly or indirectly, to NDIS-
         funded participants" in the varied corporate order identifies specified activities with the
         clarity required by the reasoning in Sunflower Care Services Pty Ltd v Commissioner of the
         NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission (No 2) [2025] FCA 1442 is a question specific to
         FFG's instrument, suitable for determination with the preliminary issues in the Second
         Applicant's direction 9, and capable of resolving or materially narrowing matter 2023/4494.

D. Further directions sought
F1.      Instruments and register history. Within 7 days, the Respondent serve certified or sealed
         complete copies of the original corporate banning notice and order, the internal review
         decision and reasons of 8 June 2023, the order as varied, the instrument of delegation for
         each decision, and the register history recording each commencement, variation and expiry
         entry for FFG.

F2.      Disposition and schedules. By the same dates as in the Second Applicant's directions 4, 5, 7
         and 8, the Respondent provide in respect of FFG and matter 2023/4494: the statement of
         its proposed disposition, including the decision it will ask the Tribunal to make in an
         application concerning an order no longer in force and its position on correction or
         annotation of the register; its position on Sunflower as it concerns the corporate order; its
         statement of any investigation reliance as it concerns FFG; its witness notice; and a
         schedule stating, for every claim or allegation relied upon against FFG, the matters in
         categories (a) to (g) of the Second Applicant's direction 6 as they concern FFG, identifying in
         every case the entity said to have made each claim. No claim be attributed collectively to
         "FFG and Hybrid" without allocation.

F3.      Preliminary question. The question at paragraph 5 above be determined with the
         preliminary issues in the Second Applicant's direction 9.

F4.      Liberty to apply on reasonable notice.
