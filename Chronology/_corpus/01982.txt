    THIRD APPLICANT'S AMENDED RESPONSE AND PROPOSED ADDITIONAL DIRECTIONS
                          In response to the Respondent's Proposed Minute of Order dated 14 August 2026


 A. SCOPE, CAPACITY AND POSITION
 1. This document states the position of Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd (FFG) in ART 2023/4494 only. Subject to the
     pre-filing verification stated above, Simon Reid proposes to act as its director and authorised representative under s
     66(1) of the Administrative Review Tribunal Act 2024 (Cth) (ART Act). If the company has ceased to exist or is under
     external administration, s 84 and representation must be addressed before this response is filed.

 2. FFG does not adopt any factual assertion, concession or applicant-specific relief advanced by either individual
     applicant. In particular, it does not adopt questions concerning s 73ZN(2), an individual's conduct, intimidation
     allegations, privilege or interpreter arrangements.

 3. The decision under review is the internal-review decision dated 8 June 2023 varying the corporate banning order. The
     varied instrument relied on s 73ZN(1)(a) of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (Cth) (NDIS Act) and
     prohibited FFG from providing supports or services "directly or indirectly" to NDIS-funded participants.

 B. EXPIRY, CONTINUING UTILITY AND RELIEF
 4. The order is recorded as no longer in force. The instrument, reasons, correspondence and public register use
     inconsistent expiry dates or times. FFG therefore seeks the complete signed instruments and register history and
     does not ask the Tribunal at this stage to adopt one disputed timestamp.

 5. Expiry does not necessarily remove the utility of the review. Section 73ZDA(4) permits historical information on the
     NDIS Provider Register. The current Provider Registration and Practice Standards Rules require the Commissioner, on
     a future registration application, to consider whether a banning order has ever been in force, and r 16 provides a
     correction-request mechanism for a provider or former provider.
 6. FFG does not contend that a favourable Tribunal decision automatically removes the historical entry. It seeks a final
     decision under ss 105 and 108 of the ART Act setting aside the internal-review decision and substituting a decision
     that the original decision be set aside and no banning order be made, with the temporal effect addressed expressly. It
     also asks the Respondent to state what correction, annotation or continuing-publication position would follow.

 C. FFG-SPECIFIC SUNFLOWER QUESTION
 7. FFG does not contend that the individual-order holding in Sunflower Care Services Pty Ltd v Commissioner of the NDIS
     Quality and Safeguards Commission (No 2) [2025] FCA 1442 automatically determines its s 73ZN(1) case. However, at
     [125]-[131] the Court addressed the need for a corporate order to concern specified activities by the provider and for
     a banning order to be sufficiently clear to be reasonably capable of obedience.
 8. Whether the expression "providing supports or services, directly or indirectly, to NDIS-funded participants" identifies
     sufficiently clear specified activities by FFG is a company-specific statutory question suitable for decision before
     factual merits. Its answer may resolve or materially narrow ART 2023/4494.
