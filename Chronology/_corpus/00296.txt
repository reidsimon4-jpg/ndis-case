BACKGROUND

The NDIS, NDIS Participants and Participant Plans

5.    The NDIS is a Commonwealth Government Insurance Scheme established to provide
      funding to Australians with disabilities. This scheme is administratively managed by the
      National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA). Persons assessed as being eligible to
      participate in the scheme each are given an individual identification number. Participants
      may represent themselves, in the case of children, a nominee or guardian may be
      appointed and in some cases a primary carer will act as the nominee or spokesperson for
      an adult participant.

6.    Participants of NDIS then negotiate with the NDIA to formulate a funding package to
      support the necessary and reasonable needs of the particular individual participant. This
      arrangement between the NDIS and the participant is documented and becomes the
      participant plan. Plans range in duration from three months to two years. In accordance
      with the wishes of the participant, the funds are either NDIS managed, plan managed by
      a nominated registered service provider or self-managed. Funds of self-managed plans
      are released directly to the individual and are out of the control of the Commonwealth.
      Monetary transactions on all other plans are managed through the Public Sector
      Collection and Disbursement System.

7.    The NDIA maintains a Customer Relationship Management (CRM) database that
      records participant plan data.

Service providers

8.    Participants are free to choose companies, businesses or individuals to provide the
      services they require. A facility is available through the NDIS for entities to become
      registered service providers. To achieve a certificate of registration, the applicant submits
      evidence to the Commission of their qualification /capabilities relevant to the services
      they wish to provide. A decision is then made whether or not to accept that entity as a
      registered provider of supports. Registered service providers are recorded on the NDIS
      webpage to assist NDIS participants to locate services and supports. Responsibility for
      the registration of service providers to operate in WA was held by the NDIA until 30
      November 2020. On 1 December 2020, the provider registration process became the
      responsibility of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission (Commission).

9.    Participants are free to choose unregistered service providers if their funding is “plan
      managed”. The provisions for “plan managed” funds are contained in Division 3 of the
      National Disability Insurance Act 2013. Unregistered providers must submit invoices to
      a registered provider who then submit claims directly to the NDIA. The NDIA pays
      funds to the registered provider who then forwards those funds to the unregistered
      provider.
