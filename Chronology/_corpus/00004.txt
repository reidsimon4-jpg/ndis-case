                       IN THE ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW TRIBUNAL
                                       BRISBANE REGISTRY
                                        GENERAL DIVISION
                          ART Nos. 2023/3361, 2023/3363 and 2023/4494
                               MINUTE OF PROPOSED DIRECTIONS
                                Interlocutory Hearing – 20 March 2026


1. Noting that Mrs Vida Reid withdraws her application for a stay of proceedings.
2. ART No. 2023/3363 be case managed independently of ART No. 2023/3361 and not be delayed
    by interlocutory issues arising in that matter.
3. The Respondent produce in ART No. 2023/3363 within 21 days the primary documents identified
    in the Applicant’s short directions submissions, including the original reasons, briefing notes,
    tactical report, Jan Nuttall material, PRODA attribution records, Cassandra Burke annexures,
    and the internal review new-information package.
4. Mr Simon Reid have leave to file supplementary submissions or an amended Statement of Facts,
    Issues and Contentions within 28 days after production in ART No. 2023/3363.
5. The parties in ART No. 2023/3363 file and serve witness statements within 42 days after
    compliance with the production order.
6. ART No. 2023/3361 be severed from ART Nos. 2023/3363 and 2023/4494, or alternatively
    separately programmed.
7. The Respondent file and serve in ART No. 2023/3361 within 21 days the affidavit and targeted
    production identified in Mrs Vida Reid’s short directions submissions.
8. Mrs Vida Reid have leave to file a Statement of Facts, Issues and Contentions or supplementary
    submissions within 42 days after receipt of production in ART No. 2023/3361.
9. A Persian/Farsi interpreter be made available for all future hearings involving Mrs Vida Reid, as
    previously requested.
10. ART No. 2023/4494 remain on foot and not be treated as moot, but be deferred and relisted for
    further case management after the Tribunal has materially progressed or heard ART Nos.
    2023/3363 and 2023/3361.
11. The Tribunal allocate the earliest available substantive hearing dates for ART No. 2023/3363
    and, subject to the above directions, ART No. 2023/3361.
