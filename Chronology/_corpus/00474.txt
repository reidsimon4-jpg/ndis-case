## Read me
How to use this workbook
Disclosure = documents the Commission has not given you or the Tribunal, and what to do about each.
Evidence matrix = every allegation the internal review relied on against you, and what you need to answer it. The Tribunal decides afresh on present suitability, so answering these with documents is what wins or loses the case.
