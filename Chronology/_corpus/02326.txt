Method Statement — LGS Modular Installation
Light Gauge Steel Frame & Envelope Assembly
Version 1.0 | 12 February 2026

1. Scope
Assembly of LGS floor frame onto pile caps
Erection of prefabricated wall frames
Roof frame installation
Wall and roof cladding installation
Door and window installation
MEP rough-in (electrical, plumbing)
2. Work Sequence
Step 1: Install steel floor chassis onto pile caps — bolt to brackets
Step 2: Lay floor panels onto chassis
Step 3: Lift and bolt prefabricated wall panels
Step 4: Install roof frame on top of walls
Step 5: Fix metal roofing — ensure overlap and sealant
Step 6: Install wall cladding
Step 7: Fit doors and windows
Step 8: Run electrical wiring and plumbing
Step 9: Final inspection and handover
3. Tools & Equipment
Impact drivers / power drills
Manual lifting equipment
Spirit level, plumb bob, tape measure
Metal cutting tools
Sealant gun
Full PPE set
4. Safety
Harness mandatory during roof installation
Exclusion zone during panel lifting
Full PPE at all times
Daily safety briefing
