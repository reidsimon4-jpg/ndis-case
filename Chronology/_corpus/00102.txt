## B. THE FORM — correction to earlier working advice

**Use Form 69 — "Originating application for relief under section 39B Judiciary Act 1903" (FCR r 31.11(1)). Not Form 66.**

- FCR r 31.01(1) (Form 66) is confined to applications under **s 11(1) of the ADJR Act** (verified rule text).
- FCR r 31.11(1): "A person who wants to make an application for relief under section 39B of the Judiciary Act 1903 must file an originating application, in accordance with **Form 69**" (verified rule text).
- Sunflower's "Combined originating application for judicial review" combined ADJR s 5 + s 39B claims (joinder permitted: rr 31.11(2), 31.12(1)). The Reids' application is **s 39B only** — the ADJR Act's 28-day window (s 11(3)) closed in 2023, and Sunflower's own ADJR limb was refused for delay + alternative remedy anyway. Joining ADJR adds nothing and invites a leave fight. Do not nominate the ADJR Act anywhere.
- r 31.12(2) permits joining other claims within jurisdiction. Recommendation: **don't.** Keep this proceeding a clean quash application. The misfeasance / interference damages claim (Route C program, drafts of 2 July 2026) is a separate, later proceeding with different evidence, different respondents (NDIA/Commonwealth/officers) and a pleadings track.

Form 69 download: Federal Court forms page (Federal Court Rules 2011 forms).

Grounds held good in Sunflower [125]–[142]: the "directly or indirectly involved in the provision of supports or services" formula is (i) beyond the s 73ZN(2) power and (ii) void for uncertainty; plus Vida's ultra vires s 100 variation grounds.

Filing one is harmless; omitting one risks a requisition. Content: the internal review, the ART proceedings, correspondence with AGS. Twenty minutes' work — do it.

Later fees for budgeting: setting down $3,610; hearing days 2–4 $1,430/day; days 5–9 $2,405/day. **Exemption:** available if the applicant holds a health care card / pensioner concession card / Commonwealth seniors health card, has legal aid, is under 18, detained, or on youth allowance/Austudy/ABSTUDY; separate discretionary financial-hardship exemption (individuals only; assessed on income, expenses, liabilities, assets, per application to a Judicial Registrar). ## F. Risks — stated plainly

1. **The alternative-remedy discretion is the main battleground, and your position is weaker than Sunflower's on this one point.** When Feutrill J decided Sunflower there was no live merits review on foot. You have **three live ART matters over the same orders, plus two reserved ART decisions** (severance; s 70). Expect AGS to run *McGowan v MARA* (cited in Sunflower at [118]): the ART is a "more convenient and satisfactory remedy" — and to seek summary dismissal or a stay of the federal application. Your counters: *Bechara v Bates* via Sunflower [117]–[119] — clear jurisdictional error → the writ issues "almost as of right"; validity of the order is a pure question of law the ART cannot conclusively determine; and the ART's own delay (severance reserved since April, s 70 since May) undercuts "convenient and satisfactory". Budget for this fight before any merits hearing.
2. **Delay.** Orders Dec 2022, varied Apr 2023 — over three years. No statutory time bar under s 39B, but delay is discretionary. The affidavit should explain the sequence: the defect only became identifiable when Sunflower was decided (21 Nov 2025) and matched to the Reid orders' wording (July 2026), with the ART process running throughout.
3. **Pre-filing evidence check — do not skip.** The entire lead ground depends on the operative sealed orders actually containing the "directly or indirectly involved…" formula. The working copies match (both Reid individual bans as varied 22 Apr 2023), but the sealed-copy verification for Vida is flagged as outstanding in the case notes. Verify against sealed copies before lodging.
4. **A win here is a drafting/vires win, not vindication.** Sunflower [134] effectively tells the Commission how to re-issue a valid, properly-worded ban. The value is: invalidity of the operative orders, the gap period, costs leverage, and the knock-on effects in the ART — not the end of the regulatory case. Price it accordingly.
5. **ART interaction.** Quashing the variation decisions reshapes what the ART is reviewing, and you have two decisions reserved. Decide deliberately — before the reserved decisions land — whether to inform the ART of the federal filing. Candour favours disclosure; timing is tactical. Don't let it happen by accident.
