NDIS Provider Register
As set out in section 73ZS of the NDIS Act, the NDIS Provider Register must include certain
information in relation to a person against whom a banning order is in force.
The information that must be included on the NDIS Provider Register is:
e@ The name of the person;
e@ The person’s ABN (if any);
e Information about the banning order.
The Commissioner must not publish a part of the NDIS Provider Register if the Commissioner
considers that its publication would be contrary to the public interest, or the interests of one or
more persons with disability receiving supports or services.
Should | decide to make a banning order against you, information about the banning order will be
included on the NDIS Provider Register and published on the NDIS Commission’s website. You may
