The Tribunal ORDERS that:

1. Pursuant to section 35 of the Administrative Appeals Tribunal Act 1975 (Cth) (AAT
Act) and until further order, the documents required to be provided by the
Respondent under section 37(1) of the AAT Act must not be published to other
than the parties, their representatives, and members of the Tribunal and staff of
the Tribunal, and any person performing services for the Tribunal acting in the
course of their duties.

2. Pursuant to section 37(1AE) of the AAT Act and until further order, by reason of
current criminal proceedings and/or legal professional privilege, the Respondent is
not required to give to the Applicants a copy of the documents filed with the
Tribunal pursuant to section 37(1) of the AAT Act in the bundle marked ‘Index —
Confidential Tribunal Documents’;
