## Decline reasons (Hybrid Hive, SR2022-659MM) — insurer's "Decision" section as quoted by the broker, 11 Mar 2024

Two independent bases:

1. **Policy not triggered.** ProRisk considers Insuring Clause 1.3 (Investigation Costs Cover) and Extension Clause 2.1 (Advancement of Defence Costs and Investigation Costs) "do not apply on the facts and circumstances currently known and so the Policy is essentially not triggered." That position "does not change due to the criminal charges being dropped."
2. **Exclusion 3.1 enlivened.** Even if Insuring Clause 1.3 applied (not admitted), Exclusion Clause 3.1 excludes the claim — "not... by the criminal charges but due to allegations of veiled and direct threats, bullying and harassment by employees and/or officers of Hybrid Hive to NDIS participants in contravention of section 73V of the NDIS Act."

The FFG claim (SR2022-284MM) was denied earlier on the IDR Stage 1/Stage 2 track (denial ~5 Jan 2023; Stage 2 affirmed 13 Feb 2023). Obtain the two IDR letters for the exact FFG reasoning.

## Is it out of time? — depends entirely on the forum

**Short answer: AFCA is largely gone; a court claim is not.**

### AFCA (free external dispute resolution) — LIKELY OUT OF TIME for a fresh complaint
AFCA generally must receive a complaint within **2 years of the IDR response** (or, absent an IDR response, within 6 years of awareness of the loss), subject to a narrow "exceptional circumstances" discretion.

- **FFG (SR2022-284MM):** IDR response 13 Feb 2023 → 2-year window closed **~13 Feb 2025**. A *new* complaint is out of time. BUT case **956418 was lodged in time (Feb 2023)** — so the AFCA route was preserved *if that complaint is still on foot or can be reopened.* **Verify the status/outcome of 956418** — this is the single most important unknown.
- **Hybrid Hive (SR2022-659MM):** if the 27 Feb 2024 declinature was the final IDR response → 2-year window closed **~27 Feb 2026 (already past).** The 6 Mar 2025 draft was within time *if lodged* — but it appears never lodged. Possible lifeline: ProRisk expressly said (1 Mar 2024) it was treating correspondence as an *enquiry, not a complaint*, and there may have been no formal Hybrid Hive IDR response — in which case the 2-year clock may not have cleanly started and the **6-year-from-awareness** limb (→ ~2030) could still be open. Contestable; needs the IDR paper trail.

### Court — NOT out of time (breach of insurance contract)
WA **Limitation Act 2005 (WA) s 13**: 6 years for a contract claim, including a claim on an insurance policy. Time runs (broadly) from the wrongful refusal — accrual date is genuinely debatable and needs an opinion.

- **FFG:** denial ~Jan–Feb 2023 → 6 years → **~Jan–Feb 2029.** In time.
- **Hybrid Hive:** declinature ~Feb 2024 → 6 years → **~Feb 2030.** In time.

So a coverage claim in court against Swiss Re/ProRisk is preserved for years; the free AFCA path is the one slipping away.

## Substantive hook (for the coverage merits, not limitation)
Consider **s 54 Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth):** an insurer generally cannot refuse a claim by reason of an act of the insured/another except to the extent that act caused the loss. Exclusion 3.1 here was enlivened by **unproven "allegations"** of s 73V conduct. There is an argument the insurer cannot decline on mere allegations without establishing the conduct and causal contribution. This is a real merits question for coverage counsel — do not assume the declinature is unanswerable.

The durable option is a **court coverage claim** (in time to 2029/2030) — but only worth funding if the quantum (uninsured defence/investigation costs) justifies it and the s 54 argument is viable. Get a short insurance-coverage opinion before anything. **Keep this entirely out of the banning-order judicial review.**
