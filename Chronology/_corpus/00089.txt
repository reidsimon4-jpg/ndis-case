# Email — NDIS Commission: entity-identity clarification for the 28 Sep 2023 infringement notices

**Send to:** contactcentre@ndiscommission.gov.au  (FOI fallback: foi@ndiscommission.gov.au)
*Confirm the current address on the Commission's "Contact us" page before sending.*

**Subject:** Clarification request — entity identity for six infringement notices dated 28 September 2023 (public infringement notice list)

---

Dear NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission,

I am writing to seek clarification of an entry on the Commission's public **Infringement notice list** (https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/about-us/compliance-and-enforcement/compliance-actions/infringements).

The entry reads:

> "Nurse Assist Pty Ltd — 28 September 2023 — 6 notices. On 28 September 2023, six infringement notices were issued to Nurse Assist Pty Ltd (ABN 59 106 123 773). The decision to issue each of the infringement notices is based on Nurse Assist Pty Ltd having allegedly contravened section 73J of the National Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013."

There appears to be an inconsistency between the **named entity** and the **ABN** recorded against it:

- The Australian Business Register records **ABN 59 106 123 773** as **LEADING BOOKKEEPING SERVICES PTY LTD** (ACN 106 123 773) — a Victorian company that has carried that legal name since **30 January 2014** (formerly "Nurse Assist Pty Ltd" until that date), and whose ABN was **cancelled on 3 January 2024**.
- The entity currently registered as **"NURSE ASSIST PTY LTD"**, and registered with the Commission as an approved NDIS provider (trading as "Nurse Assist 24/7"), is **ABN 21 640 694 060** (ACN 640 694 060) — a Western Australian company.

Because section 73J infringement notices concern a breach of a condition of registration, and the named entity "Nurse Assist Pty Ltd" corresponds, as a current legal name, to ABN 21 640 694 060 rather than to the cancelled ABN 59 106 123 773 shown, I would be grateful if the Commission could confirm:

1. The full legal name, ABN, ACN and NDIS provider registration number of the entity against which the six infringement notices dated 28 September 2023 were in fact issued;
2. Whether ABN 59 106 123 773 (Leading Bookkeeping Services Pty Ltd) was, at the date of issue, a registered NDIS provider; and
3. If the ABN published against this entry is incorrect, whether the Commission will correct the public register accordingly.

I would be grateful for a response within 28 days. If any part of this request is more appropriately dealt with under the *Freedom of Information Act 1982* (Cth), please treat it as such, or advise me so that I may lodge it through that channel.

Thank you for your assistance.

Yours faithfully,
