Hi Terri,

The whole process for complex home modification takes up to 20 hours. Please find the breakdown of hours below.



Currently I have only seen Angela for the initial assessment and no other assessments have been completed. The general process for complex home modification is:
- After the initial visit with the client, the OT and the client will work out where the modifications are required. The OT will then develop a scope of works (SOW) for the
modification.
- The OT would then send the SOW to the builders and arrange the 2-x builder to visit to obtain a quote. The OT has to be present during both the visits.
- After that first visit- generally the SOW often needs to be adapted- so I would adapt the SOW and give it a new date so that I can then send this to the second builder for
their quoting visit.
- After second builder visit, I would make any final changes to the SOW and re-send to both builders ensuring that they quote the most up-to date SOW
- I would start completing the NDIS complex home modification template that you send through.
- Once quotes come back- I would need to check that the quotes and SOW carefully to make sure they match up.
- Once quotes are in- I would finish writing and finalise the CHM application. The quote and the SOW will provide the justification in the application for features/fitting.
- Then send all parties the documents for a signature.

The quote that has been provided by the current builder is not detailed enough for me to complete the CHM application. Furthermore, I only have 7 hours remaining from
the service agreement originally signed which is insufficient to complete the whole CHM process.
