This document sets out the scope and payment schedule for: (A) the concrete pad and (B) the supply and
installation of the LGS frame in accordance with Structural Estimate Ref 34.




1. Scope of Work

1.1 Package A - Concrete Pad (Supply + Install)
•   Foundation design drawings in revit and pdf format,Pre-start confirmation of pad dimensions, levels, and
    set-out based on approved drawings.
•   Site preparation within the pad footprint (clear area, basic trimming, and preparation for sub-base).
•   Supply and placement of compacted sub-base suitable for slab construction (standard site conditions).
•   Formwork to slab perimeter and edge details as required by the approved drawings.
•   Supply and install reinforcement (mesh/bars/chairs) in accordance with the approved
    drawings/specification.
•   Set-out and install cast-in / hold-down locations to suit the LGS base tracks and anchor requirements.
•   Concrete supply, pour, vibration, finishing, and basic curing.
•   Site clean-up of the working area on completion.
•   NOTE: drainage to site will be considered in the design phase and inclusion in this estimate will be based
    upon the final BOQ and site requirements

Exclusions (Package A)
• Rock excavation, dewatering, contaminated material removal, or major import/export of fill.
• Underground services relocations, drainage, plumbing/electrical rough-ins unless documented.
• Permits/approvals, authority fees, and third-party inspections unless stated otherwise.

1.2 Package B - LGS Frame (Design + Supply + Delivery + Install)
Included items per Structural Estimate Ref 34:

•   Design and shop drawing development for the LGS frame package .revit and pdf format
•   Supply of LGS components and fixings as per the bill of quantities (BOQ).
•   Delivery and transport logistics to site.
•   Erection, assembly, and installation of the LGS frame to practical completion.
•   Electrical rough in. per drawings.
