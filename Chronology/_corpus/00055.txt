AFFIDAVIT OF VIDA REID – Supplementary (ABF stop 25–26 September 2024)

1. I am the Applicant in proceeding 2023/3361. I make this affidavit from my own knowledge save where
otherwise stated.

2. On 25–26 September 2024, at Perth International Airport, I, together with my family, was stopped by
Australian Border Force officers upon arrival/departure [select as applicable].

3. One ABF officer stated, in substance, that they were acting on a direction/request from the NDIA/NDIS
to stop and search us on each border crossing. The words used were to that effect.

4. Devices were taken/retained for inspection and later returned on or about [insert date if known].

5. Attached are true copies of the available documents I refer to: (A) boarding passes/itinerary; (B) any
correspondence or receipts regarding device handling/return; (C) photographs or notes taken
contemporaneously.

6. The stop and the statement attributed to NDIA/NDIS caused me concern that investigative activity was
continuing notwithstanding the dismissal of charges on 23 February 2024.

7. I make this affidavit in support of my application for a short adjournment and protective directions, and
for targeted production from the Department of Home Affairs/ABF.
