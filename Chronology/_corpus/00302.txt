   Simon Reid Proposed Banning Order – Response Submission

   We refer to the matter above and your letter dated 17 November 2022 in relation to the Notice of
   intention to make a banning order and make submissions.

   We confirm that we act for Mr Simon Reid (‘Client’) in this matter.

   We refer to our letter dated 22 November 2022 to the Commission seeking an extension of time
   to respond to the proposed banning order considering over 20 complainants and numerous
   allegations that were levelled against our Client.

   Our Client received the abovementioned Notice on 18 November 2022, where the Commission
   provided our Client with a time frame of 12 days to respond to these serious allegations. To date,
   the Commission has not responded to our request for an extension of time.

   Given what is considered an unreasonable timeframe to respond to these allegations, a detailed
   response to the proposed Banning Order has not been possible. As such, the only option available
   for our Client is to provide a response based on a rudimentary review of the available information.
   It is our opinion that the Commission has compromised any procedural fairness afforded to our
   Client and gives the perception that the Commission has already made a determination in this
   matter.

   RESPONSE TO ALLEGATIONS
   Under Relevant concerns, the Commission's letter notes the arrest of Ms Vida Reid and the four
   offences she has been charged with, an overview of the statement of material facts and Ms Vida
   Reid’s bail conditions. Given that our Client has not been charged with any criminal offence, we
   question the relevance of this in relation to our Client’s fitness.

   Our Client rejects and/or denies his involvement in the following allegations contained in the
   Commission’s notice of the proposed five-year Banning Order.

         •     NDIS participants’ plans were claimed against by Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd without the
               participants' knowledge of any service delivery nor with their consent.

         •     Hybrid Hive Pty Ltd submitted falsified and inappropriate claims against the expiring or
               expired NDIS plan of NDIS participants.

responsible for the above allegations.

CONCLUSION

As noted in the Commission’s letter, Ms Vida Reid has been criminally charged with a number of
offences. The evidence to be provided to the Court in that matter will demonstrate that our Client
has not been involved in any behaviour that compromises his integrity. As such, a banning order
prohibiting our Client from being involved in the provision of services and support, both directly
and indirectly, to people with disability should not be considered until the outcome of Ms Reid’s
criminal matters. Any decision to ban our Client is premature and would be procedurally unfair
if Ms Reid is acquitted.
