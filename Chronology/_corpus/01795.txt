### Drafting cautions

- An affidavit gives facts, not legal submissions. Remove the bracketed drafting prompts and any argument before affirmation.
- Do not waive legal professional privilege inadvertently when explaining delay. State non-privileged facts and obtain advice about any limited waiver.
- Authenticate every ART date from the actual order or filing receipt.
- Use the transcript’s exact terms: dismissed for want of prosecution and formally discharged. Do not say acquitted.
- Obtain and exhibit any current CDPP correspondence about whether a recharge decision has been made. Passage of time alone does not establish that prosecution risk has ended.
- Keep the applicants' personal affidavits confined. Prove T65/T65.1, the decision record and the transcript through this affidavit, an agreed court book or another non-applicant witness where possible.
- Prove T6, T9.4, T44, the executive briefs and the review corrections through this affidavit or an agreed court book. Do not require Simon to authenticate or explain their allegations.
- Criminal defence counsel must review the proposed affidavit evidence and cross-examination strategy before filing.
- Interim relief needs documentary proof of present harm or opportunity, not only historical hardship.
