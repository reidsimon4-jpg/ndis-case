## 1. Bottom line

**(2B)/(2C) do not save the 21 Apr 2023 instruments.** Those orders were made under **s73ZN(2)** (person employed/engaged / key personnel — “engaging in **specified activities**”). The Integrity overlay adds **who** may be banned and **criminalises** breach; it does **not** authorise a sector-wide “any involvement … in any capacity” / “directly or indirectly” / “assistance of any form” formula.

**Real risk is substitution, not validation:** *Khalil* [2025] HCA 33 [15]–[16], [22]–[24] + ART Act ss 54/105 — Tribunal applies **current** s73ZN at decision time and may **substitute** a narrower lawful order. Put a preferred substitute on the table; do not let (2C)(d) “facilitate” language become a back-door rewrite of the chapeau.

---

## 2. What (2B)/(2C) actually do (Comp 26)

**(2B)** — order “**prohibiting or restricting specified activities**” by a person in **(2C)** if: registration revoked; auditor approval revoked; reasonable belief of Act contravention / involvement in another’s contravention; **or** (only for a person involved in a **(2C)(c) or (d)** service) not suitable to be involved in **that service**; fraud conviction; insolvency.

**(2C) persons:** (a) pending s73C registration applicant; (b) approved quality auditor; (c) employee/engaged/key personnel of (a) or (b); (d) person involved in providing  
- (i) services that **enable or facilitate** provision of supports/services for people with disability;  
- (ii) without limiting (i) — assisting with / advising on s73C applications.

**Still required:** **specified activities** (same *Sunflower* form constraint as (2)). Suitability limb in (2B)(c)(iii) is **service-specific**, not “NDIS sector in any capacity”.

**s73ZNA** (Integrity) — breach is fault-based offence (5 years / 300 PU), strict liability (150 PU), plus civil penalty. *Sunflower* [129]–[131] clarity / capable-of-obedience is **stronger** under criminal overlay than under old s73ZN(10).

## 3. Facial attack on **existing** instruments — unchanged

| Attack | Why (2B)/(2C) do not answer it |
|---|---|
| Reid varied chapeau + “directly or indirectly” + “any form” fail *Sunflower* [126]–[141] | (2B) still demands **specified activities**; no new power to ban “indirect involvement” or sector-wide “any capacity”. |
| s100 vary re-exercises **s73ZN(2)** (*Sunflower* [72]–[76]) | 21 Apr 2023 reviewer re-made a **(2)** order. (2B) did not exist as the making power then; it cannot **launder** ultra vires form of a (2) instrument. |
| Validity judged on the order as made | *Khalil* is about **Tribunal-time law on substitution**, not retrospective validation of a 2023 notice. |

Do **not** accept a Commission submission that “current Act now covers facilitators / labour hire / assistance, so the 2023 wording is close enough.”

## 4. Substitution / re-wording risk (live)

If ART sets aside the varied orders, it may **vary or substitute** (*Khalil* current-law; ART ss 54/105). Watch:

1. **(2C)(d)(i) “enable or facilitate”** — possible hook for labour-hire / “guidance or assistance” if the Tribunal recasts those as **specified activities** of a (2C) person. That is a **new order**, needing current grounds, current evidence, and *Sunflower*-clear specification — not a rubber stamp of limb (c).  
2. **Class mismatch** — Reids were banned as **(2)** (employed/engaged / key personnel of NDIS providers), not as pending applicants or auditors. (2B) only bites if the Tribunal finds they **now** fall within (2C). Do not concede (2C)(d) identity without evidence.  
3. **Preferred substitute** — activity-specific, direct engagement only, no “indirectly” / “any capacity” / “any form”; duration/conditions via *Sunflower* [191] four-element bridge; dropped charges + IR attribution discard + $1.485m minimal weight into current risk.  
4. **s108(4)** default retrospectivity of substituted decision × **s73ZNA** — seek **s108(5)** order if substituting.

## 5. Oral / SFIC one-liner

> “(2B)/(2C) expand **who** may be banned and **criminalise** breach; they still require **specified activities**. They do not authorise, and cannot retrospectively validate, the 21 April 2023 ‘any involvement in the NDIS sector in any capacity’ instruments. Any ART substitute must be a fresh, *Sunflower*-clear order — not a re-wording of the invalid chapeau.”
