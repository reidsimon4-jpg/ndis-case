      FIRST APPLICANT'S RESPONSE AND PROPOSED ADDITIONAL DIRECTIONS
          in response to the Respondent's Proposed Minute of Order dated 14 August 2026

1.       The First Applicant, Ms Vida Reid, has read the Second Applicant's Proposed Minute of
         Order. She adopts the orders proposed there insofar as they concern the procedure
         common to the three applications, including the determination of the Respondent's
         application under section 70, the requirement that the Respondent state the decision it will
         ask the Tribunal to make and its position on Sunflower Care Services Pty Ltd v
         Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission (No 2) [2025] FCA 1442, the
         witness notice, and the fixing of the hearing estimate after those matters are known.

2.       In addition, the First Applicant seeks the following orders in her own application
         (2023/3361).

3.       Interpreter. Pursuant to section 68 of the Administrative Review Tribunal Act 2024 (Cth), an
         interpreter in Bahasa Indonesia be provided at the Tribunal's arrangement for the
         directions hearing and all subsequent case events, and the suitability of those arrangements
         be confirmed before the matters are set down for hearing. The First Applicant recorded this
         request before the decision of 31 July 2026, and it is noted at paragraphs 2(e) and 41 of that
         decision.

4.       Allocation schedule. A schedule corresponding to the schedule sought by the Second
         Applicant be provided in respect of the First Applicant, stating for each claim relied upon
         against her: the participant, the date or period and amount, the entity that submitted the
         claim, the source document, the act or omission of the First Applicant personally on which
         the Respondent relies, the evidence relied upon, and whether that material was before the
         original decision-maker or the internal reviewer.

5.       Privilege protocol. Until further order: (a) the Respondent give advance written notice of
         any question, topic or category of document sought from the First Applicant that may
         engage the privilege against self-incrimination or penalty privilege; (b) any claim of privilege
         be determined by the Tribunal before the First Applicant is required to answer or produce;
         and (c) any hearing of such a claim occur in private, with such orders under section 70 as
         are appropriate. This order is sought because the criminal charges against the First
         Applicant were dismissed for want of prosecution on 23 February 2024 with the
         prosecution stating an intention to recharge, and her position must be managed question
         by question rather than by a general stay.

6.       Separate question. The First Applicant renews her request, recorded at paragraph 24(c) of
         the decision of 31 July 2026 and not yet determined, that the question whether the
         decision of 21 April 2023 lawfully varied the statutory basis of her banning order from
         section 73ZN(2)(a)(i) to section 73ZN(2)(a)(iii) of the National Disability Insurance Scheme
         Act 2013 (Cth) be determined as a separate question, and asks that it be dealt with together
         with the separate question proposed by the Second Applicant.

7.       The First Applicant otherwise does not oppose the Respondent's proposed orders 1 to 4,
         subject to the sequencing sought by the Second Applicant.
