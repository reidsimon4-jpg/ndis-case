Dear ART Registry,

I refer to the ongoing proceedings involving myself, Vida Reid (2023/3361), and Forward
Focused Group Pty Ltd (2023/4494). I am now self-representing, and I would like to

--- page 4 ---
sincerely apologize for any delay in providing this update to the Tribunal. I was previously
advised by my legal representatives that they would be providing updates to the ART and
have assumed that all necessary notifications have been made up to this stage.

I wish to request that the Tribunal grant a stay of proceedings in my matter, as well as the
associated matter for Forward Focused Group Pty Ltd, based on the principles and

reasoning already presented, argued and accepted earlier on in these ongoing hearings.

As demonstrated in the submissions from the Respondent (dated 29 November 2023- I have
attached it in this email for your convenience), the argument for a stay was successfully
made on the basis that:

1. Concurrent investigations and proceedings exist, which could be compromised by
the continuation of the AAT process.

2. Witnesses involved in criminal proceedings or ongoing investigations may be
reluctant or fearful to participate, potentially jeopardizing fair outcomes in either

forum.

3. Evidence and facts remain unresolved and are the subject of dispute in concurrent
proceedings.

The same principles apply directly to these matters. The ongoing and unresolved
investigations, as referenced in the Respondent’s submissions, are still active and
substantive, which makes it unreasonable to progress the AAT applications at this time.

Given this, I propose the following directions:

1. That the proceedings for Vida Reid (2023/3361) and Forward Focused Group Pty
Ltd (2023/4494) be stayed until further order of the Tribunal to ensure no
prejudice arises due to concurrent investigations and unresolved evidence disputes.

2. If the Respondent, NDIS and the Commissioner of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards
Commission can confirm that no further investigations are ongoing, I would be

open to continuing with my application.

In requesting this stay, I note the Respondent’s own prior argument made in support of their

application for a stay, as highlighted in their submissions:

e “Criminal proceedings will be potentially jeopardized if witnesses to those
proceedings also give evidence in these AAT proceedings” (Respondent’s
Submissions, 29 November 2023, Paragraph 10).

e “[The Tribunal] must exercise care in ensuring no prejudice arises to the concurrent
investigations or future proceedings” (Respondent’s Submissions, Paragraph 12).

--- page 5 ---
Applying the same rationale and precedent already accepted in this matter, it is evident

that staying these proceedings aligns with principles of procedural fairness and efficiency.

Ihave copied the Respondent’s legal representative, (AGS), into this email for their
awareness and to ensure full transparency.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if further information is required. I look forward to
your confirmation regarding this request.

Kind regards,
Vida Reid
