## A. New findings that help (strongest first)

- **Fact:** Simon's Statement of Reasons (SoR, 21 Dec 2022, c292 @12400 para 5) attributes conduct "during the periods in which you were a co-director and sole director", and that includes the Clulow claims.

- **Fact:** The 20 Hybrid invoices start 25 Jan 2022. That is 23 days before the 17 Feb 2022 directorship date the SoR itself relies on.
- **Fact:** Internal review para 29(j) gives the Tactical Intelligence Report (TIR) "minimal weight". The TIR is the source of the $1,485,527 figure.
- **Use:** SFIC; the s 39B error-of-fact argument.
- **What could kill it:** an ASIC record showing Simon was a Hybrid Hive director before 25 Jan 2022.
